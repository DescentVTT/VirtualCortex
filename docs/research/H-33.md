## The text, as §11.1 held it

- [x] **H-33 (H-25's configuration on another network of the same size; [ADR-0165](../adr/0165-another-network-of-the-same-size.md), written 2026-10-11 before any run).** Every learning result since H-13 was read on ADR-0044's prior at 1 024 units from seed 22. Of the configuration's constants seven came from that network, each by a rule written before it was read: the geometry's rotation, the first whose four couplings by census are within ten per cent (0); the gain, the first of 1.75 and 2.0 at which a frozen block sees the stimulus in 56 trials of 64 (1.75); the settled network, by ADR-0055's criterion over the lead-in's windows (the 96th); the stimulus's cancel, its offset, its ticks and its least messages (201, 9 and 6); the inhibitory rule's target period, from the settled network's rate (58 201 ticks); the critic's window, the shortest delay the image carries (100 ticks); and the critic's step and scale, by an arithmetic over the stimulus's units (9 and 2). The rest is the configuration's and no network's. ADR-0165 asks whether the configuration is the rules' or that network's: on two priors of the same generator from seeds 23 and 24, the next two, with the drive's seed and the task's unchanged, each constant is derived again by its rule, nothing carried over, and H-25's two arms are run on H-20's schedule. No file under `src/` changes. **Clause 0** every rule gives a value and the frozen block passes both of its measures, every stimulus unit firing once and the stimulus seen in 56 trials of 64; **clauses 1 to 4** are H-25's: every mapping learned, at least 80 of its last 128 correct; no coupling past 1.30 of its image's; each of the three reversals passing 40 of 64 within 23 blocks; the excitatory sum outside the four couplings within 0.75 and 1.25 of the image's; all on both networks, in both arms. Yes when all five hold. **No prediction for the verdict**: no rule of the seven has been run on a second network, and the 23 blocks were read on seed 22, where H-25 took 14 to 21. **Its stopping rule, written with it:** (1) one round, brief 067, no file under `src/` changed, the seeds, the order of the derivations and H-33's constants committed before any rule is run on a new network; (2) a calibration in which a pinned number moves, the rules on seed 22 not returning the constants the tree holds, stops the round before any such run and is a finding; (3) yes: the configuration is the rules', and the next decision is an ADR choosing among another size, the operating regime, a reward delayed past a trial and a less reliable reward, named and not taken; (4) no on clause 0 on either network: an ADR on the rule that did not transfer; (5) otherwise no on clause 4: an ADR on what the learning reaches there; (6) otherwise no on clause 1 or 2: an ADR on what differs between the networks, before any other question is asked of the configuration; (7) otherwise no on clause 3 alone: an ADR on the bound, what a reversal takes over the networks read; (8) no constant moves after a rule is run on a new network, no second attempt and no other seed. **Reached ([ADR-0167](../adr/0167-another-network-of-the-same-size-measured.md), 2026-10-11): H-33 is yes, step 3.** Steps 1 and 2 met: the harness's constants for seed 22 became what the rules return for a prior — each function of a run's path that read one gained a form that is given it, and the function it was calls that form with seed 22's, so no call of the tree changed — and `derive` runs the rules on a seeded prior in ADR-0165's order, stopping at the first that returns nothing; the seeds, the range each rule scans and H-33's constants were committed and pushed before any rule ran on a new network; and the tree's 96 other whole-domain tests then passed, among them the rules on seed 22 returning every constant the tree holds, each table on the way the one its round pinned and the image H-25's by its CRC. The cancel's rule was run as the tree holds it, ADR-0076's pick among three candidates, of which ADR-0165's table names the one seed 22 picked. **Clause 0 held on both networks**: on seeds 23 and 24 the rotation is 0, the gain 1.75 and the cancel's messages 6, the second candidate, as on seed 22; the network settles at the 106th and the 99th window, where seed 22's settled at the 96th; the cancel's span is 8 and 12 ticks against 9; the target period 59 510 and 59 179 ticks against 58 201; the critic's window is 100 ticks and its shift and scale 9 and 2; and the frozen block fires each stimulus unit once and sees the stimulus in 64 trials of 64 on each. **Clause 1 held in every mapping of all four arms**: 126, 124, 124 and 123 of each mapping's last 128 from the assignment on seed 23 and 122, 115, 118 and 126 from the mirrored assignment; 117, 123, 120 and 115, and 124, 120, 121 and 123, on seed 24; H-25 read 122, 124, 119 and 116, and 124, 118, 123 and 117. **Clause 2**: no coupling past 1.30 of its image's, the highest 1.183 and 1.182 on seed 23 and 1.195 and 1.191 on seed 24, beside H-25's 1.181 and 1.191. **Clause 3**: the reversals passed 40 of 64 in 15, 13 and 13 and in 16, 14 and 12 blocks on seed 23, and in 21, 12 and 15 and in 17, 16 and 14 on seed 24, where H-25 took 19, 21 and 16 and 20, 15 and 14; the slowest of the twelve is two blocks inside the bound. **Clause 4**: the excitatory sum outside the couplings within two parts in ten thousand of the image's at every block's end. Beside the clauses: the first mapping passes 40 of 64 in its 4th and 3rd block on seed 23 and in its 1st and 8th on seed 24, sooner in the arm whose assignment the frozen network leans to; each stimulus's value lies within 0.14 of the reward of $2p - 1$ over every mapping's last 128; the drawn sources are the presented stimulus's units in 99.67 and 99.68 per cent of their presentations with 1.6 other units a trial, as H-25's were; and the inhibitory sum ends at 0.908 and 0.910 of the image's on seed 23 and at 0.896 and 0.897 on seed 24, where H-25's ended at 0.848 and 0.850. Read from the tables and not measured: five of the eight constants came back as seed 22's on both networks, so those rules were tested in returning a value that worked and not in returning another; why the inhibitory sum ends higher was not varied; and no rule returned nothing, so a derivation that stops a network was run only over tables written by hand. By step 3 **the learning configuration is the rules' and not one network's, on three networks of one generator at one size, and the next decision is an ADR choosing among another size, the operating regime, a reward delayed past a trial and a less reliable reward**. It is not taken; steps 4 to 7 did not arise, and step 8 is kept. **Taken ([ADR-0168](../adr/0168-twice-the-size.md), 2026-10-12): another size, 2 048 units, the configuration's constants derived by their rules on two networks there, with the critic's rule extended first where it returned nothing — as H-34 below.**
