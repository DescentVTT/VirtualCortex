# H-43: a reward past the trial, its address kept, on three networks

What H-43 asks of the engine, as [ADR-0207](../adr/0207-a-reward-past-the-trial-its-address-kept.md) wrote it on 2026-10-25, before any run.
Brief 080 adds its readings to this report.
Its row, with its verdict and its ADRs, is in [the table of hypotheses](README.md).

## The hypothesis

H-42 read that the engine settles its own network and holds it by a step from its own loop ([ADR-0204](../adr/0204-a-step-the-engine-makes-from-its-own-loop-measured.md)).
Its rewards come at the end of the trial they are for.

Exploration 001 gave the reward one or two trials late, with no new mechanism, and nothing was learned.
The rewarded pair still held 56 to 73 % of its trace when the reward came, but the address named the rewarded pair in only 20 to 27 % of rewards.

With `Config::kept_addresses` set to 2:
- each trial's close keeps its drawn address and its critic counts;
- the reward for trial t is given at the end of trial t + k, naming age k;
- the engine consolidates under trial t's address, and reads the value from trial t's counts.

**On each of seeds 22, 23 and 24 at 1 024 units, from the image an engine born under `Config::reads_its_targets()` settles, in both of H-25's arms on H-20's schedule, at k = 1 and at k = 2:**
- every mapping learned, at least 80 of its last 128 trials correct;
- no coupling past 1.30 of its image's;
- each of the three reversals passing 40 of 64 within 23 blocks of its flip;
- the excitatory sum outside the four couplings within 0.75 and 1.25 of the image's.

**Yes** when all four hold in all twelve arms; otherwise **no**, with the network, the arm, the delay and the clause.

**Predicted: yes.**

| Predicted reading | Bound |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. The address each reward writes names the rewarded trial's pair | in at least 0.94 of rewards, in every arm |
| 2. The reversals' summed blocks | at k = 1 at least the on-time twin's (H-42's arm), at k = 2 at least k = 1's |
| 3. Each mapping's last block's mean critic value | above 0.5, in every arm |

A predicted reading that misses is recorded as a miss, and decides nothing.

## Its stopping rule

1. One round: brief 080 builds the kept addresses and the task's delay, with their tests and their ADR, and runs H-43 once. Its clauses, constants and readings are committed before any arm runs at a delay.
2. A calibration stops the round, and is a finding, if a pinned number moves with the addresses unset, or if an arm with them kept and rewarded at age zero is not H-42's arm, bit for bit.
3. Yes: the reward right three times in four asked again on the default, with a control at step zero; then a delay the engine is not told.
4. Yes at one trial and no at two: an ADR on the trace across two trials.
5. No at one trial: an ADR on what else must cross the delay.
6. No constant moves after such a run; no second attempt, no other delay, arm or network.
