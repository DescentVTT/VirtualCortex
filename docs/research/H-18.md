## Its rounds, as §6.5 told them

[ADR-0093](../adr/0093-the-punished-pair-consolidates-against-its-trace.md) took the depression and wrote H-18 (§11.1) before any run: a signed gate, under which an addressed excitatory synapse consolidates under `clamp(baseline + dopamine, −1, 1)` so that a punishment moves its weight against its trace and spends the trace by as much, over 4 608 trials with the mapping flipped at trial 1 536 — a mechanism brief 041 builds. [ADR-0094](../adr/0094-the-signed-gate-built.md) built it (the modulator section's `[25]`, format 16; `consolidate_signed` beside `consolidate`), [ADR-0095](../adr/0095-an-image-pin-moves-with-its-format.md) amended H-18's stopping rule before any run for the one whole-image pin the format moves, and [ADR-0096](../adr/0096-the-punished-pair-measured.md) ran H-18 once in `tests/inhibition.rs` beside H-17, the shared harness's oracle consolidating by the signed rule (`earned_run_signed`): the calibration reproduced ADR-0077's settled candidate in each arm first. H-18 is yes: 128 of the last 128 correct before the flip and **128 of the last 128 at the 4 608th trial**, in both arms. In the first half the gate depressed the wrong pairs to 0.96 to 0.98 of the image's while the answer pairs rose to 1.37 to 1.44; after the flip the old answer's pairs, punished against a trace of +5.7 to +9.5 per synapse per trial, fell by 9 070 to 13 310 a punished trial at first and came back to 0.95 to 1.06 of the image's, each stimulus's selection crossing in the fourteenth to the twentieth block after the flip, the first new selection 211 to 279 trials after it; a punishment potentiated 18 to 24 per cent of the addressed synapse-trials it reached — those whose trace was negative — and depressed slightly more; the inhibitory sum stopped falling at 0.110 and 0.114 of the image's, and the new answer's couplings ended at 1.41 to 1.55, still rising. The next decision, by H-18's stopping rule, is an ADR choosing among the reward-prediction error, a schedule of reversals, the operating regime and another size, and it is not taken there.
