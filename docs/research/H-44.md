# H-44: a reward right three times in four, on the default, beside a control at step zero

What H-44 asks of the engine, as [ADR-0212](../adr/0212-three-in-four-on-the-default.md) wrote it on 2026-10-25, before any run.

## The hypothesis

H-35 and H-36 asked H-25's configuration to learn under a reward right three times in four, with the gain held at 1.75 ([H-35](H-35.md), [H-36](H-36.md)).
It read yes on seed 23 and no on seeds 22 and 24, where four mappings ended at 71 to 80 of their last 128.

The engine now starts from `Config::reads_its_targets()`.
It settles its own network, reads its targets, makes its own step and runs the rate's rule at it through every arm ([H-42](H-42.md)).
The step is the homeostasis record's `control_step_q0_16`, and an image with that field written zero holds the image's gain through an arm.

**On each of seeds 22, 23 and 24 at 1 024 units, from the image an engine born under `Config::reads_its_targets()` settles, in both of H-25's arms on H-20's schedule, with the task's feedback `Feedback::ThreeInFour`:**
- every mapping learned, at least 80 of its last 128 trials correct;
- no coupling past 1.30 of its image's;
- the excitatory sum outside the four couplings within 0.75 and 1.25 of the image's at every block's end;
- each stimulus's mean value over each mapping's last 128 trials within a quarter of the reward of a half of $2p - 1$, $p$ = 3/4.

**Yes** when all four hold in all six arms; otherwise **no**, with the network, the arm and the clause.

**The control**: the same six arms, from the image with its step written zero and re-sealed, every other byte the image's.
It is read by the same four clauses, by network, arm and clause, and splits the reading of a yes. It is not the verdict.

## Its stopping rule

1. One round: brief 081 builds the control's image and runs H-44 once. Its clauses, constants and readings are committed before any arm runs at three in four.
2. A calibration stops the round, and is a finding, if a pinned number moves; if a network's derivation is not H-42's; if a default arm's first block under the true feedback is not its H-42 arm's; if the coin's count is not H-35's, 1 913 of 7 680; or if the control's image differs from the default's but in the step's two bytes and the image's checksums, or its gain moves in an arm.
3. Yes: the default holds a reward right three times in four on three networks, and the next decision is a delay the engine is not told. With the control no, the rule moving the gain through the arm is what holds the share; with the control yes, the network the default settles holds it, and the rule is not needed for this share.
4. No on clause 3 on any network: an ADR on what a misleading reward reaches.
5. Otherwise no on clause 1 or 2: the ADR H-35's step 5(b) named, on a single error's weight and the gate's clamp, with the default's readings and the control's as its need.
6. Otherwise no on clause 4 alone: an ADR on the critic's step under noise.
7. No constant moves after such a run; no second attempt, no other share, step, arm or network.
