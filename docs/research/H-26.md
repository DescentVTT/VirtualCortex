## The text, as §11.1 held it

- [x] **H-26 (on H-25's runs, what releasing the address's targets would cost; [ADR-0141](../adr/0141-the-readouts-own-competition.md), written 2026-10-01 before any run).** The second step ADR-0138 planned replaces the efference copy on the target side with a neural form; nothing in the network today makes the losing readout fire less while the response is under way, since the selection compares the readouts' counts after the window and the lateral competition of §5.2.5 is Specified. ADR-0141 measures first: on H-25's two arms, reproduced bit for bit, an open-loop shadow — the composer's rule with the targets every unit, its own traces and weights, replayed from the run's train and signal and never written back — reads the release's cost. **The cost** of a mapping is the shadow's consolidation on the pairs of the readout not selected, signed against the answer, summed over the mapping; **the signal** is the answer pairs' net less the other pairs' net under the run; **H-26 holds** when the cost is at most half the signal in every mapping of both arms. Predicted no. **Its stopping rule, written with it:** (1) one round, brief 059, nothing of the engine changed, the rule committed before the run; (2) a calibration in which a pinned number moves, H-25's arms do not reproduce bit for bit beside the shadow, or the shadow under the drawn address does not reproduce the composer's moves stops the round and is a finding; (3) yes: an ADR on the release as its own hypothesis, nothing built; (4) no: an ADR on the lateral competition §5.2.5 specifies, the cost per block its need and the attention-gated feedback its fallback; (5) no constant moves, and no second attempt. **Reached ([ADR-0142](../adr/0142-the-readouts-own-competition-measured.md), 2026-10-07): H-26 is no, step 4.** Steps 1 and 2 met: the shadow built as the composer's own rule taken out as a function and called again for a copy of its synapses with one flag, the address's targets released (`advance`, `Shadow`); H-26's rule committed before the run, with what ADR-0141 left to the protocol fixed — a tie's two readouts both not selected, a consolidation entered at the trial it fell in, the cost a net, the shadow's selected side outside it; every one of the weekly job's 79 other whole-domain tests passed first, and in each arm H-25's run reproduced every pinned table with the shadows beside it, the one under the drawn address held to the composer at every trial, before the shadow was read. **The cost was 0.486 to 0.602 of the signal**: past half in the assignment's four mappings (0.560, 0.514, 0.594 and 0.558) and in the mirrored's first and fourth (0.520 and 0.602), and under it in the mirrored's second and third (0.486 and 0.496). As predicted, and at the bar: from 0.7 per cent under it to 20.4 per cent over. The release moved as much weight on the readout not selected as on the one selected, 0.97 to 1.06 of it; there the answer's pairs fell on net and the other pairs rose, in every mapping. The readout not selected fired 0.57 to 0.62 of the selected one's spikes in the readout window and 0.95 to 0.97 over the trial, and carried its eligibility by magnitude, 0.93 to 1.06 of it, and a quarter to a half of it on net; 4.4 to 7.0 per cent of the trials tied, and the margin was at most two spikes in 23 to 31 per cent. Two readings stand beside the verdict: with the ties' part left out it is still no; and the shadow's selected side delivered 1.01 to 1.15 of the run's signal, so that net of it the release kept 0.51 to 0.63 of the signal, a first-order loss under half in every mapping, which the rule as committed does not read. By step 4 **the next decision is an ADR on the lateral competition §5.2.5 specifies, with this round's cost per block as its need and the attention-gated feedback as its fallback**. It is not taken; step 3 did not arise, and step 5 is kept. **Taken ([ADR-0143](../adr/0143-the-gates-output-delivered.md), 2026-10-07): the gate's output delivered to the network, with the release alone as the control, as H-27 below.**
