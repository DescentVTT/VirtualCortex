## The hypothesis

H-41 read that the engine reads both of its network's targets for itself ([ADR-0195](../adr/0195-the-inhibitory-target-by-the-same-read-measured.md)).
The rate's rule still runs at a step the designer chose, step = 1/16.
At that step the loop gain κS is about 0.94, and the count alternates around its target in 591 to 623 of 959 pairs of windows.

With a flag set at the modulator section's `[27]` (format 25), the read's end starts a probe:
- the gain held for 16 windows at g₀·(1 − 1/64), g₀·(1 + 1/64), g₀·(1 + 1/64), g₀·(1 − 1/64), four, eight and four windows;
- S = 4D/c*, D the + windows' counts less the − windows', c* the read's target count;
- the step κ = (1/4)/S, the gain back to g₀, and the rate's rule from the next window.

**On each of seeds 22, 23 and 24 at 1 024 units, under `Config::reads_its_targets()` with the probe's flag set:**
- **clause 0**: the read and the probe each give a value the rules accept, the lead-in settles by H-39's two criteria within 320 windows, every later rule gives a value, and the frozen block passes both measures;
- **clauses 1 to 4, in both of H-25's arms**: every mapping learned, at least 80 of its last 128 trials correct; no coupling past 1.30 of its image's; each of the three reversals passing 40 of 64 within 23 blocks of its flip; the excitatory sum outside the four couplings within 0.75 and 1.25 of the image's.

**Yes** when all five hold on all three networks; otherwise **no**, with the network, the arm and the clause.
