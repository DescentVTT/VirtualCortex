## The text, as §11.1 held it

- [ ] **A rule held by the network** ([ADR-0111](../adr/0111-a-rule-held-by-the-network.md)). H-20 read no learning set: each reversal passed 40 of 64 in the 13th to the 23rd block after its flip, with no steady speed-up, because one set of couplings holds whichever mapping is in force. The prefrontal account holds the rule in force as persistent activity, a context under which each mapping is learned apart, so that a reversal becomes a switch driven by errors (Rougier et al. 2005; Collins and Frank 2013). Taken in three rounds, each decided by the one before: (1) measured and nothing changed — on ADR-0077's settled network with every weight frozen, whether an assembly of its excitatory units, wired among themselves as the test's own network, holds its activity after a kick, stays quiet without one, lets go on a signal and does not spill into the rest; the rules give reasons to doubt it, since short-term plasticity is depression-dominated (release 0.2, facilitation about 164 ms against depression about 328 ms, where a synaptic account of working memory needs facilitation to outlast depression; Mongillo, Barak and Tsodyks 2008) and the threshold adapts by 0.02 a spike over about 41 ms (brief 048); (2) a readout gated by the context, if an assembly holds; (3) the schedule of reversals asked again with a context and a switch on errors. If no assembly holds without running the network away, the next decision is an ADR on a mechanism of persistence, a rule change with its own need. **Round 1 measured ([ADR-0112](../adr/0112-an-assembly-that-holds.md), 2026-09-27): no assembly is usable.** On ADR-0077's settled image with every weight frozen, assemblies of 16, 32 and 64 excitatory units were each wired as the test's own network, every member sending one synapse to each of the next 15, 31 or 32, at a quarter, a half, three quarters and the top of Q1.15's range. Each ran a lead-in and then eight rounds of an unkicked, a kicked and a released epoch of $2^{14}$ ticks under the reference drive. The kick was a ramp with a reset, derived from the membrane rule so that it fires each member once and leaves nothing behind; the release was ADR-0076's cancel. The rules were written before any run. **No cell is usable.** At a quarter and a half of the range nothing holds. Three cells hold by the rule, 32 units at the top weight and 64 at three quarters and at the top, and each of them also ignites without a kick in two to six of eight epochs and lets go in at most two; nothing spills. The tables say what the holds are: population bursts, each member firing three or four times at the refractory limit, the members' vesicle pool left at 3.5 to 10.6 per cent, then silence until it recovers. Each kick sets off one burst, and the stronger cells burst again of their own; no last half the rule reads as held is without a quiet window. The arithmetic written first said as much: no cell's mean input can hold a member at threshold at any rate, since at a sustained 20 Hz a synapse releases 0.362 of its efficacy at rest. Rounds (2) and (3) are not taken.
