# H-17: the engine revises a selection it has learned

What H-17 asked of the engine and what its rounds read.

## The text, as §11.1 held it

- [x] **H-17 (the engine revises a selection it has learned; [ADR-0089](../adr/0089-the-assignment-reversed.md), written 2026-09-23 before any run).** That under the configuration H-16 named — ADR-0077's settled image, the excitatory baseline zero, the inhibitory baseline 0.5, [ADR-0076](../adr/0076-two-injections.md)'s stimulus, the task as built — a run of **3 072 trials whose answer mapping is flipped once, between trial 1 536 and 1 537** ends by selecting the new answer: **yes** when, in an arm that starts from the assignment and an arm that starts from the mirrored assignment, (1) at least 80 of trials 1 409 to 1 536 are correct under the first mapping and (2) at least 80 of the last 128 are correct under the second, a tie not correct; **no** otherwise. A round in which clause 1 fails is a failure to replicate H-16, not an answer to H-17. Asserted, not a criterion, as [ADR-0090](../adr/0090-the-old-answer-held-from-the-trial-after-the-flip.md) amended it before any run: (a) every synapse of the two pairs that were the answer before the flip has at the 3 072nd trial the weight it had at the end of the **1 537th** — the first trial after the flip, which still consolidates the 1 536th trial's reward, a delivery being consolidated in the trial after it — since after it such a pair can consolidate only in the trial after one that selected it and was rewarded, which it never is again, and nothing under the gate depresses it; (b) in that 1 537th trial only the old answer pair the 1 536th trial addressed can move, so the other stimulus's has at the 3 072nd trial the weights it had at the end of the 1 536th; (c) no excitatory synapse outside the four stimulus–readout pairs moves from the image's. **Hypothesis: no.** `Readout::select` and `cortex-basal-ganglia`'s `compute_gating` select the larger count with no noise, no temperature and no sampling; a learned engine's answer readout counts about twice the other's (28.5 and 27.8 against 12.8 and 11.6, [ADR-0081](../adr/0081-the-reinforced-form-measured.md)); so after the flip the engine goes on selecting the old answer, earns no reward, and consolidates nothing. **What the round measures is the size of that no** — the trials of 1 536 in which the other readout is selected at all, the rewards earned and the counts' gap — which is the measured need the mechanism after it must meet. Brief 040 runs it. **Measured (2026-09-23)** ([ADR-0091](../adr/0091-the-assignment-reversed-measured.md); `tests/inhibition.rs` beside H-16, the flip made by the shared harness's `run_on_flipped`): the calibration reproduced ADR-0077's settled candidate in each arm's test before its rewarded run, both arms decoding the same image; the first half of each arm is H-16's arm of its first mapping bit for bit, so H-16 is replicated. **Answer: no**, with its scope — this task, these two arms, one flip at the half of 3 072 trials, ADR-0077's settled network at 1 024 units with the gain 1.75 held and the controller off, the task's own delivery with a reward of 1.0, the excitatory gate at zero and the inhibitory baseline at 0.5 — as predicted: clause 1 reads 128 and 128, clause 2 reads **0 and 0**, against 80. The assertion as ADR-0090 amended it held in both arms, and the carry-over it was amended for happened as derived: the 1 536th trial presented B and was rewarded, and in the 1 537th 69 and 68 synapses of B's old answer pair consolidated that reward (+1 970 and +3 264). The measured need: after the flip the other readout was selected in 2 and 1 of 1 536 trials, with 2 and 1 rewards and 0 and 1 ties, the first reward at trials 1 763 and 2 374; the old answer's readout counted 2.2 to 3.2 times the new's, 16 to 24 spikes a trial, the gap the one before the flip; each reward raised its new pair by 162 to 3 415, against a lead of 2.2 to 3.0 million the first half built from 589 to 695 rewards per stimulus; the old answer's pairs, punished in 1 534 trials in each arm, stayed at 1.34 to 1.46 of the image's while their trace went on pairing +5 to +10 per synapse per trial; the signal sat at the negative fixed point, −0.712 at a trial's end; the inhibitory sum went on falling in every block, to 0.173 and 0.170 of the image's; the sight read 64 and the stimulus fired once in every block.
      **Its stopping rule, written with it:** (1) one round, brief 040, its criterion committed before its first rewarded run; (2) a calibration that does not reproduce ADR-0077's settled candidate stops the round before any rewarded run and is a finding; (3) yes: H-17 recorded yes with its scope — the engine learns and revises in this configuration — and the next decision is an ADR choosing among the reward-prediction error, the operating regime and another size; (4) no: H-17 recorded no with its scope, and the next decision is an ADR choosing between **an exploration in the selection** — a gate that sometimes takes the action it does not prefer, which the rule has no term for — and **a depression under the gate** — a rule by which an unrewarded or punished pair loses what it gained, which the modulation's clamp at zero forbids — with this round's readings as the measured need; (5) no constant moves after a rewarded run, the flip's trial is not moved after a run, and there is no second attempt at H-17 in this configuration. **Reached ([ADR-0091](../adr/0091-the-assignment-reversed-measured.md), 2026-09-23): steps 1 and 2 met — one round, the criterion committed before its first rewarded run with ADR-0090's amendment before it, the calibration reproducing ADR-0077's settled candidate — and step 4 reached: H-17 is no with its scope, and the next decision is an ADR choosing between an exploration in the selection and a depression under the gate, with this round's readings as the measured need each must meet; it is not taken there. Step 3 did not arise. Step 5 kept: no constant moved after a rewarded run, the flip's trial did not move, no second attempt.** **Taken ([ADR-0093](../adr/0093-the-punished-pair-consolidates-against-its-trace.md), 2026-09-24): the depression under the gate, as H-18 below.**

## Its rounds, as §6.5 told them

[ADR-0089](../adr/0089-the-assignment-reversed.md) took it and wrote H-17 (§11.1) before any run: the same configuration over 3 072 trials with the mapping flipped at the half, predicted to fail because the gate is a deterministic comparison and a pair consolidates only when it is selected and correct, and run for the size of that failure. [ADR-0090](../adr/0090-the-old-answer-held-from-the-trial-after-the-flip.md) amended its assertion before any run — a trial's delivery is consolidated in the trial after it, so the first trial under the second mapping still consolidates the last reward of the first — and [ADR-0091](../adr/0091-the-assignment-reversed-measured.md) ran it once, in `tests/inhibition.rs` beside H-16, the flip made by the shared harness's `run_on_flipped`, which negates the task's `mirrored` before one trial and touches nothing else: the calibration reproduced ADR-0077's settled candidate, and the first half of each arm reproduced H-16's arm bit for bit. H-17 is no: 128 of the last 128 correct before the flip and 0 of the last 128 after it, in both arms; over the 1 536 trials after the flip the engine selected the other readout 2 and 1 times, earned 2 and 1 rewards and tied 0 and 1 times, the old answer's readout counting 2.2 to 3.2 times the new's — 16 to 24 spikes a trial — and the old answer's pairs, punished 1 534 times in each arm, not moving, while their trace went on pairing +5 to +10 per synapse per trial that the modulation's clamp at zero consolidates into nothing; the inhibitory sum went on falling, to 0.17 of the image's. The next decision, by H-17's stopping rule, is an ADR choosing between an exploration in the selection and a depression under the gate, with these readings as the need each must meet, and it is not taken there.
