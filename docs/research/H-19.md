## The text, as §11.1 held it

- [x] **H-19 (with the reward less its expectation, the engine learns, revises and settles; [ADR-0106](../adr/0106-the-reward-prediction-error.md), written 2026-09-26 before any run).** That H-18's configuration — ADR-0077's settled image, the excitatory baseline zero with the signed gate set, the inhibitory baseline 0.5, [ADR-0076](../adr/0076-two-injections.md)'s stimulus, the task as built and addressed, the reward 1.0, 4 608 trials with the mapping flipped between trial 1 536 and 1 537, both arms — learns, revises and settles when **a critic** is set in the task: an expected reward $V_s$ per stimulus, Q16.16 and zero at the start; the outcome's reward $r = \pm 1$ as `Feedback::Answer` gives it, a tie an error; the dopamine signal receiving $\delta = r - V_s$ through `Executor::reward`, saturating; then $V_s \leftarrow V_s + (\delta \gg 5)$. The critic is the task's state, off unless set; no record and no format moves, and with it unset every pinned number stands. **Yes** when, in both arms, (1) at least 80 of trials 1 409 to 1 536 are correct, (2) at least 80 of trials 4 481 to 4 608, and (3) each answer pair of the first mapping moves by less than 1 per cent of its image coupling over trials 1 281 to 1 536 and each of the second mapping's over trials 4 353 to 4 608 — where H-18 moved them by 3.6 to 9.5 per cent (3.7 as first written; F-52) — a tie not correct; **no** otherwise, the failed clause named. Asserted: no excitatory synapse outside the four pairs moves, and the oracle, fed the prediction error each trial delivered, agrees with the record at every trial. Predicted readings: the first mapping's pairs stand below H-18's at the flip (1.372 to 1.441), and each stimulus's expectation falls below zero within 64 trials of the flip. **The tension, stated before the run**: the critic bounds the couplings ($\delta \to 0$ once a stimulus is answered), and it shortens the punishment after the flip ($\delta_n = -2(31/32)^n$, about 54 presentations' worth of full punishment where H-18's revision took 450 to 650; with the dopamine signal's carry-over from trial to trial, which that arithmetic leaves out, about 42 presentations at −1 and 68 in all, F-53), against a correct selection reinforced by up to +2 and a smaller lead to undo; **no prediction is written for the verdict**. Read, not argued: the trials after the flip whose modulation of the old pair was at −0.5 or below, each expectation and the dopamine signal by block, the inhibitory sum. Brief 046 builds the critic and runs it. **Built ([ADR-0107](../adr/0107-the-critic-built.md), 2026-09-26):** `Task::critic`, off unless set, a task without one the task it was; H-19's constants, clauses, assertion, readings' rules and gate committed before the first rewarded run; the calibration in each arm extended to H-18's first block reproduced with the critic unset. **Measured (2026-09-26)** ([ADR-0108](../adr/0108-the-reward-prediction-error-measured.md); `tests/inhibition.rs` beside H-18, the oracle fed the error each trial delivered, the task's error and expectations held to the harness's critic at every trial): every whole-domain test but H-19's reproduced its pinned numbers with the critic unset before the first rewarded run, and each arm reproduced ADR-0077's settled candidate, H-18's image and H-18's first block before its own. **Answer: no**, clause 3 the one that failed, with its scope — this task, these two arms, one flip at the 1 536th trial of 4 608, ADR-0077's settled network at 1 024 units with the gain 1.75 held and the controller off, the task's own delivery with a reward of 1.0, the excitatory gate at zero with the signed gate set, the inhibitory baseline at 0.5, and a critic of one expectation per stimulus with a shift of 5 — with no prediction written for it: clause 1 reads 122 and 125, clause 2 reads 119 and 121, against 80; clause 3 fails in both arms — over trials 1 281 to 1 536 A→R0 moved +1.22 per cent and B→R1 +0.30 (the assignment first), A→R1 −0.08 and B→R0 +1.87 (the mirrored first), and over trials 4 353 to 4 608 A→R1 +1.31 and B→R0 +0.34, A→R0 +1.43 and B→R1 +0.12, where H-18's moved by 3.6 to 9.5. The assertion held in both arms (3 188 synapses of the four pairs moved and no excitatory synapse outside them; the oracle equal to the record at every trial). ADR-0106's two predicted readings held for both stimuli in both arms: the first mapping's pairs stood at 1.125 to 1.164 of the image's at the flip, below H-18's 1.370 to 1.441, and each expectation fell below zero within 64 trials of the flip, after 20 to 24 of its presentations. The expectations settled at 0.78 to 0.98 in the last four blocks before the flip and 0.81 to 0.96 at the end, never 1.0, because two to five trials of 64 stayed wrong or tied, so a correct trial still delivered about a tenth and the pairs it reached kept rising where the carry-over of the errors did not balance it; the signal after a block's last reward read 0.04 to 0.23 where H-18's read 1.712. After the flip each stimulus first selected its new answer within 3 to 68 trials (H-18: 211 to 279) and crossed in the fourteenth to the twenty-third block after it (H-18: the fourteenth to the twentieth); the strong punishments of the old answer — its selection leaving a signal at or below −0.5 — were nearly every presentation in the first two blocks, none in the fourth, and came back as the expectations rose, 253 to 324 per stimulus in all; the old pairs fell to 0.89 to 1.00 of the image's and the new ones rose to 1.11 to 1.16. The inhibitory sum fell in every block of both runs, to 0.095 and 0.098 of the image's; the sight read 61 to 64 and the stimulus fired once in every block.
      **Its stopping rule, written with it:** (1) one round, brief 046 — the critic, its tests and its ADR, then the runs, the criterion committed before the first rewarded one; (2) a calibration that does not reproduce, or a pinned number that moves with the critic unset, stops the round before any rewarded run and is a finding; (3) yes: H-19 recorded yes with its scope, the configuration the engine learns, revises and settles in named, and the next decision an ADR choosing among a schedule of reversals, the operating regime, another size and a critic of the engine's own; (4) no: H-19 recorded no with its scope and the failed clause — clause 2 alone, an ADR on an exploration in the selection under the critic or on the critic's time scale, with this round's readings as its need; clause 1, an ADR on why the prediction error does not learn where the reward did; clause 3, an ADR on what else keeps the couplings rising; (5) no constant moves after a rewarded run, the critic's shift included, neither the flip's trial nor the run's length moves, and there is no second attempt at H-19 in this configuration. **Reached ([ADR-0108](../adr/0108-the-reward-prediction-error-measured.md), 2026-09-26): steps 1 and 2 met — one round, the critic and its ADR first, the criterion committed before the first rewarded run, every pinned number standing with the critic unset and the calibration reproducing ADR-0077's settled candidate, H-18's image and H-18's first block — and step 4 reached: H-19 is no with its scope, clause 3 alone the one that failed, and the next decision is an ADR on what else keeps the couplings rising, with this round's readings as its need — an expectation that stays about a tenth below the reward because the selection stays wrong or tied in two to five trials of 64, and an error's carry-over that reaches the next correct trial's pair below zero; it is not taken there. Step 3 did not arise. Step 5 kept: no constant moved after a rewarded run, the critic's shift included, no second attempt.** **Taken ([ADR-0109](../adr/0109-a-schedule-of-reversals.md), 2026-09-26): what keeps the couplings rising is the advantage the critic delivers while the selection still errs and an error's carry-over; an expectation per stimulus and readout is rejected, since it would starve the learning with the rise; H-19's configuration is kept, and a schedule of reversals is asked under it, as H-20 below.**

## Its rounds, as §6.5 told them

[ADR-0106](../adr/0106-the-reward-prediction-error.md) took the reward-prediction error and wrote H-19 (§11.1) before any run: H-18's configuration, schedule and arms with a critic in the task. [ADR-0107](../adr/0107-the-critic-built.md) built it — `Task::critic`, an expected reward per stimulus from zero, the reward delivered the outcome's less it and the expectation then moved by that error shifted right by five, off unless set, a task without one the task it was — and fixed H-19's constants, rules, calibration and gate before any rewarded run, the harness's critic written a second time (`critic_step`) and held to the task's at every trial (`earned_run_predicted`). [ADR-0108](../adr/0108-the-reward-prediction-error-measured.md) ran H-19 once in `tests/inhibition.rs` beside H-18, after every other whole-domain test had reproduced its pinned numbers with the critic unset and each arm had reproduced ADR-0077's settled candidate, H-18's image and H-18's first block. H-19 is no, clause 3 the one that failed: the engine learned the mapping (122 and 125 of the last 128 trials before the flip) and revised it (119 and 121 of the last 128 at the 4 608th trial) in both arms, and over each mapping's last 256 trials one answer pair of each mapping moved by 1.22 to 1.87 per cent of its image coupling while the other stayed within 0.34, where H-18's moved by 3.6 to 9.5. The expectations settled at 0.81 to 0.98, never 1.0, because the selection stayed wrong or tied in two to five trials of 64, so a correct trial still delivered about a tenth; the first new selection came 3 to 68 trials after the flip, where H-18's came 211 to 279 after it; the inhibitory sum fell in every block, to 0.095 and 0.098 of the image's. The next decision, by H-19's stopping rule, is an ADR on what else keeps the couplings rising, and it is not taken there.
