## Its rounds, as §6.5 told them

[ADR-0082](../adr/0082-plasticity-everywhere.md) took it and wrote H-15 (§11.1) before any run: H-14's configuration with the modulation baseline at 0.5, so that every synapse consolidates half of what it pairs and a reward still has room, beside a withheld arm that reads what that consolidation does alone. [ADR-0083](../adr/0083-plasticity-everywhere-measured.md) ran it once, in a test binary of its own that shares the instrument's harness as one module: the calibration reproduced ADR-0077's settled candidate first; then, from the same engine's image as encoded at 0.5, the assignment, the mirrored assignment and the reward withheld, each 1 536 trials under the task's own delivery, the oracle consolidating every stimulus–readout synapse under the baseline plus the signal where addressed and the baseline alone elsewhere and held to the record at every trial. H-15 is no: the selection went to the answer in 78 of the last 128 trials in the assignment, two short of 80, and in 106 in the mirrored assignment; the unrewarded consolidation carried every coupling up by a third to a half of the image's and faster onto readout 1, so the withheld arm drifted there for both stimuli as predicted, the reward parted an answer pair from that course by two to nine per cent, and the stimulus whose answer is readout 0 decided each arm as predicted, A never reaching its answer in the assignment; the assertion held on every arm and the inhibitory sum fell to 0.41 of the image's. The next decision, by H-15's stopping rule, is an ADR choosing between consolidation gated by the reward alone — the baseline at zero, with what it costs — and the operating regime, and it is not taken there.
