## Its rounds, as §6.5 told them

[ADR-0085](../adr/0085-inhibition-off-the-reward-gate.md) took the reward's gate and its named cost first, and wrote H-16 (§11.1) before any run: every excitatory synapse under the gate and every inhibitory one under a baseline of its own, 0.5, a mechanism brief 039 builds. [ADR-0086](../adr/0086-the-inhibitory-baseline-built.md) built it — the inhibitory baseline in the modulator section's reserved bytes, the format moved to 15, unset the rule before it bit for bit — and [ADR-0087](../adr/0087-inhibition-off-the-reward-gate-measured.md) ran H-16 once, in `tests/inhibition.rs` on the shared harness: the calibration reproduced ADR-0077's settled candidate with the parameter unset; then, from the frozen image with the inhibitory baseline written at 0.5, the assignment, the mirrored assignment and the reward withheld, each 1 536 trials under the task's own delivery, the oracle consolidating every stimulus–readout synapse under the signal where addressed and under zero elsewhere and held to the record at every trial. H-16 is yes: 128 of the last 128 in both assignments, the crossing at trials 512 and 256, the inhibitory sum falling in every block to 0.41 of the image's as under H-15, every excitatory synapse outside the two answer pairs the image's bit for bit and none moving with the reward withheld; so H-15's no was the excitatory synapses' unrewarded consolidation and not the inhibitory rule's course, and the configuration the engine learns in is named. The withheld arm's lean read a coin either way, so ADR-0085's second predicted reading did not hold as written for A, and is recorded so. The next decision, by H-16's stopping rule, is an ADR choosing among the reward-prediction error, the assignment reversed within a run, the operating regime and another size, and it is not taken there.
