## Its rounds, as §6.5 told them

[ADR-0081](../adr/0081-the-reinforced-form-measured.md) ran it once: the calibration reproduced ADR-0077's settled candidate first; then, from the one image, the assignment, the mirrored assignment and the shuffled reward, each 1 536 trials under the task's own delivery with no change to the task, ADR-0079's oracle following the pair the task addressed and the signal its reward left and held to the record at every trial. H-14 is yes: the selection went to the answer in 128 of the last 128 trials in both assignments, passing 40 of 64 per block by trial 448 and 384, the mirrored first; the answer couplings rose to 1.35 to 1.45 of the image's and were still rising, every synapse outside them the image's bit for bit and the wrong pairs never consolidating, as ADR-0080 derived; the shuffled reward locked stimulus A onto one readout at chance accuracy. The next decision, by H-14's stopping rule, is an ADR choosing what the learning line asks next — the baseline at which every synapse consolidates, the assignment reversed within a run, the operating regime, another size — and it is not taken there.
