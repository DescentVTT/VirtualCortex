# H-21: with the inhibitory rule's target at the settled network's own rate, H-20's schedule keeps its inhibition and still learns

What H-21 asked of the engine and what its rounds read.
The text below moved verbatim from whitepaper 4.134.0 by [ADR-0199](../adr/0199-one-place-for-each-kind-of-fact-built.md): the hypothesis from §11.1, and the account of its rounds from §6.5.
Its row, with its verdict and its ADRs, is in [the table of hypotheses](README.md).

## The text, as §11.1 held it

- [x] **H-21 (with the inhibitory rule's target at the settled network's own rate, H-20's schedule keeps its inhibition and still learns; [ADR-0128](../adr/0128-a-target-the-network-fires-at.md), written 2026-09-29 before any run).** Under H-20's configuration the network's inhibitory sum falls to 0.07 and 0.08 of the settled image's (ADR-0110). The inhibitory rule's target rate is its default, 5 Hz (`ISTDP_TARGET_PERIOD_TICKS`), while the settled network fires at about 1.7 Hz, and from the rail the rule can only weaken inhibition (ADR-0053, ADR-0057), so it weakens the inhibition onto nearly every unit. The target is set to the settled network's own rate by a rule written first — the population rate under ADR-0044's drive over $2^{20}$ ticks with every weight frozen, the period 100 000 ticks over it — and H-20's two arms are run once with it. **Clause 1, the drain stops**: in both arms the inhibitory sum stays at or above 0.5 of the settled image's at every block's end. **Clause 2, the learning holds**: in both arms every mapping is learned, at least 80 of its last 128 correct, and no stimulus–readout coupling passes 1.30 of its image's at any block's end. Yes when both hold in both arms; no prediction for the verdict. **Its stopping rule, written with it:** (1) one round, brief 054, the target's rule and H-21's constants committed before the first rewarded run; (2) a calibration that does not reproduce ADR-0077's settled image, or a pinned number of an earlier test that moves, stops the round before any rewarded run and is a finding; (3) yes: the learning configuration named with the target, and the next decision an ADR choosing among the operating regime, another size and a critic of the engine's own, named and not taken; (4) no on clause 1: the drain is not the target's alone, and the next decision is an ADR on the inhibitory rule's start below the rail or its form; (5) no on clause 2: the next decision is an ADR weighing the target against the learning; (6) no constant moves after a rewarded run, and there is no second attempt. **Reached ([ADR-0129](../adr/0129-a-target-the-network-fires-at-measured.md), 2026-09-29): H-21 is yes, step 3.** Steps 1 and 2 met: the target's rule and H-21's constants committed before the first rewarded run, the calibration reproducing ADR-0077's settled image, the image each arm decodes H-20's in every byte but the period's and the seal, and the eleven whole-domain tests through the harness's run reproducing their pinned numbers before it. The rule read the settled network at 18 449 spikes over $2^{20}$ ticks, 1.718 Hz a unit, and gave **58 201 ticks**, a depression of 23 per spike against the default's 67; 411 of the 1 024 units fired at or above it. **Clause 1 held in both arms**: the inhibitory sum's lowest was 0.848 of the settled image's, at the last block of each; H-20's ended at 0.072 and 0.078. **Clause 2 held in both arms**: 121, 119, 115 and 119 of each mapping's last 128 correct from the assignment and 124, 113, 115 and 119 from the mirrored, no coupling past 1.30, the highest 1.190 and 1.193. The drain slowed rather than stopped: the sum rose to 1.01 over the first mapping, then fell, and was still falling at the end by about 0.15 per cent of the image's a block. The inhibition onto the stimulus units, which fire at 4.5 Hz, rose to the rail by the second mapping; onto the inhibitory units and the readouts, at 2.2 and 1.75 Hz against the target's 1.71, it fell to 0.65 and 0.82 of the image's. The settled image's inhibition was already below the rail, 45 of its 6 528 synapses at it (F-59), and the rule moved it both ways, 833 and 856 at the end. The reversal speeds were H-20's within a block but for one. The learning configuration is named with the target — ADR-0077's settled network, the excitatory synapses under the reward's gate with the signed gate set, the inhibitory ones under a baseline of 0.5 with the rule's target at the settled network's own rate, and a critic of one expectation per stimulus with a shift of 5 — and the next decision, by step 3, is an ADR choosing among the operating regime, another size and a critic of the engine's own. It is not taken. Steps 4 and 5 did not arise; step 6 is kept. **Taken ([ADR-0130](../adr/0130-a-critic-of-the-engines-own.md), 2026-09-29): a critic of the engine's own, as H-22 below.**

## Its rounds, as §6.5 told them

[ADR-0128](../adr/0128-a-target-the-network-fires-at.md) took the inhibitory drain and wrote H-21 (§11.1) before any run: H-20's configuration, schedule and arms with the inhibitory rule's target period, a parameter of the image, set to the settled network's own rate by a rule written first. [ADR-0129](../adr/0129-a-target-the-network-fires-at-measured.md) ran it once in `tests/inhibition.rs`: the rule read the settled image frozen under the reference drive at 18 449 spikes over $2^{20}$ ticks, 1.718 Hz a unit, and gave 58 201 ticks, a depression of 23 per spike against the default's 67; each arm's image was H-20's in every byte but the period's and the seal, by a masked check; and the eleven whole-domain tests through the harness's run, which gained a sibling whose observer reads the train, reproduced their pinned numbers before the rewarded run. H-21 is yes: the inhibitory sum stayed at or above 0.848 of the settled image's at every block's end in both arms, where H-20's fell to 0.072 and 0.078, and every mapping was learned, 113 to 124 of each mapping's last 128, the highest coupling 1.190 and 1.193. The sum rose to 1.01 over the first mapping, then fell, and was still falling at the end by about 0.15 per cent of the image's a block. The inhibition onto the stimulus units, which fire at 4.5 Hz, rose to the rail by the second mapping; onto the inhibitory units and the readouts, at 2.2 and 1.75 Hz, it fell to 0.65 and 0.82 of the image's. The reversal speeds were H-20's within a block but for one. The next decision, by H-21's stopping rule, is an ADR choosing among the operating regime, another size and a critic of the engine's own, and it is not taken there.
