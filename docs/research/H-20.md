## The text, as §11.1 held it

- [x] **H-20 (the engine learns across a schedule of reversals, its couplings bounded; [ADR-0109](../adr/0109-a-schedule-of-reversals.md), written 2026-09-26 before any run).** That H-19's configuration — ADR-0077's settled image, the excitatory baseline zero with the signed gate set, the inhibitory baseline 0.5, [ADR-0076](../adr/0076-two-injections.md)'s stimulus, the task as built and addressed, the reward 1.0, the critic of one expectation per stimulus with a shift of 5, both of H-19's arms from H-19's image — learns every mapping of a schedule of **7 680 trials** with the mapping flipped between trials 1 536 and 1 537, 3 584 and 3 585, and 5 632 and 5 633 (four mappings, the first 1 536 trials long and each later one 2 048), and keeps its couplings bounded. **Yes** when, in both arms, (1) at least 80 of the last 128 trials of each mapping are correct (trials 1 409–1 536, 3 457–3 584, 5 505–5 632 and 7 553–7 680), a tie not correct, and (2) at the end of every block every one of the four stimulus–readout couplings stands at or below 1.30 of its image coupling — H-19's stood at or below 1.165, H-18's passed 1.30 in the 15th and 18th blocks; **no** otherwise, the clause, the mapping and the block named. Asserted: the first 56 blocks are H-19's arm's bit for bit, no excitatory synapse outside the four pairs moves, and the oracle agrees with the record at every trial. The first two mappings' clause 1 and the first 56 blocks' clause 2 were H-19's tables, read before the run; the third and fourth mappings were the run's. The assertion held in both arms (3 188 synapses of the four pairs moved and no excitatory synapse outside them). Both predicted readings held in both arms: each stimulus selected its new answer 3 to 119 trials after each flip, and each mapping's first four blocks held fewer correct trials than its last four. Each reversal passed 40 of 64 in the 13th to the 23rd block after its flip — 19, 21 and 16 blocks (the assignment first), 23, 13 and 18 (the mirrored first) — with no steady speed-up; in each mapping's last four blocks 52 to 63 of 64 were correct, never 64, the expectations averaging 0.73 to 0.91 there. Over each later mapping's last 256 trials the answer pairs moved by +0.59 to +4.92 per cent of their image couplings, still rising at every flip; each flip stopped the rise, the new answer's pair starting from where the last punishment had left it, at 0.89 to 1.00 of the image's. The inhibitory sum fell to 0.120 and 0.122 of the image's at the second flip and 0.081 and 0.087 at the third and stopped falling from the 111th block, ending at 0.072 and 0.078 of the image's; the stimulus fired once in every block.

## Its rounds, as §6.5 told them

[ADR-0109](../adr/0109-a-schedule-of-reversals.md) took it — the advantage the critic delivers while the selection still errs, and an error's carry-over — kept H-19's configuration and wrote H-20 (§11.1) before any run: H-19's arms and critic over 7 680 trials with the mapping flipped before the trials of index 1 536, 3 584 and 5 632. [ADR-0110](../adr/0110-a-schedule-of-reversals-measured.md) ran it once in `tests/inhibition.rs` beside H-19, the flips made by the shared harness's `run_on_scheduled`, which negates the task's `mirrored` before each trial of a list and touches nothing else and which the one-flip form now calls, after every other whole-domain test had reproduced its pinned numbers through it; each arm reproduced ADR-0077's settled candidate, H-19's image and H-18's first block, and its first 56 blocks were H-19's arm's, table by table. H-20 is yes: every mapping learned in both arms, 113 to 125 of each mapping's last 128 trials, and no stimulus–readout coupling above 1.30 of its image's at any block's end, the highest 1.166 and 1.174. Each reversal passed 40 of 64 in the 13th to the 23rd block after its flip, with no steady speed-up; the answer pairs were still rising at every flip, by up to 4.9 per cent of their image couplings over a mapping's last 256 trials, and each flip stopped the rise; the inhibitory sum stopped falling from the 111th block, at about 0.07 and 0.08 of the image's. The next decision, by H-20's stopping rule, is an ADR choosing among the operating regime, another size, the inhibitory drain and a critic of the engine's own, and it is not taken there.
