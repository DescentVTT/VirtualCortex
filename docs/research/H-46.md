## The hypothesis

Under the engine's default the couplings rise through every schedule of a long run, past 1.30 of the image's by the second or third, while every mapping is learned. M12 asks for them bounded.

With the zero sum at the stimulus set, each stimulus set's couplings to the readouts held at their sum when the host sets it, each synapse's dropped fraction carried, and the excitatory depression's exponent at a quarter:

**On each of seeds 22, 23 and 24 at 1 024 units, from the image an engine born under `Config::reads_its_targets()` settles, in both of H-25's arms on H-20's schedule, sixteen schedules back to back:**
1. in each of the six arms, H-25's four clauses hold in every schedule:
   - every mapping learned, at least 80 of its last 128 trials correct;
   - no coupling past 1.30 of its image's at any block's end;
   - each reversal passing 40 of 64 within 23 blocks of its flip;
   - the excitatory sum outside the four couplings within 0.75 and 1.25 of the image's;
2. in each of the six controls, the default with both rules unset, some coupling passes 1.30 of its image's before the sixteenth schedule ends.

**Yes** when both hold; otherwise **no**, with the network, the arm, the schedule and the clause.
