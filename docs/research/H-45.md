## The hypothesis

H-43 read that a reward one or two trials late teaches when it names the age of the trial it is for ([H-43](H-43.md)).
Here the host gives the sign late and does not name its age.

With `Config::reads_its_delay` set and `kept_addresses` 2:
- the engine carries a value critic per age 0, 1 and 2, each predicting from and learning on the counts of the trial its age closes before the newest;
- an age's score is the sum of its error magnitudes over its last 64 rewards;
- the engine consolidates at age zero until one other age has the strictly lowest score at 64 rewards running, each time below every other by at least 64 × a thirty-second of the reward; then it moves to that age, once, and stays.

**On each of seeds 22, 23 and 24 at 1 024 units, from the image an engine born under `Config::reads_its_targets()` settles, in both of H-25's arms on H-20's schedule:**
1. at a delay of one trial and of two, given without its age, the engine moves once, to the true age, before the last mapping begins, trial 5 632;
2. in each of those twelve arms, H-25's four clauses hold over every mapping and reversal after the mapping the move comes in:
   - every such mapping learned, at least 80 of its last 128 trials correct;
   - no coupling past 1.30 of its image's;
   - each such reversal passing 40 of 64 within 23 blocks of its flip;
   - the excitatory sum outside the four couplings within 0.75 and 1.25 of the image's;
3. with the sign on time, given without its age, the engine never moves, and each of the six arms is H-42's arm on its network bit for bit.

**Yes** when all three hold; otherwise **no**, with the network, the arm, the delay and the clause.
