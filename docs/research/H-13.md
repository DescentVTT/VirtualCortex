## The text, as §11.1 held it

- [x] **H-13 (a reward raises the response it is delivered to; [ADR-0078](../adr/0078-the-rewards-direction.md), written 2026-09-22 before any run).** That on the network [ADR-0077](../adr/0077-the-background-side.md) settled — its image, the gain 1.75, the controller off, the modulation baseline at zero so that a synapse consolidates only under the dopamine term and only where it is addressed — with [ADR-0076](../adr/0076-two-injections.md)'s stimulus and the instrument's geometry, window, trial, seed and run of 512 trials, a reward of 1.0 delivered after every trial to the synapses from the presented stimulus onto its assigned readout, whatever the engine selected (a teaching signal, not a credit assignment), raises the couplings it reaches and the response the engine reads from that readout. **Yes** when, in the assignment and in the mirrored assignment, (1) each assigned pair's excitatory coupling sum after the 512th trial is above the image's, and (2) over the last 128 trials the assigned readout counts more spikes in the task's window than at the same trial of the run with the reward withheld in at least 80 (by chance about once in 337; a tie counts against); **no** otherwise. Hypothesis: clause 1 holds, since ADR-0077's trace is positive after 53 trials of 64 and every pair's sum after its frozen block is positive; for clause 2 no prediction is written, the depression's scaling with the magnitude ([ADR-0055](../adr/0055-a-weight-that-settles.md)) and the pair rule's reading of the target's last spike only being the two mechanisms in the tree that bound the rise. A yes is a claim about the rule's direction in this regime, not about learning a task, and reopens nothing of H-12. Brief 036 runs it. **Measured (2026-09-22)** ([ADR-0079](../adr/0079-the-rewards-direction-measured.md); `tests/instrument.rs`): the calibration reproduced ADR-0077's settled candidate step by step — the lead-in of 96 windows, the quiet run, the image, and the withheld arm's first block bit for bit, the stimulus firing once, the sight 62, the sign 53 — before any rewarded run; the taught delivery built in the harness from the two calls the executor has, the task's own addressed set rewritten between the trial's last tick and the reward; ADR-0072's oracle extended to replay the consolidation and held to the record's weights, traces and signal at every trial of every arm; the three arms run once from the one image. **H-13 is yes**: in the assignment the couplings A→R0 and B→R1 rose to 1.118 and 1.138 of the image's and in the mirrored assignment A→R1 and B→R0 to 1.153 and 1.139, every synapse outside the two pairs the image's bit for bit and the excitatory sum rising by exactly the pairs' rise; the assigned readout counted more spikes than at the same trial of the withheld arm in 128 and 127 trials of the last 128 (one tie), by six spikes per trial on average against a response of ten, where the other readout's count rose by under one; the couplings' prediction held in both arms. The readings: the selection went to the assigned readout in 106 and 115 trials of the last 128 beside 80 — a reading, not a clause, since the delivery read the assignment and not the selection, and it reopens nothing of H-12; the trace's potentiation grew as the couplings rose (the terms per synapse per trial on A→R0 from +0.7 to +4.8, the volley's potentiation from +4.5 to +7.2 as the readout fired 17 spikes per set against 10, the background's depression from −10.3 to −11.7 as the magnitude rose by 12 per cent), so neither mechanism named as bounding the rise stopped it within 512 trials and the rise is not settling at the eighth block; the dopamine signal at every trial's end 0.712 and after every reward 1.712 from the eleventh trial, the per-tick decay's step being the floor of $2^{-14}$ of the signal (ADR-0078's arithmetic said about 0.58 and 1.58). The scope: this network, this taught delivery, this regime, this rule. What the round did not do: take the next decision, run the reinforced form, move a constant, lengthen the run to read where the rise stops, or claim anything about learning a task, 256 units or Appendix A's scale.
      **Its stopping rule, written with it:** (1) one round, brief 036, its criterion committed before its first rewarded run; (2) a calibration that does not reproduce ADR-0077's settled candidate — the lead-in of 96 windows, the stimulus firing once, the sight 62, the sign 53 of 64 — stops the round before any rewarded run and is a finding, not an outcome; (3) yes: H-13 recorded yes with its scope, and the next decision is an ADR on the reinforced form, a delivery the selection decides, on this network; (4) no: H-13 recorded no with its scope, and the next decision is the operating regime below, by an ADR, with no further round of the learning line in this regime; (5) no constant moves after a rewarded run, and there is no second attempt at H-13 in this configuration. **Reached ([ADR-0079](../adr/0079-the-rewards-direction-measured.md), 2026-09-22): steps 1 and 2 met — one round, the criterion committed before the first rewarded run, the calibration reproducing ADR-0077's settled candidate — and step 3 reached: H-13 is yes with its scope, and the next decision is an ADR on the reinforced form, a delivery the selection decides, on this network; it is not taken there. Step 5 kept: no constant moved after the rewarded run, no second attempt.** **Taken ([ADR-0080](../adr/0080-the-reinforced-form.md), 2026-09-22): H-14 below.**

## Its rounds, as §6.5 told them

[ADR-0079](../adr/0079-the-rewards-direction-measured.md) ran it once: the calibration reproduced ADR-0077's settled candidate first; then, from the one image, the reward withheld, the assignment and the mirrored assignment, the delivery two calls the executor has in a new order (`Executor::address` to the presented stimulus's units onto its assigned readout's units between the trial's last tick and the reward, then `Executor::reward`) and ADR-0072's oracle replaying the consolidation and held to the record at every trial. H-13 is yes: the assigned couplings rose by 12 to 15 per cent over 512 trials and were still rising, every synapse outside the pairs the image's bit for bit, and the assigned readout's count in the task's window was above the same trial's with the reward withheld in 128 and 127 trials of the last 128, by six spikes per trial; the selection followed the response, to the assigned readout in 106 and 115 of the last 128, a reading that reopens nothing of H-12. The next decision, by H-13's stopping rule, is an ADR on the reinforced form, a delivery the selection decides; [ADR-0080](../adr/0080-the-reinforced-form.md) took it and wrote H-14 (§11.1) before any run — this task, `Delivery::Addressed` with `Feedback::Answer`, on the settled image with the baseline at zero, over 1 536 trials, asked to move the selection to the answer in both assignments.
