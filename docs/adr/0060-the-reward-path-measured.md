# ADR-0060: The reward path measured — a two-alternative task on the reference network at 256 and 1 024 units under a criterion written before the run, against a shuffled reward, a fixed modulation, the mirrored assignment and a second worker count; the accuracy stays at chance in every block of every run at both sizes, so the rewarded clause fails and no constant moves; the readings locate the reason in the readout and the pairing statistics, not in the modulator: a stimulus reaches both readouts alike, their difference under one spike per trial against a background of twenty and seventy, and every coupling from a stimulus quarter into a readout quarter falls under every feedback; what a next round would have to change is named and not built

> Amended by [ADR-0061](0061-the-learning-runs-leave-the-gate.md) (2026-09-20), which moves the five full runs at 256 units from the pull request's gate to the weekly job as `exhaustive` tests and keeps the rewarded run's first block in the gate. Every number below is unchanged and still pinned.

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0059](0059-a-task-a-readout-and-a-reward.md) composed the loop; this decision runs it and says what it did. The question brief 027 wrote is the first falsifiable form of "does a reward change what this engine does": a stimulus into the reference network of [ADR-0044](0044-reference-network.md), a behaviour read out of the engine's own train through `compute_gating`, a reward as an input, and a measurement that tells a change the reward made from a change something else made. The something else is known: under a stationary drive the pair rule moves every synapse whose two units pair, whatever the stimulus was ([ADR-0053](0053-the-waking-day-and-the-target-period.md)'s drain, settled by [ADR-0055](0055-a-weight-that-settles.md) at 0.45 of the prior's excitatory sum at 1 024 units), the anatomy the seed drew favours one readout or the other for each stimulus, and a two-action task has a chance level a bare accuracy cannot be read against. Four controls each remove one of these: a shuffled reward removes the information and keeps the dopamine; a fixed modulation removes the reward and keeps the pair rule; the mirrored assignment swaps what is rewarded, so the anatomy's favourite is the wrong answer; a second worker count exercises [ADR-0023](0023-executor.md)'s property, that the run is the same.

The decision rule was written in the brief before any run and is applied as written; what the numbers say beyond it is a reading, and no constant of the task, the readout or any rule was moved after the run it was measured in (brief 024's lesson, [ADR-0051](0051-the-estimator-at-4096-units.md); brief 025's, [ADR-0054](0054-the-causal-count-inside-the-loop.md)).

## Decision Drivers

- [ADR-0010](0010-measured-or-target.md) and principle 3: every number here is the engine's own on a stated network at a stated size, pinned from one run and held on every worker count and architecture; "a trial" is the engine's, in ticks and simulated milliseconds; what the same loop does at Appendix A's scale is a Target with the same generator, and no word such as "learns" is used outside the task, the accuracy, the block size and the controls that produced it.
- The three time constants that bound the design: the eligibility trace's $2^{16}$ ticks (655 ms), the dopamine signal's $2^{14}$ (164 ms), and a trial of $2^{12}$ (41 ms), so that a reward at a trial's end finds every pairing of the trial pending and reaches the next trials' presynaptic spikes, where consolidation happens.
- The modulator's clamp ([ADR-0032](0032-three-factor-plasticity.md)): a punishment withholds a pairing and never reverses it, so the reward's whole effect is the fraction consolidated, and the baseline is the experiment's main knob.
- [ADR-0036](0036-criticality-control.md), [ADR-0049](0049-dale-principle-in-plasticity.md), [ADR-0053](0053-the-waking-day-and-the-target-period.md) and [ADR-0055](0055-a-weight-that-settles.md): the controller off (`control_step_q0_16` 0, the gain held at 2.0), no sleep, the inhibitory rule at its default period, the excitatory depression scaled by the magnitude; the drift these leave is what the shuffled-reward control reads.
- The literature under §2.1: Izhikevich 2007 (the trace times a global dopamine signal solves the distal-reward problem), Legenstein, Pecevski and Maass 2008 (the conditions under which reward-modulated STDP ascends the expected reward, and where it does not: a global scalar cannot separate two synapses whose traces are alike), Frémaux and Gerstner 2016 (Appendix D reference 5), Gurney, Prescott and Redgrave 2001 (reference 6).

## Considered Options

1. **The protocol below, written first, run at 256 units on the gate and at 1 024 units in the weekly job, its outcome recorded whatever it is.**
2. The protocol with a constant chosen from a pilot (a baseline, a reward, a set size) and the run repeated.
3. A per-unit learning signal, a structural rule or a different task where the global scalar fails.

The worker clause holds at both sizes. **The engine did not learn the task**, under the rule as written, at 256 and at 1 024 units; no constant of the task, the readout or any rule moved after the run.
- **What the readings say beyond the rule.** *The behaviour is the background's.* A readout quarter holds about twenty spikes per trial at 256 units and seventy at 1 024 with no stimulus in it (8 Hz per unit, the reference network's rate at this gain); a stimulus adds three to six to each readout quarter in the first block and, over the run, none that the block's counts resolve, and it adds the same to both: the difference between the two readouts on a stimulus is under one spike per trial in every block at both sizes (over the run at 256, 19.0 against 19.2 per A trial and 18.7 against 18.2 per B trial; at 1 024, 69.9 against 70.2 and 70.0 against 69.8), against a trial-to-trial spread of about six and eleven. The selection is therefore the background's coin, the accuracy is binomial around 32 of 64 in every run (22 to 40), the mirrored run's curve is close to 64 minus the rewarded run's block by block, and the reward's sign is uncorrelated with anything a synapse from the stimulus did. *The coupling from a stimulus into a readout falls under every feedback alike.* At 256 units the excitatory coupling from quarter A into readout 0 falls from 1.02 M after the first block to 0.51 M after the eighth in the rewarded run, from 0.96 to 0.48 M with the modulation fixed at 1.0 and from 1.10 to 0.50 M under the shuffled reward, and the other three couplings likewise; at 1 024 units from 2.83 M to 1.37 M by the eighth block and to 1.29 M by the thirty-second, where it has settled since the sixteenth (1.27 to 1.31 M in every run) while the arena's excitatory sum was still falling. The reason is the pairing statistics: a stimulus quarter fires 3.4 spikes per unit per presentation, so each of its synapses onto a readout unit carries three presynaptic spikes per presentation, and the readout unit follows within the window on few of them; the pair rule reads the rest as depression, which the magnitude-scaled rule of ADR-0055 settles rather than drains. The modulation scales the consolidation of a trace that is net depression; a reward gates nothing that could rise. *The signal sums across trials.* A trial is a quarter of the dopamine window, so the signal at a trial's end is the last four or five outcomes with the newest weighted most (a walk that decays by 0.78 per trial): the block-end signals range from −3.07 to +2.77 at 256 units and from −2.63 to +0.98 at 1 024 in the rewarded run, and the modulation at a presynaptic spike is at the ceiling or the floor most of the time rather than the 0.5 ± 0.78 one outcome would give. *The weight sums beside ADR-0055's figure.* With the gain held at 2.0 and no controller the excitatory sum is 0.802 of the prior's after 512 trials (sixteen windows) at 256 units in the rewarded run and 0.780 with the modulation fixed, where ADR-0055's day at the same gain and baseline without the task read 0.721 at the sixteenth window; at 1 024 units it is 0.741 after 2 048 trials (sixty-four windows), still falling by 0.8 per cent over the last eight blocks, where ADR-0055's day under the controller settled at 0.45 by the sixty-fourth window: the drift is slower at a held gain and a rate of 8 Hz than on the controller's limit cycle at 10 to 37 Hz, and the fixed point it approaches is a different one, since the pairing statistics are. The inhibitory sum weakens from the rail as in ADR-0053's day (to 0.948 of the prior's at 256 units and 0.864 at 1 024). *The criterion's statistics.* A block's accuracy at chance is binomial with a standard deviation of four trials; the observed spread is that; a difference of two blocks has one of 5.7, so a control's clause (at most three) fails by noise alone in about one draw in four and the rewarded clause (at least ten) passes by noise in about one in twenty-five. The controls' clauses held here, and the reading above, not the clause, is what says nothing moved.
- **What the reading constrains.** *A reward does not change this behaviour at these sizes* because the loop as composed gives the reward nothing to act on: the behaviour it signs is decided by the background, and the synapses that could carry a stimulus to a readout depress whatever the sign. That is a statement about this readout and this stimulus on this network, not about the three-factor rule, whose consolidation of a pending trace under a reward the two-unit tests of ADR-0032 and ADR-0043 hold. *H-11's synaptic half* (whitepaper §11.1): the path it names, a reward consolidating the traces pending at its moment and a later behaviour reading them, is not testable through this loop as composed, since no behaviour here reads a trace; it needs the changes below before the question can be put to the network again. *The modulation baseline and the reward's magnitude are not the constraint*: the couplings fell alike at a modulation of 1.0 throughout and at one that the outcomes carried between the floor and the ceiling.
- **What a next round would have to change, named and not built.** (1) *The readout's window*: a count over the whole trial reads 41 ms of background against a stimulus whose spikes land within a few milliseconds of the volley; a count over the sub-window in which they land, or a rate above the quarter's own rate before the stimulus, is the readout that could resolve one stimulus from the other, and ADR-0059's `Readout::count` takes the window as an argument already. (2) *A stimulus that fires its set once*: a contiguous quarter reverberates through its own window (3.4 spikes per unit per presentation); a set of units spaced beyond the window, or a smaller set, would fire once and give each synapse one pairing per presentation. (3) *The trial's spacing*: a trial at or beyond the dopamine window ($2^{14}$ ticks, still inside the trace's $2^{16}$) lets one outcome gate one trial's traces instead of the last four or five. (4) *The gain*: at 2.0 the background is 8 Hz per unit; ADR-0044 read the network six times quieter at 1.75, where a stimulus's spikes would stand out of the count. (5) Beyond those, the changes brief 027 named as later rounds' ADRs: a per-unit learning signal, a structural rule, a different task. None is built here; the constants of this round stand as written.
- **What the mutation gate found.** On the changed lines, 84 mutants in `task.rs` on a developer machine: 76 caught, 7 unviable (the tool's synthesized returns that do not compile) and one survivor, `<` to `<=` in the constant that picked the shorter of the two refractory windows, equivalent because the windows differ and both arms agree at equality. Answered by restructuring rather than by an exclusion: the constant is the burst window outright, with a compile-time assertion that the other window is not shorter, so no comparison remains to mutate. The harness is test code and is not mutated; CI's run of the gate is the truth.
- **Not adopted.** *A repeat with a sub-window readout in this round*: the round wrote the whole-trial count first and the rule says a constant is not moved after the run it was measured in; the reading names the change. *A claim about Appendix A's scale*: none; what the same loop does there is a Target with the same generator. *"Learns"*: not used of the engine outside the task, the accuracy, the block size and the controls above.

### Consequences

- Good: the first falsifiable answer to whether a reward changes what this engine does exists in the tree, is no at 256 and 1 024 units under a rule written first, and the readings say why in terms a next round can act on (the readout's window, the stimulus's reverberation, the trial's spacing, the gain), each named without being built.
- Good: the harness reads per block what tells a curve that does not move from a network that fell silent; every run is pinned and the same on every worker count.
- Neutral: the gate's runtime tests gain five runs of $2^{21}$ ticks at 256 units, each its own test so that the test harness runs them beside one another; the weekly job gains five of $2^{23}$ ticks at 1 024.
- Bad: the answer is no, and H-11's synaptic half waits on the changes above; the debt of ADR-0059 (a learning run is not resumable from an image) stands.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Option 2**: a constant chosen from a pilot and the run repeated is the tuning after the run that ADR-0051 and ADR-0054 name as the lesson; the constants here were derived from the time constants and the modulator's clamp and written down first, and the reading says what they did.
- **Option 3**: outside brief 027's empowerment ("no new plasticity rule, no per-unit learning signal, no surrogate gradient"); named above as what a next round would have to change, which is the whole of this round's licence about it.
- **A resumable harness** (the cursor and the counts in the image): the debt ADR-0059 states; a measurement that may say the loop does not move the behaviour does not earn a format bump.
