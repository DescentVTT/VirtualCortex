# ADR-0069: The addressed reward measured — the instrument, the controls, the seeds and the criterion of ADR-0066 with the delivery of ADR-0068 the one variable, at 1 024 units under the criterion and at 256 as a reading; the addressed rewarded run reads 56 correct of the last 128 and the mirrored assignment 65 against the 80 asked for, the addressed shuffled reward 53, the fixed modulation 60 (ADR-0066's run bit for bit) and the global form 49 (ADR-0066's, reproduced under this round's code), the run the same on one worker and on four, so the rewarded clause fails with the global form reproduced; the couplings from a stimulus into its rewarded readout part from the other two by three to five per cent in the direction the trace's sign set, in both assignments and beyond the shuffled control's drift, and the behaviour reads nothing of it; at 256 units the instrument is below the mark from the first block and the size is not measured; no constant moves, and what a next round would change is named and not built

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0068](0068-the-reward-addressed.md) built the delivery [ADR-0066](0066-the-reward-path-measured-again.md) named first among what a next round would change: the dopamine term reaches the synapses from the units of the stimulus presented onto the units of the readout the engine selected, and every other synapse consolidates under the baseline alone. It also wrote, before any rewarded run, what that delivery can do on this task: with the reward symmetric the sign and the selection combine into the stimulus's answer, so the delivery is a teaching signal toward the answer, lagged by one trial and reaching a stimulus's own synapses at about half the trials, and its direction on the behaviour is the sign of the trace it consolidates more of — down, by ADR-0066's reading of net depression. This decision asks whitepaper §11.1's H-12 for the third time, with the instrument of [ADR-0065](0065-the-instrument-recalibrated.md) unchanged and not recalibrated, the controls, the seeds and the criterion ADR-0066's, and the delivery the one variable; so an answer either way is about the delivery.

The criterion was written before the run, in the commit that holds the addressing and the addressed set's definition (`c5b125c`), and is applied as written; the first commit that holds an addressed run's outcome is the one after it. What the numbers say beyond the rule is a reading, and no constant of the task, the instrument, the delivery or any rule moved after the run it was measured in (brief 024's lesson, [ADR-0051](0051-the-estimator-at-4096-units.md); brief 025's, [ADR-0054](0054-the-causal-count-inside-the-loop.md); brief 027's, [ADR-0060](0060-the-reward-path-measured.md); brief 029's, ADR-0066).

## Decision Drivers

- [ADR-0010](0010-measured-or-target.md) and principle 3: every number here is the engine's own on a stated network at a stated size, pinned from one run and held on every worker count and architecture; what the same loop does at Appendix A's scale is a Target with the same generator; "learns" is not used of the engine outside the task, the accuracy, the trials and the controls that produced it.
- The criterion is ADR-0066's, clause by clause, with one clause added and none changed: the global form, rerun under this round's code, must read ADR-0066's 49 and is the comparison the claim rests on; its rates are the integer oracle's, one in 337 for the rewarded clause and one in 74 per control, so five controls exceed a ceiling by noise about once in 15.
- **The rule for a size that stops seeing**, written before the run: a size whose calibration measure (the trials of a block in which the window after the volley held more readout spikes than the window before the injection) falls below 56 of 64 in any block before the criterion's window (blocks 7 and 8) is recorded as **not measured**, never as a pass or a fail. ADR-0066 read 256 units below the mark from its second block and 1 024 units above it throughout, so the criterion is at 1 024 units and 256 is one reading; if the addressed form kept 256 above the mark, that would itself be a result.
- [ADR-0061](0061-the-learning-runs-leave-the-gate.md) and [ADR-0067](0067-the-weekly-dispatch-has-a-scope.md): the full runs are weekly `exhaustive` tests; the gate gains one run, the addressed rewarded run's first block at 256 units, held to the first row of the weekly run's table; this round adds tests, so its evidence dispatch is `scope=both`.
- Legenstein, Pecevski and Maass 2008 (the condition under which a global signal is sufficient, which ADR-0066 read this network as failing); Roelfsema and van Ooyen 2005 (the addressed form); Izhikevich 2007; Frémaux and Gerstner 2016.

## Considered Options

1. **The protocol below, the criterion as written, run at 1 024 units under the criterion and at 256 units as a reading, as weekly `exhaustive` tests with the first block in the gate, its outcome recorded whatever it is.**
2. The protocol with a constant moved after an addressed run (a reward of one sign, a larger reward, a longer trial) and the run repeated.
3. A structural rule, a different task, or a background under which the pairings can rise.

### Consequences

- Good: the third falsifiable answer to whether a reward changes what this engine does exists in the tree, is no at 1 024 units under a rule written first with the global form reproduced beside it, and this time carries the first measured asymmetry between the couplings the assignment could read — in the direction the decision that built the delivery wrote down before the run.
- Good: the readings locate the two things a next round would change in terms a brief can act on and this round did not build: the sign of the trace on this background, and the reward's sign structure.
- Good: every run is pinned block for block with its trace and the addressed rewarded run is the same on one worker and on four; the fixed modulation under the addressed delivery is ADR-0066's run bit for bit, and the global form reproduces ADR-0066's table and trace under this round's code.
- Neutral: the gate's runtime tests gain one run of $2^{20}$ ticks at 256 units and three tests that run nothing heavy; the weekly job gains six runs of $2^{23}$ ticks.
- Bad: the answer is no a third time, and H-11's synaptic half waits on it; the debt of ADR-0059 stands with the addressed set added to what an image does not hold.
- Bad: at 256 units the addressed form does not keep the instrument above the mark, so the reading there is a reading of the couplings in the first block and nothing of the behaviour.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Option 2**: a constant chosen from an addressed run and the run repeated is the tuning after the run that four rounds name as the lesson; the reading names what a moved constant would meet.
- **Option 3**: outside brief 030's empowerment ("no new learning rule beyond the delivery of the modulation"); named above as what a next round would change, which is the whole of this round's licence about it.
- **A criterion that reads the couplings rather than the behaviour**: H-12 is about a behaviour the engine reads back; the couplings are the reading that explains the behaviour, and a rule written after the run to pass on them would be option 2 in another form.
- **Counting the 256 reading against the criterion**: the rule for a size that stops seeing was written before the run and says not measured; a reading through a readout that does not see is ADR-0060 again.
