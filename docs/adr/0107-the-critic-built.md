# ADR-0107: The critic built — ADR-0106's expected reward per stimulus placed in the task as `Task::critic`, off unless set: the reward delivered is the outcome's less the presented stimulus's expectation, the expectation then moved by an arithmetic shift of the error; `Outcome` carries the error and the expectation before and after; an expectation beyond the reward's magnitude refused; no record field and no format; unset, every run is the run it was; the harness's critic written a second time, the calibration extended to H-18's first block as brief 046 asks, and two findings from reading the engine before the run: ADR-0106 mis-rounded three of H-18's moves (F-52), and its arithmetic of the punishment's fading leaves out the dopamine signal's carry-over from one trial into the next (F-53)

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0106](0106-the-reward-prediction-error.md) decided the rule and wrote H-19: *"A critic in `Task`, off unless set, holds `expected_q16[s]` for each stimulus, Q16.16, zero at the run's start"*; the outcome's reward $r = \pm$`reward_q16` as `Feedback::Answer` gives it, a tie an error; $\delta = r - V_s$, saturating, is what `Executor::reward` receives and what the trial records as its reward; then $V_s \leftarrow V_s + (\delta \gg 5)$, an arithmetic shift, saturating; no record field and no format; unset, every pinned number stands. Brief 046's empowerment leaves to this round where in `Task` the critic lives and how it is set, what `Outcome` carries of it, how H-19's arms reuse H-18's harness, which readings it takes beside the criterion's, and whether the round writes one ADR or two.

**What the tree did before this decision**, read for it on 2026-09-26 (`runtime/cortex-runtime/src/task.rs`, `crates/cortex-neuromod/src/lib.rs`, `runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/instrument/harness.rs`, `runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/inhibition.rs`):

- `Task::trial`, after the selection and the address, takes `positive` from the feedback (the answer, the shuffled coin, or nothing when withheld, which returns early with a reward of zero), delivers `reward_q16` or its negation through `Executor::reward`, and returns an `Outcome` whose `reward_q16` is what it delivered and whose `signal_q16` is the modulator's signal after it. `Task` is `Copy`, its fields public; five places build one field by field (the task's own tests, `probe_task` and `task` in the shared harness, and `tests/learning.rs`'s `task`). The shared harness's `run_on_flipped` takes the task by value and calls `trial` on it every trial, so state the task holds lives for exactly one run.
- `NeuromodulatorState::reward` adds its argument to `dopamine_rpe`, saturating. The executor decays the signal every tick by the floor of $2^{-14}$ of itself and at least one LSB, so a trial of $2^{14}$ ticks leaves 0.458 of a signal of 1.0 (`SIGNAL_END_FIRST_Q16`); nothing resets it between trials, so a trial's delivery adds to what the deliveries before it left.
- The harness's `earned_run_signed` asserts at every trial that the reward the task delivered is `REWARD_Q16` signed by the outcome, and feeds the composer that reward, from which the oracle holds the record's signal at the trial's end to the course's end plus it.

## Decision Drivers

- ADR-0106's rule as written: the error saturating, the step an arithmetic shift, both saturating; the critic the task's state; unset, every pinned number of every round, H-18's arms and the determinism pin among them.
- Principle 5: what the rule cannot reach is refused by name where it would enter, and a test holds each refusal at its edge.
- [ADR-0030](0030-verification-governance.md): the rule over the lattice of `testkit/prop.rs`, every mutant in the changed lines caught.
- Brief 046: *"The engine is read before a description of it is trusted, this brief's and ADR-0106's included."*
- Latest ≠ Newest (§2.1): the Rescorla–Wagner update of a one-step episode (Rescorla and Wagner 1972), the prediction error dopamine neurons report (Schultz, Dayan and Montague 1997) and the stimulus-specific critic reward-modulated STDP needs (Frémaux, Sprekeler and Gerstner 2010); nothing newer, no dependency, no tool.

## Considered Options

1. **Where the critic lives**: a field `critic: Option<Critic>` of `Task`; a variant of `Feedback` carrying the expectations; a struct beside the task handed to a second `trial`.
2. **The shift**: a field of the critic; a constant of the task's module.
3. **A shift of 32 or more**: refused by `check`; taken at 31, where the floor of the error over $2^{\text{shift}}$ already is what any wider shift gives.
4. **An expectation beyond the reward's magnitude**: refused by `check`; accepted.
5. **Under the other feedbacks**: the critic takes whatever reward the feedback gives (the coin's under `Feedback::Shuffled`, none when withheld); refused but under `Feedback::Answer`.
6. **What `Outcome` carries**: `reward_q16` the error, and the presented stimulus's expectation before and after as `expected_q16: Option<[i32; 2]>`; that, and the outcome's reward before the critic as a field of its own.
7. **The harness**: `earned_run_predicted`, which `earned_run_signed` calls with no critic; a copy of the run for the critic.
8. **The calibration**: ADR-0077's settled candidate and H-18's image by its CRC, as H-18's arms; and H-18's arm's first block from the signed image with the critic unset, held to H-18's tables.
9. **How many ADRs**: this one, before any run, and the measurement's; one after the run.

## Decision Outcome

**Options 1 (a field), 2 (a field of the critic), 3 (taken at 31), 4 (refused), 5 (whatever the feedback gives), 6 (the error and the expectation before and after), 7 (`earned_run_predicted`), 8 (both) and 9 (two).**

- **The critic** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/src/task.rs`, `Critic`). `expected_q16: [i32; 2]` and `shift: u32`; `Critic::new(shift)` has both expectations zero. `predict(stimulus, reward)` takes the error `reward − V`, saturating, and moves `V` by `error >> shift.min(31)`, saturating, returning the error and `V` before and after. The step is the floor of the error over $2^{\text{shift}}$: never larger than the error and of its sign, so an expectation moves toward the reward and never past it, and one within $[-|r|, |r|]$ stays within it. For an `i32` error the floor over $2^{31}$ is already none at or above zero and one LSB down below it, which is what any wider shift gives, so a shift of 32 or more is taken as 31 rather than refused, as `decay_dopamine` takes its shift at 62.
- **In the task.** `Task::critic: Option<Critic>`, none unless set. `Task::trial` computes the outcome's reward as before; with a critic it hands that reward to `predict` for the presented stimulus and delivers the error; without one it delivers the reward itself. The critic takes the reward the feedback gives — H-19 runs `Feedback::Answer`, a tie an error, and nothing in the rule depends on where the sign came from — and when the reward is withheld nothing is delivered and nothing moves. `Task::check` refuses a critic whose expectation of either stimulus lies beyond the reward's magnitude, `TaskError::ExpectationBeyondReward`, after the magnitude's own refusals: the rule could not take it there from zero, and under the refusal the bound on the expectation is a precondition of every trial and not a property of how the critic was built. The critic is the task's state, as the seed and the trial's index are: no record field moves, no image carries it, and the format stays 16. A trace recorded in one run does not carry the critic into an image, which ADR-0106 names as a cost.
- **What a trial returns.** `Outcome::reward_q16` is the error under a critic, the reward delivered as before; `Outcome::expected_q16` is the presented stimulus's expectation before the trial and after it, the same twice when withheld, and none without a critic. The outcome's reward before the critic is the error plus the expectation before, so it is not a field of its own. With no critic every field `Task::trial` returned before is what it was.
- **The tests** (`task.rs`). `the_critic_s_error_and_update_at_their_edges`: an expectation at either end of the bound against a reward at either end (no error; an error of $\pm 2r$ and a step of a sixteenth of $r$), the other stimulus's expectation never moved, the step's floor from −65 to 64 across zero, saturation at the width, and shifts of 0, 30, 31, 32, 33, 64 and `u32::MAX`. `every_refusal_is_named` gains the refusal at its edges: $\pm r$ accepted, $r + 1$ and $-r - 1$ refused for either stimulus, `i32::MIN` refused, any shift accepted, a magnitude of zero bounding the expectations at zero, the magnitude refused first, and a trial refused before it injects anything. `a_trial_under_the_critic_delivers_the_error_and_moves_the_expectation`: on the sixteen-unit network against the modulator's oracle, a tie punished at an expectation of zero (the trial without a critic in every field but the expectation's), the same stimulus correct next (the error $r + r/32$, the expectation from $-r/32$ to 16 LSB), the other stimulus's own expectation, withheld with both stimuli's expectations read and none moved, and the shuffled coin's reward less the expectation. Over the lattice, `the_expectation_moves_toward_the_reward_and_stays_within_it`: every magnitude of the `i32` lattice at or above zero, either sign, every expectation within the bound, ten shifts from 0 to `u32::MAX`, the error the difference taken in `i64` and clamped, the expectation after the one before plus `div_euclid` of the error by $2^{\text{shift}}$ in `i64`, between the one before and the reward; and 256 seeded walks of 64 signed rewards, every expectation within its bound at every step. The task's earlier tests read `expected_q16` as none.
- **The harness** (`tests/instrument/harness.rs`). `critic_step(expected, reward, shift)` is the rule written a second time, the error in `i64` clamped to the width and the step by `div_euclid`, not a shift. `earned_run_predicted` sets the critic in the task, keeps the harness's own expectations and at every trial holds the task to them — the reward delivered the oracle's error, the outcome's expectation before and after the oracle's, both the same and the reward zero when withheld — and feeds the composer what the task delivered, so the oracle holds the record's signal, traces and weights exactly as it does without a critic. `earned_run_signed` calls it with none and is the run it was.
- **H-19's constants, rules and gate, committed before any rewarded run** (`tests/inhibition.rs`, the commit after this decision's code): ADR-0106's run, flip, arms and shift; no prediction for the verdict; clause 3's span and bound as `|Δ| × 100 < image`; ADR-0106's two predicted readings as constants and rules; the strong punishment's mark, −0.5; the criterion `Predicted`, the readings' rules, the arms `critic_arm`, and the gate's test `a_few_trials_under_the_critic_at_1024_units_and_the_rules_of_the_critic`. The gate holds the clauses at their edges — clause 3 at the largest move below one per cent of each pair's image coupling (62 495 for A→R0's 6 249 552) and one LSB past it, up and down, under both first mappings — clause 3 over H-18's pinned tables, the readings' rules over hand-written tables, `critic_step` against `Critic::predict` over the lattice, and eight trials over a flip on the instrument's network with the critic set beside the same trials unset.
- **The calibration** (H-19's stopping rule, step 2, in each arm before its rewarded run): the settled engine held to ADR-0077's tables step by step as H-18's arms hold it; H-18's signed image held to its CRC-64 (`PUNISHED_IMAGE_CRC_1024`); a frozen block from the zero image held to ADR-0077's frozen run; and, as brief 046 asks, H-18's arm's first block from the signed image with the critic unset, held to H-18's tables — its sight, its composition, its earned block and its moves — so that each arm reproduces the configuration it changes, not only the network it starts from. Beside the arms, before any rewarded run, every whole-domain test of the tree but H-19's two runs with the critic unset.
- **F-52 (found writing clause 3's gate).** ADR-0106 wrote that H-18's answer pairs moved by *"3.7, 7.6, 4.2 and 9.5 per cent"* over trials 1 281 to 1 536 and *"7.1, 8.8, 4.9 and 6.5"* over 4 353 to 4 608, and so *"3.7 to 9.5 per cent"*; brief 046's table and whitepaper 4.57.0's H-19 repeat it. Read from `PUNISHED_BLOCKS_1024` by clause 3's own rule the moves are 3.64, 7.67, 4.17 and 9.55 per cent, and 7.13, 8.86, 4.90 and 6.50: three figures were mis-rounded, and the range is 3.6 to 9.5. The criterion's bound of one per cent is untouched. The gate now holds the eight readings (`H18_SETTLE_PER_MYRIAD`, in parts per ten thousand, truncated) and reads H-18 under H-19's rule as learned, revised and not settled.
- **F-53 (found reading the engine as brief 046 directs).** ADR-0106's arithmetic of the punishment's fading — $\delta_n = -2(31/32)^n$ at a stimulus's $n$-th presentation after the flip, the signed modulation at −1 for about the first 22 presentations, *"about 54 presentations' worth of full punishment in all"* — reads the error alone as the modulation. The signal a delivery leaves is the error plus what the deliveries before it left, decayed over a trial to 0.458 of itself near 1.0 and to 0.410 of itself at −2.0 (−0.820), and it is the same signal whichever stimulus the next delivery addresses. Worked by the rules — `critic_step` and the executor's decay, as the harness writes them — in the course ADR-0106's arithmetic describes (both expectations at 1.0 at the flip, the signal at rest, every trial a punished presentation, the stimuli alternating), the signal after a delivery is at or below −1.0 for the first 84 trials, 42 presentations of each stimulus, where the error alone is for 44, and at or below −0.5 for 133; the full punishment summed over the course is about 68 presentations' worth of each stimulus, not 54. The carry-over reaches the other way too: from an expectation where it settles under correct trials — within 31 LSB of 1.0, where an error below 32 LSB no longer moves it — an error delivers −2.0; the trial after it ends at −0.820, and if that trial is correct it delivers +0.063 onto it, leaving −0.756, so the answer's pair that correct trial addressed consolidates in the next trial under a modulation below zero and, the signed gate set, moves against its trace. Neither is a prediction for H-19's verdict, which ADR-0106 did not write and this decision does not; the reading ADR-0106 named — the trials after the flip whose modulation of the old answer's pair was at −0.5 or below — reads what the run does. The gate holds the arithmetic (`carried_punishment`).
- **Every pinned number unchanged with the critic unset**: the workspace's tests in the debug profile pass, 639 and 65 ignored where main read 635 and 63 (the task's three tests and the gate's one; H-19's two arms); the determinism pin is unmoved; every whole-domain test but H-19's is run with the critic unset before the first rewarded run, and the measurement's ADR records the result.
- **The mutation gate** ([ADR-0030](0030-verification-governance.md)): every mutant `cargo-mutants` makes in the changed lines is caught, in CI, as the measurement's ADR records with the pull request.

### Consequences

- Good: the dopamine signal receives what it was named for, and unset the task is the task it was, by construction: the match on the critic's absence delivers the outcome's reward and returns no expectation, and the harness holds that at every trial of every pinned run.
- Good: the expectation's bound is a precondition `check` holds before every trial, not an argument about how the critic was built, and the property holds it through every step over the lattice.
- Good: the calibration reproduces H-18's first block, so each arm starts from the configuration it changes, bit for bit.
- Neutral: the critic is the task's, so an image does not carry it and a run is not resumable from an image with its expectations, as ADR-0059 already says of the task's state.
- Bad: ADR-0106's figures for H-18 were mis-rounded in three places and its arithmetic of the fading left out the carry-over; the criterion is untouched by either, and both are corrected here, not in ADR-0106, which is a record.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **A variant of `Feedback`** (option 1): the expectations are state the trial moves, and `Feedback` is where the sign comes from; a variant would carry state in a choice of source and could not ask what the shuffled or withheld feedbacks do under a critic.
- **A struct beside the task and a second `trial`** (option 1): two trials to keep in step, and `run_on_flipped`, which owns the task for a run, would need a second path.
- **A constant shift** (option 2): the shift is ADR-0106's parameter, and the tests need shifts at and past the width; H-19's constant is 5 and does not move.
- **Refusing a shift of 32 or more** (option 3): a refusal for a value the rule defines exactly, and one more line the gate must hold.
- **Accepting an expectation beyond the reward** (option 4): the bound would hold only for critics built from zero, which nothing checks.
- **Refusing the critic but under `Feedback::Answer`** (option 5): the rule reads a reward, not where its sign came from; a refusal would forbid the shuffled control a later round may want.
- **The outcome's reward as a field** (option 6): it is the error plus the expectation before, which `Outcome` already carries.
- **A copy of the harness's run** (option 7): two runs to keep in step; one run with the critic as an argument keeps every pinned run on the path it was.
- **ADR-0077 and the image alone** (option 8): brief 046 asks for H-18's first block, and it is the one reproduction that exercises the configuration the critic changes.
- **One ADR after the run** (option 9): the placement, the calibration and the two findings are decided before any rewarded run and belong to a record dated before it.
