# ADR-0110: A schedule of reversals, measured — H-20 is yes: through three reversals over 7 680 trials the engine learned every mapping, 113 to 125 of each mapping's last 128 trials in both arms, and no stimulus–readout coupling passed 1.30 of its image's at any block's end, the highest 1.166 and 1.174; the first 56 blocks were H-19's bit for bit; each reversal took 13 to 23 blocks to pass 40 of 64, beside H-19's 19 and 23, and no steady speed-up; the answer pairs were still rising at every flip, by up to 4.9 per cent of their image couplings over a mapping's last 256 trials; the inhibitory sum stopped falling at about 0.07 and 0.08 of the image's from the 111th block; both predicted readings held; the assertion held; H-20's stopping rule at step 3, and the next decision named and not taken

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0109](0109-a-schedule-of-reversals.md) kept H-19's configuration and wrote **H-20** in whitepaper §11.1 before any run: H-19's configuration exactly — [ADR-0077](0077-the-background-side.md)'s settled image at 1 024 units, the excitatory baseline zero with the signed gate set, the inhibitory baseline 0.5, [ADR-0076](0076-two-injections.md)'s stimulus, the task as built under `Delivery::Addressed` and `Feedback::Answer` with a reward of 1.0, and [ADR-0107](0107-the-critic-built.md)'s critic of one expectation per stimulus with a shift of 5 — each arm from H-19's image, over 7 680 trials, the mapping flipped between trials 1 536 and 1 537, 3 584 and 3 585, and 5 632 and 5 633. Yes when, in both arms, (1) at least 80 of the last 128 trials of each of the four mappings are correct, a tie not correct, and (2) at the end of every block every one of the four stimulus–readout couplings stands at or below 1.30 of its image coupling. Asserted: the first 56 blocks H-19's arm's bit for bit, no excitatory synapse outside the four pairs moving, the oracle agreeing with the record at every trial. Two predicted readings, **no prediction for the verdict**, and a stopping rule. Brief 047 is the one round the rule allows; it builds no mechanism of the engine. This ADR records it.

## Decision Drivers

- **Nothing chosen after a rewarded run.** H-20's flips, length, arms, clauses, assertion, predicted readings and readings' rules are ADR-0109's, committed as constants and rules before the first rewarded run; nothing moved after it.
- **The previous hypothesis as the control.** Every trial before the second flip is H-19's, so a run that starts as H-19's did is held to H-19's tables before anything of H-20 is read.
- **No rule of the engine changes.** The schedule is the harness's: the task's `mirrored` negated before three trials and nothing else.
- **The engine read before a description of it is trusted**, ADR-0109's included.
- Latest ≠ Newest: no dependency, no tool, no rule.
- [ADR-0088](0088-a-fourth-shard.md): the two arms are two tests; [ADR-0092](0092-the-shards-dealt-by-cost.md): the round regenerates the cost table from its own dispatch; [ADR-0075](0075-the-dispatch-scope-follows-the-diff.md): the dispatch's scope follows the diff.

## Considered Options

1. **How the schedule is written in the harness**: a sibling of `run_on_flipped` and of `earned_run_predicted` that takes a list of flips, which the one-flip forms call; `run_on_flipped`'s signature changed to a list at every caller.
2. **How the first 56 blocks are held to H-19's**: table by table, against H-19's pinned per-block tables; by a hash of the readings.
3. **Where the arms live**: `tests/inhibition.rs`, beside H-16 to H-19; a binary of their own.
4. **What is pinned**: the 64 blocks after the second flip, the 56 before it held to H-19's tables; the whole run.
5. **How many ADRs**: one; a decision before the run and this one.

## Decision Outcome

Options 1 (a sibling), 2 (table by table), 3 (`tests/inhibition.rs`), 4 (the 64 blocks after the second flip) and 5 (one).

- **The schedule** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/instrument/harness.rs`). `run_on_scheduled` negates the task's `mirrored` before the trial of each index in a list of flips, which strictly increase (asserted), and touches nothing else; `flipped_at` says whether the mapping in force at a trial is the other than the run's first, an odd number of flips at or before it; `earned_run_scheduled` asserts every trial's contract under that mapping and carries the critic's expectations across every flip. `run_on_flipped` and `earned_run_predicted` call them with `flip.as_slice()`: with one flip `flipped_at` is whether the trial is at or after it, which is what the one-flip form read, and with none it is never, so every earlier arm runs the trials it ran. Nothing under `src/` changed.

### Consequences

- Good: the configuration the engine learns and revises in keeps its couplings bounded across three reversals — at most 1.166 and 1.174 of the image's where H-18's reached 1.55 within one — and learns every mapping, 113 to 125 of each mapping's last 128.
- Good: the run reproduced H-19 before it asked anything new, table by table, so every reading after the second flip is the schedule's and nothing else's.
- Good: what the next decision needs is read: the rise the schedule interrupts, the inhibitory sum levelling at about 0.07 and 0.08 of the image's, and the selection never reaching 64 of 64.
- Neutral: both predicted readings held; the verdict had no prediction.
- Bad: the answer pairs never settled after the first mapping, up to 4.9 per cent over 256 trials; the bound held because the flips came every 32 blocks, and a mapping held longer, or a different schedule, is not read.
- Bad: one seed, one size, one schedule of fixed lengths; the reversal speeds read no learning set, and cannot rule one out.
- Bad: the accuracy stays below H-18's, 113 to 125 of 128 where H-18's was 128.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Reading clause 2 against the settle measure as well**: the settle measure was H-19's clause 3 and is a reading here; making it a clause after the run would be a clause added.
- **One test for both arms**: ADR-0088's floor is the longest test.
- **Reading the next decision into this ADR**: H-20's stopping rule gives it to an ADR of its own.
