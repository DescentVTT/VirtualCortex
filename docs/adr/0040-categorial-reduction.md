# ADR-0040: Categorial reduction on the term arena — a category is a term, the four combinatory rules are unifications, and a greedy shift-reduce reducer over caller slices reads a sequence of lexical categories into one with a log; type raising and a chart Specified; the runtime composes the derivation into a frame, the frame into a hypervector and back

## Context and Problem Statement

`cortex-linguistic`'s frame was filled by nothing: `bind_role` took a role and a concept from its caller, and whitepaper §6.9 (R-9) said the concepts come from Layer 1's unbinding, which did not exist either (ADR-0039 gives it). Nothing in the tree read a sequence of anything into a frame: the construction-grammar templates fixed which roles a frame needs and in which order it is realised, and no rule said which concept fills which role from a sequence of lexical items. The proposal of brief 020 asked for combinatory categorial grammar (Steedman 2000) as "algebraic type reduction" over `TermNode`, "a stack-bounded, zero-heap Shift-Reduce / CKY algebraic reduction routine executing strictly over preallocated term slices, using `cortex-reasoning::unify`", and a cortico-striatal thematic gate (Dominey 1995) in which the cerebellar delay line buffers tokens and a function word fires the basal-ganglia gate that pops them into frame roles.

Three facts shaped the answer. `TermNode` already holds compounds of up to eight children with unification over caller slices, a binding table, a trail and bounds that are results ([ADR-0025](0025-term-arena-and-unification.md)): a category `X/Y` is a compound, and "does the argument match" is `unify`. The role assignment has one owner, the frame's `bind_role`, so a second mechanism that writes the same fields (the Dominey gate) would be two owners, on a token path that does not exist (the injector delivers spikes; a category sequence is an input a caller hands a rule between ticks). And state crates declare no dependencies (TC-2), so the place where a derivation meets a frame and a body is the runtime.

## Decision Drivers

- ADR-0025's precedent: no allocation, no recursion, every bound the caller's slice and every exceeded bound a value; variables numbered by the caller, who standardises apart.
- ADR-0016's one-owner rule: the frame owns the role assignment; the derivation supplies it; the runtime composes.
- §8.3: the same sequence over the same scratch gives the same log and the same table.
- "Latest ≠ Newest": categorial grammar (Steedman 2000; Hockenmaier and Steedman 2007), shift-reduce CCG parsing (Zhang and Clark 2011) and normal-form derivations (Eisner 1996) are decades old with documented failure modes (spurious ambiguity; a greedy parser's incompleteness), and adopted where the tree has the record they need.
- ADR-0010 and principle 3: what the reducer derives on the exit test's sentences is stated; what a lexicon and a corpus would derive is a hypothesis with a protocol.

## Considered Options

1. Categories in the 24 reserved bytes of `TermNode` (a category kind, a slash, a result and an argument index), with a format bump.
2. **Categories as compounds over two reserved slash functors with three children (the result, the argument and the role the argument fills); the four combinatory rules as unifications over the categories the two top stack items dereference to; a greedy shift-reduce reducer over caller slices with a log of reductions; the runtime binding the log's roles into a frame with the head concept of each argument, encoding a frame as a bundle of role-bound concept bodies and decoding it role by role.**
3. A CKY chart over caller slices: every span's categories, complete for the rules, $O(n^3)$ with a chart the caller sizes.
4. The Dominey gate: a token buffer in `CerebellarMicrozone::pred_ring`, a channel of `BasalGangliaChannelState` per role, disinhibition on a function word.

## Decision Outcome

Option 2.

- **Categories** (`cortex-reasoning`, `category.rs`; no record changes). An atomic category is a term: a constant, or a compound whose children carry its features and its head (`S(chase)`, `NP(dog)`, `N(X)` with `X` a variable). A functor category is a compound over `CATEGORY_FORWARD` (`0xFFFF_FF01`, `X/Y`) or `CATEGORY_BACKWARD` (`0xFFFF_FF02`, `X\Y`) with `SLASH_ARITY` = 3 children: the result `X`, the argument `Y`, and the role the argument fills in the result (a constant of the caller's role vocabulary, or a variable for "whatever the complement says"); `CATEGORY_RESERVED` (`0xFFFF_FF00`) is the floor of the band, and a caller's concept ids stay below it. `forward` and `backward` build them; `slash`, `is_functor`, `result`, `argument` and `role` read them; `head(term, arena, bindings)` is a category's head concept under the bindings: a functor's result's head, else a compound's first child dereferenced to a constant. Categories unify structurally, so a lexicon keeps one shape per atom and three children per slash: a modal is `(S(V)\NP(A):R)/(S(V)\NP(A):R)` with the subject and its role shared, so that composing with a verb binds `R` to the verb's role and the subject reaches the frame.
- **The rules**, tried in this order on the categories the two top items dereference to, the first that unifies: `RULE_FORWARD_APPLICATION` ($X/Y \; Y' \Rightarrow X$), `RULE_BACKWARD_APPLICATION` ($Y' \; X\backslash Y \Rightarrow X$), `RULE_FORWARD_COMPOSITION` ($X/Y \; Y'/Z \Rightarrow X/Z$, one new node, the new slash carrying `Z`'s role), `RULE_BACKWARD_COMPOSITION` ($Y'\backslash Z \; X\backslash Y \Rightarrow X\backslash Z$). A clash or an occurs failure is "the rule does not apply". Each reduction is a `Reduction { rule, left, right, result, role, argument }`: the role is the third child of the functor whose argument was consumed, and the argument is the category that filled it (the other item for an application; the right functor for a forward composition, the left for a backward one, whose result is what fills the role once its own argument arrives).
- **The reducer.** `reduce(categories, scratch) -> Result<u32, ParseError>` over `ParseScratch` (the arena and its free cursor, the binding table, the trail and its length, the unification stack, the parse stack, the log and its count): shift each category; while the top two reduce, replace them with the result and log the step; one category left is the derivation, more is `NoDerivation { remaining }`. `Empty`, `StackFull`, `ArenaFull`, `StepsFull`, `BoundExceeded` and `Malformed` (an index outside the arena or the table, an empty node, a reserved functor without its three children) are results; a reduction that could not be completed (no node for a composition, no room in the log) is undone, node and bindings, so the arena and the table are as the reductions before it left them. The parse accumulates one substitution in the caller's table; the trail names every variable bound, and `undo` reverts it. Greedy: the reducer commits to the first applicable rule and never backtracks. With the subject present, a verb phrase takes it before a following adverb arrives, so an adverb composes backward only with a subjectless phrase, which then takes its subject in a second reduction over the same scratch; and an object relative (`the cat the dog chased`) leaves three items, since the object is never type-raised. Type raising and a chart (option 3) are Specified; whether the greedy reducer covers the constructions the templates of `cortex-linguistic` need is hypothesis H-10, with the protocol: a lexicon, a corpus of category sequences, the derivation rate against a chart.
- **The composition** (`cortex-runtime`, `language.rs`; the runtime depends on `cortex-symbolic`, `cortex-reasoning` and `cortex-linguistic` by path; no executor field). Role constants for a grammar live in a band, `ROLE_CONCEPT_BASE | role_bit` (`role_concept`, `role_of_concept`, `role_slot`), above any codebook index a frame holds and below the slash functors, asserted at compile time. `comprehend(categories, scratch, template, speech_act, politeness)` reduces, then binds into a new frame each role a reduction's role term names with the head of its argument, and the root's head as the action, every binding at 1.0; a role the frame refuses (an affect concept wider than sixteen bits) is `LanguageError::RoleRefused`; the frame is returned unsealed. `concept_in(frame, role)` reads a bound role. `encode_frame(frame, roles, book)` is the bundle, in slot order (`ROLES`), of each bound role's body bound to its concept's body in the codebook (the concept id is the index); `read_role(sealed, role, book)` is Layer 1, the unbound body's nearest entry with its distance and confidence; `decode_frame` binds every role whose readout reaches `DECODE_FLOOR_Q16` (0.125) with that confidence, so the frame's confidence is the weakest readout. Nothing allocates.
- **What the exit test holds** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/language.rs`; a lexicon table of seven words exists in the test alone). "the dog chased the cat" reduces in four steps (the dog; chased the, by forward composition; cat; the subject) into a causative frame with dog, chase and cat in subject, action and object at a confidence of 1.0, complete, sealed, and realised as subject–action–object; encoded on a codebook of sixteen seeded bodies it is the bundle of three bound pairs, and each role reads back at 2 574, 2 562 and 2 482 bits (a quarter of the width) with confidences 0.497, 0.500 and 0.515, the affect role at 4 997 (chance, confidence 0.024) and left unbound; the decoded frame is the same three concepts with the subject's readout as its confidence, and renders the same words; the swapped sentence reads back swapped at 2 532, 2 635 and 2 524 with its affect at 5 037; "the bird slept" binds a subject and an action and leaves the causative template incomplete; "dog cat" is `NoDerivation` with nothing bound, an empty sequence `Empty`, and the object relative leaves three; the whole path is bit-identical on a second run, and the AArch64 job holds every pinned number.
- **Not adopted.** *Option 1*: a record change and a format bump for what a compound expresses. *Option 3*: complete but $O(n^3)$ with a chart to size; the next decision when H-10's rate demands it. *Option 4*, the Dominey gate: a second owner for the role assignment on a token path that does not exist; what "a function word fires the gate" is, symbolically, is the application rule firing when the functor's argument arrives. *The stance record* of the proposal: three of its five axes have owners (the illocutionary force is `speech_act_type` and `intended_speech_act`; the register is `politeness_level`, set from `cortex-social`'s trust; the epistemic hedge is the epistemic template's affect role and the frame's confidence) and the other two have no reader, so a record for them fails ADR-0016's third test. *"Zero semantic distortion"* into code, proofs or a voice is not a claim the tree can make.

### Consequences

- Good: R-9's comprehension direction exists end to end on ids, and its realisation direction has its Layer 1; every step is a tested integer rule in the crate that owns the quantity, composed in the runtime.
- Good: no record changed, no format moved, no executor field, no allocation; the determinism pin is untouched by construction.
- Good: the derivation carries the role assignment, so the frame's one owner keeps it.
- Bad: the reducer is greedy and incomplete for the grammar: an object relative, a coordination and any construction that needs type raising are `NoDerivation` until a chart exists (H-10).
- Bad: a lexicon must keep one shape per atom and three children per slash, or categories that a linguist would call equal clash on arity; the runtime's test lexicon is the pattern.
- Bad: the runtime module has no reader in the engine: the workspace slot that names a hypervector (§5.2.8) and the lexicon that realises a frame are Specified, so `comprehend` and `decode_frame` are called by tests and by a host, not by the loop.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Roles as a fourth kind of node** would change `TermNode`'s kind set and the loader; a constant in a reserved band costs nothing.
- **A role on the argument category** instead of the slash loses under composition: the composed slash keeps the outer role only if it is on the slash.
- **A reducer that allocates the result nodes of applications** would need a free node per step; an application's result is a child index that exists already, so only a composition pushes a node.
- **Reporting the head instead of the argument** in the log would fix what a reader wants of an argument; the index lets the runtime take the head under the final bindings, which a composition's argument only has after the parse.

## Confirmation

`cortex-reasoning` (`category.rs`): the slash, result, argument and role of both functors and none of an atom; forward application with its role and head; backward application; the transitive sentence with determiners (four steps in the stated order, one node for the composition, the roles and heads through the bindings, the composition's argument the determiner); the modal by forward composition passing the subject and its role through; the adverb by backward composition with a subjectless phrase and the subject applied in a second reduction over the same scratch; a non-sentence, an empty sequence, a lone category and the object relative as results; a stack of two, an arena with no free node, a log of one, a trail of one, a stack of one, an index outside the arena, a slash without its three children and a trail length past the trail, each a result with nothing new bound and nothing pushed; the head through functors and bindings and its refusals; a full trail still reducing a pair that binds nothing; an application that cannot be logged undone without touching the arena; determinism and the trail's undo; a property walk of three thousand random sequences from eight lexical shapes (never a panic; every index inside the arena; the trail names exactly the bound variables; every node dereferences; the steps are one fewer than the shifts on a derivation). `cortex-runtime` (`language.rs`, `tests/language.rs`): the band's rules; encoding over bound roles only, refused outside the book; the affect role read, encoded and decoded like the others; the three exit tests above. `npx spec-guard` asserts `CATEGORY_FORWARD`, `fn reduce` and `fn comprehend`. The mutation gate on the changed lines ([ADR-0030](0030-verification-governance.md)) passes in CI.
