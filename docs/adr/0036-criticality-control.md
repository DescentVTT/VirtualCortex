# ADR-0036: Criticality control — the branching ratio by lag-one regression over bins of population activity in the homeostasis record, a bounded global synaptic gain as the actuator, stepped once per window by the executor and persisted in the image; per-unit scaling Specified; format version 12

## Context and Problem Statement

Whitepaper §8.8 carried the row "Self-organised criticality | `cortex-homeostasis` | Rescale weights by $1 - \kappa(\sigma - 1)$ during sleep to hold the branching ratio at 1 | Specified", with the equation $W_{ij}(t+1) = W_{ij}(t)[1 - \kappa(\sigma - 1)]$ in its reference block: multiplicative on every weight, an $O(S)$ sweep over the synapse arena, which is why it was confined to sleep. `HomeostaticDrivePool` held `branching_ratio_q16`, set by `update_branching_ratio(descendants, ancestors)` (ADR-0020) from a causal count nothing produced ("the spike tally that feeds $\sigma$: Specified", §5.2.16), a `target_threshold_bias` nothing read, a `thermal_stress` that `cortex-autonomic` owns since ADR-0016, and 32 reserved bytes. The proposal of brief 018 asked for a closed loop driving $\sigma$ to 1, multiplicative scaling "bypassing $O(N)$ individual synaptic sweeps", "mesoscopic columnar annealing" by seeded jitter, and an immune "quench" of hypersynchrony. Which estimator, which actuator, where do they live, and what is claimed for them?

Three facts shaped the answer. A common factor on every weight arriving at a unit is a gain on that unit's input sums, applied once per turn by the turn holder (ADR-0023's phase 1), so the whitepaper's equation costs one multiply per turn and no sweep. The branching ratio of a fully observed population can be read from its activity alone, without attributing a spike to its cause: the lag-one least-squares slope of activity per bin against the bin before is $\sigma$ for a stationary branching process observed at its generation time (Wilting and Priesemann 2018), and the executor observes every spike (ADR-0035's tally). And the record had room for the estimator's window only by taking back the two fields with two owners or none.

## Decision Drivers

- §8.8's equation and its references (Beggs and Plenz 2003; Turrigiano 2008 for scaling): the rule that is missing is a closed loop on $\sigma$ with a multiplicative actuator, not a rate target.
- TC-5 and ADR-0023: no sweep over blocks in the loop, no new phase, no new barrier; ADR-0035 gives the schedule.
- §8.3 and the review finding of PR #44: anything that changes what a run does is in the image, always written and required; the reference dynamics must be unchanged at the default (the modulator's baseline precedent, ADR-0032), so the determinism pin holds.
- ADR-0016's rule that every quantity keeps one owner: `thermal_stress` is a hardware vital (`cortex-autonomic`, `core_temperature_milli_c`), and `target_threshold_bias` was a placeholder for an actuator this decision names.
- ADR-0010 and principle 3: what the loop does on a 64-unit test network is stated as what it is; what it does on the reference population is a hypothesis with a protocol.
- ADR-0031: $\kappa$, the gain and the bins change behaviour; none joins the amendment registry.

## Considered Options

1. The sweep as written: rescale every weight by $1 - \kappa(\sigma - 1)$ during sleep.
2. A rate controller: a per-image target rate, the gain moved by the rate error (Turrigiano's rule at the population).
3. **The lag-one regression estimator over bins of population activity, in the homeostasis record; a global synaptic gain in $[0.25, 4]$ as the actuator, moved once per window by a step bounded by $\kappa \le 0.5$ and applied to every unit's input sums by its turn holder; a saturation ceiling for the regime the slope cannot read; composed by the executor on cadences and persisted as section kind 43; $\kappa$ at 0 by default.**
4. A per-unit gain in the unit record, lowered at each spike and relaxing between spikes (cell-autonomous scaling).

## Decision Outcome

Option 3, with option 4 Specified.

- **The record** (`cortex-homeostasis`, format version 12). `HomeostaticDrivePool` is: `energy_level` `[0..4)`, `sensory_fatigue` `[4..8)`, `curiosity_drive` `[8..12)`, `bin_activity: u32` `[12..16)` (the spikes counted in the open bin; `thermal_stress` removed, finding F-28), `circadian_phase: u16` `[16..18)` (the rule masked it to sixteen bits), `sleep_mode_active: u8` `[18)`, `window_bins: u8` `[19)`, `control_step_q0_16: u16` `[20..22)` ($\kappa$), `_reserved: u16` `[22..24)`, `branching_ratio_q16` `[24..28)` (the last estimate), `synaptic_gain_q16: u32` `[28..32)` (`target_threshold_bias` removed: the gain is the actuator), `sum_prev: u64` `[32..40)`, `sum_prev_sq: u64` `[40..48)`, `sum_pair: u64` `[48..56)` (the window's three sums), `last_activity: u32` `[56..60)`, `first_activity: u32` `[60..64)`. `new()` is the record at rest with the gain at 1.0, equal to `Default`; an all-zero record has a gain of 0 and is refused. `encode` and `decode` are the 64 bytes little-endian; `is_well_formed` is what the rules can produce between windows (the gain in its bounds, $\kappa \le 0.5$, fewer bins than a window, every count at or below the cap, every sum at or below what the pairs and the cap allow, no first or last bin without a bin, the sleep flag 0 or 1, the reserved bytes zero).
- **The tally and the bins.** `count_activity(spikes)` adds the tick's population count to the open bin, saturating at `ACTIVITY_COUNT_MAX` ($2^{24} - 1$, so that a window's sums fit `u64` and the estimator's products `i64`). `close_bin()` closes the bin into the window: the first bin of a window is remembered, every later one pairs with the bin before it into the three sums, the open bin restarts at zero; refused with nothing changed when the window is full. A bin is $2^{12}$ ticks (`ACTIVITY_BIN_SHIFT`, 40.96 ms): at or above the wheel's horizon, asserted at compile time in the runtime, so that every direct descendant of a bin's spikes lands in that bin or the next; below the horizon the slope would read a delayed network as sub-critical and the loop would raise the gain without bound. A window is $2^5$ bins (`ACTIVITY_WINDOW_SHIFT`, 1.31 s).
- **The estimator.** `estimate_branching_ratio()` is $\hat\sigma = (n \sum ab - \sum a \sum b) / (n \sum a^2 - (\sum a)^2)$ over the window's $n$ pairs $(a, b)$ of previous and current bin, with $\sum b = \sum a - a_0 + a_n$, Q16.16 rounded to nearest, clamped to $[0, 16]$ (`SIGMA_MAX_Q16`); the intercept absorbs the external drive. A window without a spike is $\hat\sigma = 0$ (no descendants); a window whose activity never varied, or with fewer than two pairs, is no estimate; a negative slope is 0. `is_saturated(ceiling)` is true when the window's activity is at or above `ceiling` per bin on average; a saturated population no longer forms the branching process the slope reads, so `regulate` takes such a window as $\hat\sigma = 16$. The executor's ceiling is one spike per unit per bin (about 24 Hz across the population).
- **The controller.** `regulate(ceiling)`: with an estimate, it is stored as `branching_ratio_q16` and the gain moves by $g \leftarrow g\,(1 - \kappa\,\operatorname{clamp}(\hat\sigma - 1, -1, 1))$, the step $\kappa e$ and the product each rounded to nearest, the gain clamped to $[0.25, 4.0]$ (`GAIN_MIN_Q16`, `GAIN_MAX_Q16`); without one the gain stays; either way the window's pairs are cleared. $\kappa$ is Q0.16 at most 0.5 (`CONTROL_STEP_MAX_Q0_16`), so one window moves the gain by half at most and the factor stays positive; at $\kappa = 0$ the gain never moves and the estimate is still stored. The causal form `update_branching_ratio` stays for a caller with attribution.
- **The composition** (`cortex-runtime`, which now depends on `cortex-homeostasis`). `Config::control_step_q0_16` (default 0; refused above 0.5, `ConfigError::ControlStepOutOfRange`). The executor holds one `HomeostaticDrivePool` for the engine (macro-columns do not exist yet), exposes it between ticks, and before every tick publishes its gain to the workers beside the modulation; in phase 1 the turn holder scales the unit's basal and apical sums by the gain, rounded to nearest, before `integrate` (exact at 1.0, so the reference dynamics are bit for bit the old ones, and the determinism pin of ADR-0030 holds unmoved). After every tick the coordinator sums the workers' spike counts (ADR-0035) into `count_activity`; on the bin's cadence it closes the bin; on the window's cadence it regulates with the ceiling. Both cadences are masks on the tick, so a loaded engine continues the window it was written in.
- **Persistence** (format version 12). Section kind 43, `SECTION_HOMEOSTASIS`, holds the record's 64 bytes, always written and required (`MissingSection`); the loader refuses a count other than one (`Directory`), a step above its bound (`Config(ControlStepOutOfRange)`), a record that is not well formed, and a record whose `window_bins` is not the count the clock at the write implies (`MalformedHomeostasis`). The image's step and gain outrank the configuration's: the image defines the run (§8.3), and a trial's forks (ADR-0031) run under the live engine's gain and continue its window.
- **What the exit test holds** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/criticality.rs`). On a network without synapses and a designed number of kicks per bin, the executor's record equals an oracle record fed the same counts after every window, exactly: a window growing by a tenth per bin reads above 1 and lowers the gain, one decaying by a tenth reads below 1 and raises it, a straight line reads exactly 1 and leaves it, a window at the ceiling reads as saturated and lowers it by an eighth, a window one below the ceiling on average is regulated by its slope. A silent engine's gain climbs by half per window to 4.0 and stops there exactly, and one tick short of a window boundary nothing has moved. A recurrent 64-unit network (eight synapses per unit to two targets, delays near the horizon, weights near the rail) begets almost no spike at a gain of 1.0; the loop raises the gain by an eighth per window while the slope reads sub-critical, and eight windows in every kick begets several spikes, the estimate is above a quarter, the gain between 1.5 and 3, and the population far below the ceiling; with $\kappa = 0$ the gain stays at 1.0 and the estimate is still taken. The loop is bit-identical on one and four workers. The state round-trips through the image mid-window and a loaded engine, under a configuration that says $\kappa = 0$, continues alike.
- **What is not claimed.** That the branching ratio settles at 1 on a network. The estimator reads the slope of population activity across bins, which is $\sigma$ when the activity is a stationary branching process observed at about its generation time; a 64-unit network with depleting synapses produces avalanches that finish inside one bin, which the slope reads as sub-critical, and its short-term depression keeps it far from the ceiling, so on such a network the loop raises the gain toward its ceiling and the estimate stays below 1. The regime the estimator is written for is the reference population's; whether it reads $\sigma$ there is hypothesis H-8, with the protocol: a reference image, a traced run, the estimate against a causal count from the trace. The ceiling's value (one spike per unit per bin), the bin, the window and the gain's bounds are M5's tuning, stated as Targets. No claim that the loop learns or protects anything beyond the bounds it holds by construction: the gain never leaves $[0.25, 4]$ and never moves by more than $\kappa$ per window.
- **Specified: per-unit multiplicative scaling** (Turrigiano 2008, cell-autonomous). A gain per unit in `[52..54)` of `DendriticSuperNeuron` (Q2.14), lowered by a step at each of the unit's spikes and relaxing toward its ceiling between spikes by $(1 - 2^{-k})^{\Delta t}$ at the next event, the pattern of ADR-0019, so that a silent unit's gain rises and a busy one's falls with no sweep and no cadence, applied by the turn holder beside the population gain. Its fixed point is where the steps per unit time equal the relaxation; it is the slow loop under the adaptive threshold of ADR-0018 (41 ms) and beside this decision's population loop (1.31 s). Written down in §8.8; not implemented here, since a second per-unit controller on the same input needs the first's measurement.
- **Not adopted.** *Columnar annealing* by seeded jitter at the thalamic relay: a seed is part of the run's definition (§8.3), so it is admissible when needed, but nothing in the tree exhibits the attractor deadlock it would escape, and a controller whose step is bounded already cannot lock the gain at a rail against the estimate. *An immune quench* of hypersynchrony: the branching ratio has one owner (`cortex-homeostasis`), and a second actuator in `cortex-immune` gives the quantity two, against ADR-0016's rule; `ImmuneScrubNode` is memory hygiene (§5.2.13). *A threshold bias as the actuator*: the whitepaper's equation is multiplicative, a bias interacts with an unconfigured threshold (zero, which never fires), and a gain has no such edge; the field is gone.

### Consequences

- Good: §8.8's criticality row is Implemented as a closed loop that costs one multiply per turn, one store per worker per tick and one regression per window, with no sweep; the tally R-12 called Specified exists.
- Good: the reference dynamics are unchanged at the default; the pin holds; a run is still `(image, seed, trace)` with the loop's state in the image.
- Good: the two fields with two owners or none are gone (F-28), and the record's reserved bytes became a rule.
- Bad: format version 12 makes version-11 images unreadable, as every bump does.
- Bad: the estimator's regime is not the test network's; what the loop does at scale is a hypothesis (H-8), and a network outside the regime is raised toward the gain's ceiling by a slope that reads it as sub-critical. The ceiling bounds that, and the bounds are the guarantee: nothing stronger is claimed.
- Bad: one gain for the engine; one per macro-column is Specified with the columns.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Option 1** is the sweep: $O(\text{blocks})$ per regulation over an arena Appendix A sizes at $2^{26}$ blocks, and confined to sleep for that reason; the gain gives the same factor per unit at one multiply.
- **Option 2** needs a target rate per image with no canonical value; $\sigma = 1$ is canonical. A rate ceiling is kept as the guard for the regime the slope cannot read.
- **Option 4 now**: a second per-unit controller on the input sums before the first is measured; its bytes and its rule are written so that the round that measures can take it.
- **The ratio of means** ($\langle N_{t+1} \rangle / \langle N_t \rangle$, the whitepaper's causal definition read over bins): for any finished avalanche the descendants and the ancestors sum to the same spikes but one, so the ratio reads 1 whatever $\sigma$ was; the causal form stays for a caller with attribution.
- **A multistep regression** (lags 1 to $k$, Wilting and Priesemann's full form): robust to the bin, but $k$ more sums than the record holds; the round that measures H-8 decides whether the record needs a second line.
- **$\kappa$ or the gain in the amendment registry**: they change behaviour (ADR-0031).

## Confirmation

`cortex-homeostasis`: the record at rest and its bounds; the circadian rule at its widths; the bin's cap at the close; the pairing into the sums and the full window refused; the estimate as the slope with an intercept (halving, doubling, doubling over a drive, a straight line, alternation, the cap, rounding to nearest both ways, silence, one bin, one pair, a constant window); regulation at $\kappa = 0$, at the largest step from above and below 1, at a quarter step, rounding of the step and of the product, both rails from on and beside them, saturation at and one below the ceiling, the open bin surviving a regulation; every clause of `is_well_formed` on its own with the bounds' neighbours; the bytes; the property walk (every rule keeps a well-formed record well formed, the estimate within its range, the gain within its bounds, and at $\kappa = 0$ the gain never moves). `cortex-runtime/tests/criticality.rs`: the six tests above; `tests/image.rs`: the section's count, size and absence, the step above its bound, and seven malformed records refused on their own, and the image's state read back; `tests/differential.rs`: the pin unmoved with the configuration's new field spelled out. The mutation gate on the changed lines (ADR-0030) passes in CI.
