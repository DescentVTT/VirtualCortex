# ADR-0048: Episodes tagged from the spike train — the densest span and the pattern that fired most as rules of `cortex-hippocampus`, the runtime's composition with the ledger and with a rewarded invention (the pattern active in the ripple before the reward, associated with the invented predicate's id in the caller's table); what a night does to a pattern the rule finds in an experience, to the network's own densest coincidence, and to the invention's, at 256 and 1 024 units; H-9's open item and H-11's synaptic half decided at that scale; the online capture Specified

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0044](0044-reference-network.md) held that a night's replay drives every synapse among a tagged pattern to the rail and that a cue of half a local cluster fires the rest, and left H-9's open item: "whether a pattern the network's own activity produces (tagged from the trace) is of the completing kind". [ADR-0045](0045-clause-search.md) decided H-11's symbolic half and left its synaptic half open with the rule that would close it: "writes an `Episode` from an invention (the pattern active when the reward came, tagged for replay), which is a decision for a round that has a representation of an id in the population". The tree's episodes were placed: `Executor::tag_episode` took a pattern its caller chose, and no rule read one from what the network did. The proposal of brief 023 asked for "an online coincidence-detection or avalanche-capture mechanism that monitors the active spike train during waking execution", packaging co-firing units into an `Episode` "within a ripple length", a night with "SWS downscaling", and, for H-11, capturing "the units that were co-active during that discovery window into an `Episode` tagged with the invented predicate's ID band".

Three facts shaped the answer. A rule over a spike train is a rule over a caller's slice of `(tick, unit)`, which every fork of ADR-0044 returns sorted and which `cortex-hippocampus`, the owner of the episode, can hold without a dependency; the runtime composes it with the ledger. A worker's spikes are its own thread's until it stops (`WorkerReport::spikes` exists at shutdown), so a capture inside the tick that is bit-identical on every worker count needs a merge across workers the executor does not have; `Image::encode` refuses a non-quiescent engine, so the train of a waking run is a fork's from the image the run started at, under the same drive and cues, which is the same run (§8.3). And a pattern ranked by spike count over a whole ripple is the drive's, not the network's: the first draft's rule ranked the units that fired most in the 2 048 ticks before the reward and found units the stationary drive had fired three times beside the experience's twelve; the span that shows what fired *together* is a basal time constant, the span the cued units' messages must land within for a pattern to complete (ADR-0044).

## Decision Drivers

- ADR-0016's one-owner rule: the rules that read a pattern from a train are `cortex-hippocampus`'s, beside the record they fill; the composition with the ledger, the reward and the forks is the runtime's.
- §8.3: a tag is an input between ticks; a pattern found in the train of a run and tagged at a stated tick is part of the trace, and a run that replays it is the same run.
- ADR-0043's precedent: the affect state, the store and the codebook are the caller's until a loop in the executor writes them; the association between an invented predicate and a ledger index is the same kind of table, and a field in `Episode` for it would be a field the loop never reads (F-30's class).
- ADR-0010 and principle 3: the night's numbers are the engine's on a stated network at a stated size; the gate holds 256 units, the weekly job 1 024.
- Every loop ends by construction: the burst walks the slice with `take_while`, the capture with a bounded insertion, the discovery's span by two binary searches.

## Considered Options

1. The proposal: an online capture inside the tick with a co-firing threshold, an episode field for the predicate's id, a night with downscaling.
2. **`burst` and `capture` in `cortex-hippocampus` over a caller's train; in the runtime `tag_from_trace`, `tag_burst` and `tag_discovery`, the last tagging the densest basal time constant of the ripple before a positive reward and returning the association; the exit test tagging an experience's pattern through the reward, the network's own densest coincidence through the burst, and holding the night and the readouts.**
3. The pattern as the units that fired within `latency` of each other in a sliding window, without ranking (the first `PATTERN_MAX` distinct).
4. A concept field in `Episode`'s reserved bytes, format 14.

## Decision Outcome

Option 2; no record changes.

- **The rules** (`cortex-hippocampus`, module `capture`). `burst(train, window) -> Option<Burst { from, spikes }>`: the densest span of `window` ticks over a train sorted by tick, the earliest at equal counts, every span starting at a spike's tick (a span that starts between spikes holds no more than the one starting at the next spike); `None` for an empty train or a window of zero. `capture(train, from, window, out) -> u8`: the distinct units that fired in `[from, from + window)`, ranked by their spikes in the span, then by their first spike, then by index, the first `PATTERN_MAX` written to `out` in that order and the rest of `out` zero; every unit written fired in the span and none twice, so `Episode::tag` accepts the result whenever it is not empty. Both are walks over the slice; a property walk of three thousand random trains holds the capture against a brute-force ranking and the burst against every start tick.
- **The composition** (the runtime, module `episode`). `tag_from_trace(exec, train, from, window, priority)` captures the span and tags it into the executor's ledger between ticks, returning the index and the pattern (`InvalidPattern` for a span with no spike; the ledger's refusals as `tag_episode` gives them); `tag_burst(exec, train, window, priority)` tags the densest span's pattern. `tag_discovery(exec, train, at, window, coincidence, discovery, priority)`: when the discovery's reward is positive, the spikes of `[at - window, at)` (from tick zero when `at` is nearer than the window) are found by two binary searches and the densest span of `coincidence` ticks among them is tagged and returned as `Association { predicate, episode, pattern, len }`, the invented predicate's id beside the ledger index; a reward that is not positive tags nothing. The association is the caller's table, as the store is: no record holds it, and the round that runs discoveries inside the executor's loop is the round that moves it into the image.
- **What the exit test holds** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/reference.rs`, the gate at 256 units on the lattice of ADR-0044 at a gain of 2.0 with the modulation baseline at 1.0 and the sleep shift 5). The network awake under the drive for two bins; the experience of ADR-0044's night (a cue of two messages of 1.25 into each of the twelve excitatory neighbours among units 0 to 14) at the third bin's start; a quarter of a ripple later the store of ADR-0045's exit test searched between ticks (two commits, a reward total of 1.5 passed to the modulator, whose signal reads 1.5); the run's train to that tick from a fork of the start image under the same drive and cues, 436 spikes. The invention's pattern, from the densest basal time constant of the ripple before the reward (a span starting at tick 8 088 holding 62 spikes, the experience at 8 192 inside it): units 7, 0, 1, 8, 10, 12, 2, 5, 6, 11, 13 and 163, eleven of the twelve cued, each firing three times in the span, and one unit the drive fired as often, in rank order; tagged as episode 0 with the invention's predicate (`INVENTED_BASE`). The network's own pattern, from the densest basal time constant of the two bins before the experience: a span starting at tick 3 218 holding 64 spikes, a cascade around the ring's wrap, units 241, 18, 151, 149, 247, 245, 0, 253, 254, 255, 35 and 110; tagged as episode 1. A store of two facts commits nothing and a discovery without a reward tags nothing. The night as ADR-0044's (three windows of slow-wave sleep, two of REM, two more, awake; 376 replays, 128 depotentiations, both tags from 200 to 136). The invention's pattern holds 127 synapses among itself, 1 117 805 in sum before the night and 3 193 610 after (25 146 on average): most at the rail, and the eleven from unit 0 at the negative rail, since unit 0 is in both patterns and fires in the other episode's replays too, where the pair rule at its spikes finds its targets' last spikes a ripple earlier and depresses them. The network's own pattern holds 45, 175 285 before and 825 276 after (18 339 on average). The readout, a cue of the first six of each pattern in its rank order on a fork of the image before and after the night: the invention's rest fires none before (18 spikes, the cued six) and five of six after (32 spikes; units 2, 5, 6, 11 and 13 fire, unit 163, with one synapse from the pattern, does not); the network's own rest fires none, before or after. *At 1 024 units* (the weekly form): 1 370 spikes to the reward's tick; the invention's pattern is all twelve cued neighbours in rank order (2, 0, 3, 10, 11, 13, 1, 5, 7, 12, 6, 8), the 143 synapses among them (ADR-0044's count at this size) every one at the rail after the night; the network's own densest coincidence a span at tick 4 238 holding 153 spikes, twelve units of which three (149, 499 and 1 014) are inhibitory, with twenty synapses among them summing to −77 824 before the night and every one at the positive rail after it: the pair rule's potentiation carries an inhibitory synapse among a replayed pattern across zero, which is finding F-36. The readouts: the invention's other six fire none before and five after (31 spikes; the cue in rank order differs from ADR-0044's cue in index order, which fired all six), the network's own never.
- **What is decided.** *H-9's open item at 256 and 1 024 units under this prior and this drive:* a pattern the rule finds in an experience (the densest coincidence of the ripple that held it) is of the completing kind, five of six on a cue of the other six, with one outsider the drive added; a pattern the network's own activity produces under the stationary drive (its densest coincidence, a cascade of 64 spikes in a basal time constant) holds 45 synapses among twelve units and does not complete after the same night. The tree now tags from the train and the readout says what such a pattern does; what the network's own activity is at Appendix A's scale, and under an input that is not a stationary drive, is a Target with the same rules. *H-11's synaptic half at this scale:* a rewarded invention has a pattern of units in the ledger, tagged from the activity of the ripple before its reward; the night consolidates it; a cue of half of it fires the rest of the cued neighbours; the association between the invented predicate's id and the pattern is the caller's table, so "a later behaviour reads the invention" means a caller that cues the pattern its table names, which the readout is. What the reward consolidates at the moment it comes is the two-unit test's of ADR-0043, unchanged: at the reference network's baseline of 1.0 the signal changes no weight. *Interference:* two episodes sharing a unit depress, in each other's replays, the shared unit's synapses into the other pattern to the negative rail; the completion survived it here (the cued six did not include unit 0's targets alone), and a ripple order or a tag rule that keeps replayed patterns apart is M5's tuning, a Target. *The sign of a synapse:* replay potentiates every synapse among a pattern whatever its sign, so an inhibitory synapse among a pattern the rule captured ends at the excitatory rail after a night (finding F-36); a pattern a caller places can avoid inhibitory units, a pattern the network's own activity produces cannot, and a plasticity rule that conserves the sign (or an inhibitory rule of its own) is a decision for its own ADR, since it moves the pair rule every pinned number rests on.
- **Not adopted.** *Option 1's online capture*: Specified, with its requirement: a per-tick merge of every worker's spikes in unit order, bit-identical on every worker count, and a cadence for the capture, taken by the round that also runs discoveries inside the loop. *Option 1's downscaling*: ADR-0037 adopted none. *Option 3*: a window without ranking takes the first twelve to fire, which under the drive are the drive's. *Option 4*: a field the loop never reads and a format bump for a table the caller holds. *The first draft's ranking over the whole ripple*: the reason in the Context.

### Consequences

- Good: an episode has a rule that reads it from what the network did; H-9's protocol ("episodes tagged from its own activity") is met at this scale, with the answer it gives.
- Good: the synaptic half of H-11 has the rule ADR-0045 asked for, and an exit test that cues the invention's pattern and counts what fires.
- Good: no record, no field, no format bump; the pin holds.
- Bad: the capture is offline, from a fork's train; the executor cannot tag from its own run until it merges its workers' spikes.
- Bad: the network's own pattern at this density does not complete; the transfer the literature describes needs a denser assembly than the stationary drive's cascades produce here, or a night that replays one episode.
- Bad: the interference between overlapping episodes is recorded, not resolved.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Ranking by first spike alone**: ties every unit of a volley and puts a drive spike before them.
- **A coincidence window of 64 ticks** (one volley): a background spike a few ticks before the volley starts the densest span and ranks first at one spike each; a basal time constant ranks the volley's three spikes above it.
- **Two nights, one per episode**: the interference would not be seen, and the gate would take a second night.

## Confirmation

`cortex-hippocampus` (`capture.rs`): a span's ranking by spikes, first spike and index, its bounds, the widening past the clock's width, a full pattern keeping the twelve best and dropping the worst for a better one, a thirteenth no better not written; the burst's densest span, the earliest at equal counts, a window past the width, at the clock's width; the property walk. The runtime (`episode.rs`): a span tagged in rank order, an empty span, a unit outside the arena, a full ledger; the burst's pattern, an empty train, a window of zero; a positive reward tagging the densest coincidence, a coincidence as long as the window, zero and a negative reward tagging nothing, the least positive reward, a window past the reward's tick, the reward's tick outside the window, no spike. `tests/reference.rs`: the gate's night above; the `exhaustive` night at 1 024 units. `npx spec-guard` asserts `fn capture` and `fn tag_discovery`. The mutation gate on the changed lines (ADR-0030) passes in CI.
