# ADR-0096: The punished pair, measured — H-18 is yes: on H-17's configuration with the signed gate set, an engine that learned the answer in 128 of the last 128 trials before the flip selected the new answer in 128 of the last 128 at the 4 608th trial, in both arms; the old answer's pairs, punished against their traces at 9 000 to 13 300 a punished trial in the first block after the flip, fell back to 0.95 to 1.06 of the image's couplings while each stimulus's selection crossed in the fourteenth to the twentieth block after the flip, and stopped once they were no longer selected; a punishment potentiated about a fifth of the addressed synapse-trials it reached, those whose trace was negative, and depressed slightly more; ADR-0093's three predicted readings held; the assertion held; the calibration reproduced ADR-0077; H-18's stopping rule at step 3, the configuration the engine learns and revises in named, and the next decision named and not taken

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0093](0093-the-punished-pair-consolidates-against-its-trace.md) chose, of the two mechanisms H-17's stopping rule named, the depression under the gate — a **signed gate**, under which an addressed excitatory synapse consolidates under `clamp(baseline + dopamine, −1, 1)`, a punishment moving its weight against its trace's sign and spending the trace by as much — and wrote **H-18** in whitepaper §11.1 before any run: [H-17](0091-the-assignment-reversed-measured.md)'s configuration — [ADR-0077](0077-the-background-side.md)'s settled image, the excitatory baseline zero, the inhibitory baseline 0.5, [ADR-0076](0076-two-injections.md)'s stimulus, the task as built under `Delivery::Addressed` and `Feedback::Answer`, the mapping flipped between the 1 536th trial and the 1 537th by `Task::mirrored` — with the signed gate set and **4 608 trials**, the second mapping 3 072 long; two arms, the assignment first and the mirrored first; the criterion (clause 1, at least 80 of trials 1 409 to 1 536 correct under the first mapping; clause 2, at least 80 of trials 4 481 to 4 608 under the second; a tie not correct; yes when both hold in both arms, a failure of clause 1 a no about the gate); an assertion; the readings; **no prediction for the verdict** and three predicted readings; and its stopping rule. [ADR-0094](0094-the-signed-gate-built.md) built the gate (the modulator section's `[25]`, format 16); [ADR-0095](0095-an-image-pin-moves-with-its-format.md) amended the stopping rule's step 2 before any run, because the one whole-image pin of the tree moves with the format. Brief 041 is the one round the rule allows. This ADR records it.

## Decision Drivers

- **Nothing chosen after a rewarded run.** H-18's constants, arms, length, flip, criterion, assertion and stopping rule are ADR-0093's, its stopping rule as ADR-0095 amended it; the mechanism, the amendment and the constants were committed in that order before the first rewarded run (on the branch `2a02862`, `8bc6e94` and `8e288d7`, the last at 21:04 on 2026-09-24, the arms started at 21:06), and no clause was dropped or added because of what a run read.
- **The calibration before anything else**, and a mismatch or a moved number a stop (H-18's stopping rule, step 2).
- **The assertion and the readings computed beside the verdict and not in place of it**: each arm's run dumps everything and computes the clauses, the assertion and the readings before anything is held.
- **The engine read before a description of it is trusted**: a trial's delivery is consolidated in the trial after it (ADR-0090), so the moves a punishment makes are read in the next trial; the signed modulation reaches an addressed excitatory synapse and no other (ADR-0094), which every replayed stimulus–readout synapse is.
- Latest ≠ Newest: the rule is ADR-0093's and nothing newer; no dependency, no tool.
- [ADR-0088](0088-a-fourth-shard.md): the floor of a weekly shard is its longest test, so the two arms are two tests; [ADR-0092](0092-the-shards-dealt-by-cost.md): the round regenerates the cost table from its own dispatch.
- [ADR-0075](0075-the-dispatch-scope-follows-the-diff.md): the dispatch's scope follows the diff, and the clause that applied is stated here.

## Considered Options

1. **Where the arms live**: `tests/inhibition.rs`, beside H-16 and H-17 on the shared harness; or a binary of their own.
2. **What is pinned**: the whole run; or the second half only.
3. **How the oracle reaches the signed branch**: a branch of the shared `Composer` (`Composer::signed`, `consolidated_signed`), `earned_run_flipped` its unsigned case; or a copy of the harness's run.
4. **How "a punishment potentiated" is read**: per addressed synapse per trial, by the sign of what the trial's consolidations moved it (`Composer::moves`); or per consolidation event.
5. **How many ADRs**: the mechanism, the amendment and this one; or fewer.

### Consequences

- Good: the engine learns and revises on this task: after 1 536 trials of one mapping and 3 072 of the other it answers the second as it answered the first, 128 of 128, in both arms.
- Good: the need ADR-0091 measured was met by the mechanism ADR-0093 chose, with every constant written first; the old answer's lead came back to the image's within 896 to 1 280 trials, stimulus by stimulus.
- Good: the signed rule's cost is read, not argued: a punishment potentiates about a fifth of the synapse-trials it reaches, nearly half of those it moves, and still takes back more than it gives.
- Neutral: the verdict had no prediction; the three predicted readings held.
- Bad: the new answer's couplings end at 1.41 to 1.55 of the image's and still rising, and the inhibitory sum at a ninth of the image's; the regime this revision happened in is still in transit, and the rise has no stop but the rail.
- Bad: one reversal, one seed, one size; a second reversal from a lead of 1.4 to 1.55, or a schedule of them, is not read.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Reading "punished" only after the flip**: the gate acts in the first half too, and what it did there — wrong pairs depressed, the learning a little faster — is part of the configuration's answer.
- **One test for both arms**: ADR-0088's floor is the longest test; two arms of 4 608 trials in one test would be the longest in the tree.
- **Reading the next decision into this ADR**: H-18's stopping rule gives it to an ADR of its own.
