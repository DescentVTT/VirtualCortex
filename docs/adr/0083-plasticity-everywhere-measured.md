# ADR-0083: Plasticity everywhere, measured — H-15 is no: on the network ADR-0077 settled, with the modulation baseline at 0.5, the task as built moves the selection to the answer in 106 of the last 128 trials in the mirrored assignment and in 78 in the assignment, two short of the mark; the unrewarded consolidation carries every stimulus–readout coupling up by a third to a half of the image's over 1 536 trials and faster onto readout 1, so the withheld arm drifts there for both stimuli as predicted, the reward parts an answer pair from that course by two to nine per cent, and the stimulus whose answer is readout 0 decides each arm as predicted — A never reaching its answer in the assignment; ADR-0082's assertion true on every arm, the calibration reproduced ADR-0077 first, the inhibitory sum at 0.41 of the image's after the run; H-15's stopping rule at step 4, nothing written beside H-12 and the next decision named and not taken; the harness shared as one module between `instrument.rs` and a binary of its own, named so that the round robin leaves `instrument` alone

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0082](0082-plasticity-everywhere.md) took the decision H-14's stopping rule named after [ADR-0081](0081-the-reinforced-form-measured.md)'s yes and wrote **H-15** in whitepaper §11.1 with everything fixed before any run: H-14's configuration — [ADR-0077](0077-the-background-side.md)'s settled image, the gain 1.75, the controller off, [ADR-0076](0076-two-injections.md)'s stimulus, the task as [ADR-0059](0059-a-task-a-readout-and-a-reward.md) and [ADR-0068](0068-the-reward-addressed.md) built it under `Delivery::Addressed` and `Feedback::Answer`, 1 536 trials — with one constant moved, the modulation baseline from zero to **0.5**, brief 027's `BASELINE_Q16`, the baseline the lead-in settled the network under and every rewarded run of H-12 used; the arms (the assignment, the mirrored assignment, the reward withheld as a reading); the criterion (at least 80 correct selections of the last 128 in both rewarded arms, a tie not correct); an assertion derived from the rules (the signal at a trial's end between −0.712 and 0.712, so the wrong pair consolidates under at most 0.212 after a wrong selection and the answer pair under at least 0.788 after a correct one); two predicted readings and no prediction for the verdict; the sentence for H-12 if yes; and its own stopping rule. Brief 038 is the one round the rule allows, and ADR-0082 sent its runs to a test binary of their own whose name sorts before `instrument`. This ADR records it.

The question is whether H-14's yes survives the condition its attribution rested on: at a baseline of zero nothing in the arena moved but the pairs a reward reached, which is exact for attribution and a network in which [ADR-0055](0055-a-weight-that-settles.md)'s settling and the inhibitory rule do not act. At 0.5 every synapse consolidates half of what it pairs at each presynaptic spike, whatever the selection, and the reward's term adds to or subtracts from that on the addressed pair alone.

## Decision Drivers

- **Nothing chosen after a rewarded run.** H-15's constants, arms, length, criterion, assertion, predicted readings and stopping rule are ADR-0082's; the round's integer form of the criterion, of the assertion and of the two readings is committed before the first rewarded run, and no clause is dropped or added because of what a run read.
- **The calibration before anything else**, and a mismatch a stop (H-15's stopping rule, step 2).
- **The assertion and the readings computed beside the verdict and not in place of it**: the run dumps every arm and computes the verdict, the bounds, the drift and where each stimulus reached its answer before anything is held.
- **The engine read before a description of it is trusted**: `Modulations::of` and `NeuromodulatorState::modulation` (the baseline plus the dopamine signal, saturating, clamped to $[0, 1]$; the baseline alone where a synapse is not addressed), `Task::trial` under `Feedback::Withheld` (the addressed set written, no reward, the signal read at rest), `Image::decode` (a decoded image resumes the writer's clock and its modulator section's baseline outranks the configuration's), and ADR-0079's oracle (`consolidated` clamps the modulation as the engine does) were read for the harness below. ADR-0082's 0.212 and 0.788 are the rule's exact values rounded to three decimals: with the fixed point at 46 691 in Q16.16, the bounds are 13 923 (0.2125) and 51 613 (0.7875), and the round's integer form takes those, so the clause computes as written and no amendment of ADR-0082 was needed.
- Latest ≠ Newest: no mechanism, no constant of the engine, no record, no dependency. The image is ADR-0077's, written a second time with the baseline it was settled under; the oracle is ADR-0079's with one field; the delivery is the task's own.
- [ADR-0075](0075-the-dispatch-scope-follows-the-diff.md): the dispatch's scope follows the diff, and the clause that applied is stated here.
- [ADR-0073](0073-the-whole-domain-tests-sharded.md)'s shard budget and ADR-0082's rule on the binary's name: `instrument.rs` took 4 047 s of its shard's 7 200 in ADR-0081's dispatch, and a name sorting after `instrument` would put two heavy binaries in one shard.

## Considered Options

1. **The harness shared as a module** — every item of `tests/instrument.rs` that is not a `#[test]` moved to `tests/instrument/harness.rs`, declared with `#[path]` and glob-imported by each binary, its root items `pub(crate)`; or copied into the new binary; or `include!`d whole with its tests.
2. **The binary's name**: `tests/everywhere.rs`, sorting between `cortex_core` and `instrument`.
3. **The 0.5 image as encoded** — the same quiescent engine written twice, once with the baseline patched to zero (`frozen_image`) for the calibration and once as encoded for the arms; or the zero image patched back to 0.5.
4. **The oracle under the baseline**: one field, `Composer::baseline`, the modulation the baseline plus the signal where a synapse is addressed and the baseline alone elsewhere; `earned_run` a wrapper of `earned_run_under`, which takes the feedback, the assignment and the baseline and asserts the withheld arm's zero reward and resting signal trial by trial; or a second run path.
5. **The assertion's integer form** (`bounds`): three clauses over an arm's trials, with the exact bounds.
6. **The predicted readings' integer forms**: `drift` over the withheld arm's couplings and its last 128 selections; `reached` — the trial at the end of the first block in which a stimulus's correct selections are at least 40 of 64 of its presentations — and `r0_later` over each rewarded arm.
7. **One `exhaustive` test for the three arms** from one image, the calibration a frozen block from the zero image; the gate's eight trials on the instrument's network at 1 024 units as ADR-0081's gate ran them, the assignment and the reward withheld.
8. **The shuffled arm**, not run (ADR-0082's default).

- **Why a module shared and not copied.** The harness ADR-0065 to ADR-0081 built — the network, the task, the readings, the oracle, every rule and every pinned table — is 11 700 lines, and a copy would drift from its original the first time either moved. `tests/instrument/harness.rs` is one file compiled into each binary that declares it (`#[path = "instrument/harness.rs"] mod harness; use harness::*;`), its root items `pub(crate)` so that a binary's tests see them as they did at the crate root; `tests/instrument.rs` keeps its header and its forty-five tests, the twenty-eight `exhaustive` ones and the seventeen of the gate, and `--list` after the change shows it holding twenty-eight and `everywhere` one. - **Not adopted.** *A copy of the harness, or an `include!` of `instrument.rs` whole* (option 1): above. *A name after `instrument`* (option 2): two heavy binaries in one shard, by ADR-0082's arithmetic. *The zero image patched back to 0.5* (option 3): the engine held the baseline it was settled under, and writing it as encoded reads it rather than re-deriving it. *A second replay path* (option 4): one field and one wrapper are the whole difference, and every older run is bit for bit what it was. *The shuffled arm* (option 8): not run, as ADR-0082 decided; the withheld arm is the reading of what the unrewarded consolidation does alone, and it read as predicted. *A second attempt, a constant moved, a clause dropped or added after the run*: H-15's stopping rule, step 5 — the assignment's 78 is two short of 80 and stays a no; a longer run, a larger reward or a lower baseline are each a constant moved after a rewarded run. *A claim that the engine learns beyond the scope, or does not*: the word is used with its scope — this task, these arms, these 1 536 trials, this network at 1 024 units, the baseline 0.5 — and the next decision names what the line would need to say more. *A claim about 256 units, a task beyond this one, or Appendix A's scale*: none. *A finding*: none; the assertion held, the calibration reproduced, and ADR-0082's decimals are the bounds rounded. *The sentence beside H-12*: written for a yes only, and not written.

### Consequences

- Good: the one condition H-13's and H-14's yeses rested on has been removed and its cost read: with every synapse consolidating, the reward still moves a coupling in the direction its sign says, up on the answer pair and down on the wrong one, but by a tenth of what the unrewarded consolidation moves every pair, and the selection follows the sum of the two; the no is a measurement of that ratio, not of the delivery.
- Good: both predicted readings held exactly — the drift toward readout 1 and the readout-0 stimulus deciding each arm — so the no is explained by readings written before the run and not by one found after it, and the mirrored pair shows the same drift passing and failing the same criterion under opposite answers.
- Good: the next decision is named by the rule and not chosen here, and the readings it needs are in the tree: what the baseline's plasticity does to the arena in twenty-four blocks (the inhibitory sum at 0.41, the pairs at 1.35 to 1.63), and that the reward's part is of the drift's lean's size.
- Good: the harness is one module shared by two binaries rather than two copies, the format gate still reads it, and the weekly job's heaviest binary keeps its shard to itself.
- Neutral: the gate's runtime tests gain one test of eight trials twice at 1 024 units; the weekly job gains one run of about 1.1 of ADR-0081's, in a shard estimated at about 37 per cent of its bound.
- Bad: a no on H-15 is a claim at 1 024 units on one settled network at one baseline, 0.5, with one delivery and one reward magnitude; a baseline between zero and 0.5, where the drift would be smaller and the reward's part the same, is not read and may not be tried without an ADR that argues it first.
- Bad: under 0.5 the arena is not settled at all within the run — the inhibitory sum still falling at the twenty-fourth block, the pairs still rising — so what the engine would read after a longer run is not known, and neither is whether the drift's lean is the geometry's or the regime's.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **A copy of the harness** (option 1): 11 700 lines that would drift from their original; a shared module keeps one harness and every pinned number in one place.
- **`instrument.rs` included whole** (option 1): its twenty-eight `exhaustive` tests would run twice in the weekly job, and `--list` would count them twice.
- **A name sorting after `instrument`** (option 2): `reference` or the new binary in `instrument`'s shard, past 73 per cent of its bound.
- **The zero image patched back** (option 3): a derivation where a reading suffices.
- **A second replay path** (option 4): the oracle's rule is the engine's with the baseline added; one field.
- **A clause on the withheld arm**: as ADR-0082 decided, none; it read 47 of the last 128 by the assignment's answers, below chance, because both stimuli drift to one readout, which no binomial clause describes.
- **The shuffled arm** (option 8): its budget was available, but its reading at 0.5 would have been the withheld arm's drift with a coin on top, and ADR-0082 named the withheld arm as the question's reading.
- **A longer run, a lower baseline, a larger reward**, so that the assignment's 78 reaches 80: each a constant moved after a rewarded run, H-15's stopping rule, step 5.
