# ADR-0072: What the trace is made of — the eligibility on a stimulus's synapses into a readout, read from the record at 1 024 units with the weights frozen and split by what paired it through an oracle that replays the pair rule over the executor's own train and agrees with the record at every reading; at the present gain the volley's pairing is net potentiation and the background's is net depression of four to five times it, so the sentence three decisions reasoned from is confirmed as a measurement, and its mechanism is in the census: each stimulus unit fires about twice more within one pair window after its volley, and the rule depresses once per presynaptic spike and potentiates at most once per postsynaptic one (finding F-46); a ladder of gains below the present, each rung read by the sight and by the sign, both written first, passes at none — at 1.5 the trace turns net potentiation while the readout stops seeing the stimulus — so there is no rewarded run, no constant moves, and what a stimulus that fires once would read is named and not built

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0069](0069-the-addressed-reward-measured.md) reduced the learning question to one sentence: "what the rule cannot do is turn a net-depression trace into a coupling that rises; the reward's addressing changes which synapses fall faster and not whether they fall." Why the trace is net depression was stated in [ADR-0066](0066-the-reward-path-measured-again.md) — "a readout unit's background spike within the pair window before the volley is depression, and the background's are the more" — and [ADR-0068](0068-the-reward-addressed.md) wrote its expectation from it and ADR-0069 read the expectation borne out, the answer couplings parting from the other two downward. Three decisions had reasoned from the sentence and nothing in the tree held a number for it: ADR-0069 reads the couplings and the readouts' spikes, and the eligibility trace was read as a whole in the two-unit tests of [ADR-0032](0032-three-factor-plasticity.md) and nowhere on the reference network.

Brief 032 asks for the measurement and then for one thing: from candidates fixed in a fixed order, by a measure that cannot see the answer, whether any admissible background makes that trace net potentiation, the background's one handle being the gain [ADR-0065](0065-the-instrument-recalibrated.md)'s calibration already turns. If a rung passes, ADR-0069's criterion runs under it with the expectation that the answer couplings rise rather than fall; if none does, the round records the ladder and the tree stops inferring the sentence and starts quoting a measurement of it. No rule changes: not a constant of the pair rule, not the delivery, the geometry, the window, the trial, the block or a seed.

Two facts of the tree shaped the reading. The rule of `cortex-core` (`SynapseBlock::step_stdp`; [ADR-0022](0022-synapse-fan-out-and-stdp.md), ADR-0032, [ADR-0049](0049-dale-principle-in-plasticity.md), [ADR-0055](0055-a-weight-that-settles.md)) runs at the presynaptic spike only, against the target's last somatic spike $q$: with $p$ the block's previous presynaptic stamp and $t$ the spike now, the trace gains $A_+ (1 - 2^{-11})^{q - p}$ when $p < q \le t$ and loses $A_- (1 - 2^{-11})^{t - q} \cdot M / 2^{13}$ when $q < t$, after a decay by $(1 - 2^{-16})^{t - p}$; so a trace is a sum of terms each of which one readout spike paired, and the record holds the sum and not the terms. And the executor's train ([ADR-0050](0050-the-train-inside-the-executor.md)) holds every spike of every unit in tick order on every worker count, so the terms can be computed again from it, outside the engine, and held to the record.

## Decision Drivers

- The brief's standing directives: no mechanism, no constant of the engine, no dependency; every claim Implemented, Specified, Target or Hypothesis, on a stated network at a stated size; nothing chosen after a rewarded run, the ladder and both marks written before the first rung ran; every number an arithmetic oracle can produce computed by that oracle before the test that asserts it.
- Latest ≠ Newest (§2.1): the first half is a measurement of the tree's own pair rule (Bi and Poo 1998; Izhikevich 2007; the nearest-neighbour form, Morrison, Diesmann and Gerstner 2008), the second a choice among values of a constant the instrument already has. Nothing is adopted.
- Principle 2: the sentence was an inference in three decisions; a fourth inferring it again would be worth less than a number either way.
- Principle 5: a reading that the record must agree with at every trial is a stronger structure than a reading of the record alone; the oracle is that agreement.
- [ADR-0061](0061-the-learning-runs-leave-the-gate.md) and [ADR-0067](0067-the-weekly-dispatch-has-a-scope.md): the runs are weekly `exhaustive` tests, the gate gains one test running no whole run, and a round that adds tests dispatches `scope=both`.

## Considered Options

1. **Read the record's trace per trial and split its terms through an oracle over the train, held to the record; walk the ladder under two calibrations written first; run the criterion only at a rung that passes both.**
2. Read the record's trace per trial and infer the split from the census alone, without the terms.
3. Read the terms by diffing the arena every tick: exact, but $2^{20}$ walks of eight thousand blocks per run.
4. Instrument the fan-out to count its terms: a change to the hot path for a measurement.
5. Run the criterion at a lower gain whatever the calibrations read, as a reading.

## Decision Outcome

At the present gain the run is `CALIBRATION_1024[0]`'s run bit for bit, block and trace, which the composition's test holds; the observer that reads the composition is a parameter of `run_behind`, called after every trial, that reads and never writes, and `run` passes one that does nothing, so every pinned run of ADR-0066, ADR-0069 and [ADR-0070](0070-where-256-units-settle.md) is unchanged.
- **The readings, per trial**, after the trial's last tick: (a) the record's summed `eligibility_q1_15` over the synapses from each stimulus set onto each readout set, a signed `i64` over 775, 806, 798 and 809 synapses (A→R0, A→R1, B→R0, B→R1; the census behind the couplings, pinned); (b) the terms that entered those traces since the previous reading, by stimulus, readout, class and sign; (c) the census around the readout window's opening (the trial's first tick plus the prior's `delay_min`, before which no local message of the volley can have landed, ADR-0065's rule): the spikes of readout 0, of readout 1 and of the presented stimulus set in the pair window of $2^{11}$ ticks before the opening and in the pair window after it.
- **The split, written before the run, a rule of `STDP_TAU_SHIFT` and the volley's arrival.** A term is **the volley's** when the readout unit's spike $q$ that paired it lies within one pair window ($2^{11}$ ticks, one STDP time constant) from the arrival of that synapse's volley message at the readout — the presented stimulus unit's volley spike, its first spike before the readout window opens, plus the synapse's own delay — and **the background's** otherwise; and each class holds its potentiation and its depression apart, so a trace's terms are four numbers: the volley's potentiation, the volley's depression, the background's potentiation and the background's depression. A term enters at a stimulus unit's spike and is attributed to the trial in which that spike fell, so the volley's terms of one presentation may enter a later trial's row: the response is credited at the stimulus unit's next spike. (`volleyed`, the class rule, held at its edges: a spike at the arrival is the volley's, one at the window's last tick is, one at the window's end is the background's, as is one before the arrival or with no volley on record; the latest volley is the one read.)
- **The oracle.** `Composer` collects the train after every trial (every spike since the previous reading, per unit; the ring holds a trial's most spikes, and the assertion that nothing it let go was younger than the previous reading holds at every trial), notes the presented set's volleys, and replays the rule for every synapse from a stimulus unit onto a readout unit over the stimulus unit's spikes since the previous reading: the block's decay since its stamp (`decayed`, at least one LSB toward zero), the potentiation for the target's last spike after the previous stamp, the depression for the target's last spike before this one at the frozen magnitude (`pair_window`, `depression_at`: `cortex-core`'s `window`, `depression_at` and `decayed` written a second time, in `i64` and saturating `i16`, held at their edges to the amounts the rule documents — the amplitude at no distance, 120 and 126 of 328 and 344 at one time constant, 344 at the reference magnitude and 1 376 at the rail, 1 at a magnitude of twelve and nothing at eleven, 985 from 1 000 over a thousand ticks, computed apart from the tree first), then the stamp. **At every trial of every run the replayed trace of every synapse equals the record's `eligibility_q1_15`**, 3 188 synapses over 64 trials at four gains, so the terms are the numbers the engine computed and the split is of nothing else; a same-tick coincidence of a stimulus and a readout spike pairs as the rule pairs it (a potentiation and no depression), which the agreement holds.

### Consequences

- Good: the sentence three decisions rested on is a measurement with the numbers behind it, pinned per trial and per block, and its mechanism is stated in terms the tree can act on: the count of a stimulus unit's spikes within the pair window, which a constant of the instrument sets.
- Good: the oracle holds the reading to the record at every trial, so a later change to the pair rule, the train or the fan-out that moves a term is caught in the gate by eight trials.
- Good: the ladder was walked as written, in its order, under two measures that see no outcome, and it picked nothing; nothing was chosen after a run and no rewarded run was made.
- Neutral: the gate's runtime tests gain one run of $2^{17}$ ticks at 1 024 units; the weekly job gains two tests of about a minute together in the release profile on a developer machine.
- Bad: the answer to H-12 does not move: no rewarded run, no reading of the couplings' direction under a positive trace, and the expectation written for one stays untested.
- Bad: the instrument's stimulus does not do what the documents said of it since ADR-0065; the sentence "fires each once" was true of the window it was read in, and F-46 records the rest.
- Bad: the weekly exhaustive job read 104 minutes of its 120 on this run's runner, with two tests of about a minute added and the unchanged binaries 1.35 to 1.67 times slower than in the run before; the job's shape, which ADR-0071 left to the round that has the numbers, is now the first thing a measuring round must decide, and this round did not, the workflow being outside its brief.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Option 2**: above; the brief's first deliverable asks for the terms, not an inference about them.
- **Option 3**: exact and a million times the cost, for a reading the train gives exactly.
- **Option 4**: the brief's standing directives; and a counter in the fan-out would read the engine's terms without the check that the oracle gives.
- **Option 5**: the brief's rule; the lesson of five rounds.
- **A criterion clause on the trace's sign**: H-12 is about a behaviour; the sign is a calibration and a reading.
