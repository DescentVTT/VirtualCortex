# ADR-0038: The episodic ledger and its replay — an append-only second record of `cortex-hippocampus` under ADR-0016's test, tagged as an input, replayed into the network on a ripple cadence in slow-wave sleep and consolidated by the modulator's rule, depotentiated in REM; the canvas hydration Specified; sections 44 and 45

## Context and Problem Statement

`cortex-hippocampus` held one record with six fields and no rule: `HippocampalAttractorState`, with `swr_replay_ticks` "a sharp-wave ripple replay cycle countdown" that nothing counted down; whitepaper §8.8 carried "Complementary learning systems | fast one-shot CA3 attractor; replay during slow-wave sleep into slow neocortical weights | Specified" and R-6 "the hippocampus replays tagged episodes at compressed speed and drives slow neocortical plasticity". Since ADR-0035 a countdown is a cadence, a mask on the tick, so the field was a rule's placeholder the tree already had another form for (finding F-29, with ADR-0037's gate). The proposal of brief 019 asked for an append-only, index-addressed episodic event ledger hosted in the record or an arena, a zero-copy path from an episode into `cortex-imagination`'s canvas for counterfactual variation, the ledger immutable; slow-wave sleep as ripple replay transferring compressed representations to neocortical weights; and REM as recombination and affective depotentiation. What is an episode in an engine whose state is units, blocks and spikes; what does replay do to the network; what does REM do; and where does the ledger live?

Three facts shaped the answer. The engine has one plasticity rule, the three-factor pair rule of ADR-0022 and ADR-0032, which potentiates a synapse whose target fires at or after the source's spike and consolidates under the modulator: a pattern of units fired *together* pairs every synapse among them as potentiation, so "transfer to neocortical weights" needs no second rule, only the reactivation. The executor's phase 3 already delivers into mailboxes from outside the wheel (the injector), on worker 0, in a fixed order, so a replay can be delivered the same way and stay bit-identical on any worker count. And a tagged episode changes what a run does, so it is an input between ticks, like a reward, recorded in the image.

## Decision Drivers

- ADR-0016's six-part test for a second record in a crate: a gap (no record holds a pattern of units to reactivate), no other owner (the spike trace is a test report, `PlasticDelta` is a weight change, `SemanticOntologyNode` is what survives consolidation), the mechanism in §8.8 with the layout, formats that fit their widths (a unit index is 32 bits, a tag and a count 8), the boundaries of §1.5 and §8.10 untouched.
- §8.3: a run is `(image, seed, trace)`; a tag is an input in the trace and the ledger is in the image; a replay's delivery is worker 0's in a fixed order.
- TC-5 and ADR-0023: no allocation and no new phase; the ledger is an arena sized once; a ripple's work is bounded (`RIPPLE_SCAN`).
- ADR-0018's membrane: the soma settles near half the basal potential, a basal input decays with a time constant of 512 ticks, and the refractory window is 200 ticks. Whether one replay is one spike of each unit is a property of the drive and the interval, measured before the constants were chosen.
- "Latest ≠ Newest": the two-stage model of consolidation (Buzsáki 1989) and active system consolidation (Diekelmann and Born 2010), replay during sharp-wave ripples as the mechanism, and REM's depotentiation of affective salience (Walker and van der Helm 2009) are decades old with documented failure modes.
- ADR-0010 and principle 3: what replay does to a four-unit ring in a test is stated; what it does at the reference scale is a hypothesis with a protocol (H-9).

## Considered Options

1. An episode as a wheel event: the replay countdown as written, one timer per episode.
2. **An `Episode` record (a tagged pattern of up to twelve units, the tick, a tag, a replay count) appended to an arena the executor holds and the image carries as section 45, indexed by a hand in `HippocampalAttractorState` (section 44); tagged as an input between ticks; on a ripple cadence in slow-wave sleep the hand walks the ledger round robin to the first episode that is not spent, and worker 0 delivers its units a drive that fires them together in phase 3, so that phase 2 of the next tick pairs and consolidates every synapse among them; in REM the same walk lowers the episode's tag, which is the number of REM ripples it survives; the canvas hydration Specified.**
3. An episode as a set of weight deltas (`PlasticDelta`) applied at replay.
4. A priority queue over the ledger, the most salient episode replayed first.

## Decision Outcome

Option 2.

- **`Episode`** (`cortex-hippocampus`, 64 bytes, format version 13): `tagged_tick: u32` `[0..4)`, `tag: u8` `[4)` (the REM ripples the episode survives; 0 is spent), `replays: u8` `[5)` (saturating), `len: u8` `[6)`, `_pad: u8` `[7)`, `pattern: [u32; 12]` `[8..56)` (unit indices, the first `len`; the rest MUST be zero), `_reserved: [u8; 8]` `[56..64)`; `PATTERN_MAX` 12. `tag(tick, units, priority)` is refused for no unit, more than twelve, a unit twice or a priority of zero. `pattern()`, `is_spent()`, `replay()` (`None` when spent; counts), `depotentiate()` (the tag down by one, saturating), `is_well_formed` (one to twelve units, the unused slots zero, no unit twice, the pad and the reserved bytes zero; the tag may be zero), `encode`, `decode`; `Default` is the empty slot beyond the ledger's length, which is not well formed. The pattern and the tick are never written after `tag`; the tag and the count are the ledger's annotations. That is the immutability the proposal asked for: the ground truth of an episode is what fired and when, and it is written once.
- **`HippocampalAttractorState`** (format version 13): `[12..16)` is `episodes: u32`, the ledger's length (`swr_replay_ticks` removed, F-29); `[24..28)` `replay_hand: u32`, the episode the next ripple considers; `[28..64)` reserved. The six Specified fields stay. `new()` (= `Default`), `append() -> Option<u32>` (the index; refused at the width), `next_hand() -> Option<u32>` (the hand, then the hand moved to the next modulo the length; `None` for an empty ledger), `is_well_formed` (the hand below the length or both zero; the reserved bytes zero), `encode`, `decode`.
- **The ripple** (`RIPPLE_SHIFT` 11: every $2^{11}$ ticks, 20.48 ms). The brief named $2^9$ (512 ticks, 195 Hz, "inside the 150 to 250 Hz band of sharp-wave ripples"); that band is the ripple's oscillation, not the rate of replay events, and at 512 ticks successive drives sum in the basal compartment (time constant 512 ticks), so every replay after the first fired the pattern twice (once at the drive, once as the refractory window ended). At 2 048 ticks, four basal time constants, two per cent of a drive remains and one replay is one spike of each unit; the runtime asserts at compile time that the ripple exceeds the refractory window and four basal time constants. The rate of replay events in sleep (about one per second in the literature) is a Target to tune; the interval here is about the length of one ripple.
- **The drive** (`REPLAY_DRIVE_Q16` 1.25, `REPLAY_MESSAGES` 2: two messages of 1.25 into the basal compartment, 2.5 in all, asserted at compile time against the threshold's base). Probed on a unit at its base threshold with drives delivered 2 048 and 4 096 ticks apart, eight times (a test run, not a Measured figure): at 2.0 nothing fires (the soma settles near half the basal potential); from 2.25 to 2.875, in eighths, every drive is exactly one spike, at 19 down to 9 ticks; at 3.0 about every second drive at 2 048 ticks is two spikes, the second 201 ticks after the first. 2.5 is the middle of the band, twelve ticks on. The exit test pins one spike per unit per ripple and a latency between 5 and 20 ticks; the band's edges are not pinned by a committed test.
- **The composition** (`cortex-runtime`, which now depends on `cortex-hippocampus`). `Config::episodes` (room for tagged episodes beyond what an image holds; 0 by default). The executor holds one `HippocampalAttractorState` and an arena of `Episode`s in `Shared`, sized once. Inputs between ticks: `tag_episode(units, priority) -> Result<u32, TagError>` (the index; `LedgerFull`, `NoSuchUnit`, `InvalidPattern`) and ADR-0037's `wake`. Before every tick's first barrier the coordinator decides the ripple: on the cadence, in slow-wave sleep the hand walks up to `RIPPLE_SCAN` (16) episodes to the first that is not spent, counts its replay and publishes its index + 1 in `Shared::replay`; in REM the same walk depotentiates that episode and publishes nothing; awake, off the cadence or with nothing found, nothing. In phase 3, after the injector, worker 0 delivers the drive to every unit of the published episode in the pattern's order: the pattern integrates the drive at the next tick, fires together about twelve ticks on, and phase 2 of that tick pairs every synapse among its units with $q = t$ (potentiation, no depression) and consolidates under the tick's modulation. `hippocampus()`, `episodes()`, `episode_room()`, `replays()` and `depotentiations()` read between ticks. The tally counts replay spikes like any others; a small replaying network reads as saturated to ADR-0036's ceiling, which at $\kappa = 0$ moves nothing.
- **Persistence** (format version 13). Section 44, `SECTION_HIPPOCAMPUS`, one record, always written and required; section 45, `SECTION_EPISODE`, the ledger in order, written when it is not empty and required when the record says it is not (`MissingSection`). The loader sizes the arena as the image's count plus `Config::episodes`, refuses an episode that is not well formed or names a unit outside the arena (`MalformedEpisode(index)`), a record that is not well formed or whose length is not the section's count (`MalformedHippocampus`), and a count other than one (`Directory`).
- **What the exit test holds** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/sleep.rs`). In slow-wave sleep with the regulation off, a tagged ring of four units (one synapse each, weight 8 000, one tick of delay) fires together at every ripple, twelve ticks on, exactly once per unit per ripple, eight ripples in 16 384 ticks; its four synapses end at the weight an oracle block fed the same spike ticks gives, 120 per ripple after the first (the pair rule's $A_+ (1 - 2^{-11})^{2048}$ as the crate's binary exponentiation rounds it), with nothing left in the traces; an untagged ring's synapses do not move and its units never fire; the episode's count and `replays()` equal the ripples. In REM the tags fall by one per ripple in the hand's order, a spent episode is skipped, nothing is delivered and nothing fires; a wake between ticks returns the engine to awake with its pressure kept, and a slow-wave engine whose ledger is spent replays nothing. Two episodes in turn over four slow-wave windows and one REM window are bit-identical on one and four workers (256 replays, 768 spikes, 45 depotentiations). The ledger and the stage round-trip through the image mid-sleep and a loaded engine continues alike under a configuration that says no sleep and no room. Tagging is refused for a full ledger, a missing unit and a bad pattern.
- **What is not claimed.** That replaying a pattern transfers a memory. What the tree holds is the arithmetic: the schedule, the pattern firing together, and the weights among it moving by the pair rule's amount. Whether reactivation of tagged patterns at the reference scale consolidates anything a later behaviour reads is hypothesis H-9, with the protocol: a reference image, episodes tagged from its own activity (a pattern the trace shows firing within a ripple's length), the weights among each pattern before and after a night, and a behaviour that reads them. The pattern's width, the ripple's interval, the scan's bound and the drive are M5's tuning, stated as Targets.
- **Specified: the canvas hydration.** A `MentalCanvasFrame` with `simulation_id` the episode's index in the low word and its tagged tick in the high word, `hypothetical_action_hash` the FNV-1a hash of the pattern, every other field at rest, then `wander_at(temperature)`: the counterfactual variation of an episode, reading the ledger and never writing it. Not built: nothing reads a frame's outcome yet, and a frame no rule reads is a field with no reader; the round that composes affect or the executive takes it.
- **Not adopted.** *Option 1*: a timer per episode in the wheel for what a cadence and a hand express. *Option 3*: a replay that applies deltas is a second plasticity rule beside the pair rule, and the transfer the literature describes is reactivation, not a write. *Option 4*: a priority order over a ledger Appendix A sizes at a million records is a heap or a scan; the tag gives salience its effect as the number of REM ripples an episode survives, and the hand's round robin costs a ripple at most `RIPPLE_SCAN` reads. *A replay through the injector ring*: the ring's capacity is a configuration, and a replay dropped for a full ring would make a run depend on it. *Affect in REM*: ADR-0037.

### Consequences

- Good: the whitepaper's complementary-learning row has its replay Implemented on the existing plasticity rule, with the ledger in the image and the tag an input, and the pin holds at the default.
- Good: a ripple costs at most sixteen reads and at most twenty-four deliveries on worker 0; no phase, barrier or allocation was added.
- Good: the six-part test admitted a second record without a crate, and the countdown with no rule (F-29) is gone.
- Bad: format version 13 makes version-12 images unreadable.
- Bad: the ledger has no eviction: a full ledger refuses a tag, and a spent episode keeps its slot; reclaiming slots is the same indirection the unit arena lacks (ADR-0024, Specified).
- Bad: the drive's band was probed on a unit at its base threshold; a unit whose threshold or compartments a caller set differently may fire twice or not at all (a residual basal potential of 1.0 fires it twice at the first ripple; a threshold of 2.0 not at all for four ripples), and the exit test's ring is the case that is held.
- Bad: the drive is an input of the unit and is scaled by the synaptic gain of ADR-0036 like every input, so the once-per-ripple property holds for a gain between about 0.875 and 1.175. On a network small enough that its own replay reads as saturated to the controller's ceiling, a control step above zero halves the gain, the drive stops firing, the window reads as silent, the gain rises, and replay is intermittent (three windows of six on a four-unit ring at $\kappa = 0.5$). The exit tests run at $\kappa = 0$; at the reference scale a ripple's spikes are far below the ceiling, which is H-8's regime and H-9's.
- Bad: a ripple takes `REPLAY_MESSAGES × PATTERN_MAX` mailbox nodes on worker 0 in one tick; `Executor::new` refuses a pool below that while the ledger has room (`ConfigError::TooFewNodes`), and a pool the rule of `Config::nodes_per_worker` sizes for the injector and the wheel alone is not enough for a ripple.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **The ledger inside `HippocampalAttractorState`'s forty reserved bytes**: ten unit indices and no length, tick, tag or count; one episode, not a ledger.
- **A hash of the pattern stored in the episode**: a field no rule reads; the hydration computes it.
- **Reading the ledger from every worker** (a replay by whichever worker holds the unit): phase 1 references are the turn holder's and a replay is an input, which belongs in phase 3 with the injector.
- **The drive at 3.0** (the kick the tests use): the edge of the band; at the ripple's interval it fires twice.

## Confirmation

The mutation gate on the changed lines (ADR-0030) passes in CI.
