# ADR-0081: The reinforced form, measured — H-14 is yes: on the network ADR-0077 settled, with the baseline at zero, the task as ADR-0059 and ADR-0068 built it — the reward addressed to the synapses from the presented stimulus onto the readout the engine selected, its sign the outcome's — moves the selection to the answer in 128 of the last 128 trials in the assignment and in the mirrored assignment over 1 536 trials; the selection passes 40 of 64 per block by trial 448 and 384, the mirrored first, as predicted and earlier than estimated; the answer couplings rise to 1.35 to 1.45 of the image's and are still rising, every synapse outside them — the wrong pairs among them — bit for bit the image's, ADR-0080's derivation true on every arm; the shuffled reward locks one stimulus onto one readout and leaves the other leaning; the calibration reproduced ADR-0077 first; H-14's stopping rule at step 3, the note written beside H-12 and the next decision named and not taken

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0080](0080-the-reinforced-form.md) took the decision H-13's stopping rule named after [ADR-0079](0079-the-rewards-direction-measured.md)'s yes and wrote **H-14** in whitepaper §11.1 with everything fixed before any run: the network (ADR-0077's settled image, the gain 1.75, the controller off, the modulation baseline zero), the constants (ADR-0076's stimulus, the instrument's geometry, `WINDOW`, `TRIAL_TICKS`, `SEED`, `REWARD_Q16`, `BLOCK`, `LAST_BLOCKS`, `REWARDED_MIN`, and **1 536 trials**, derived from H-13's blocks), the delivery (the task as [ADR-0059](0059-a-task-a-readout-and-a-reward.md) and [ADR-0068](0068-the-reward-addressed.md) built it: `Delivery::Addressed`, the synapses from the presented stimulus onto the readout the engine selected addressed after every trial, none at a tie, the reward $+1.0$ for the answer and $-1.0$ otherwise), the arms (the assignment, the mirrored assignment, the shuffled reward as a reading), the criterion (at least 80 correct selections of the last 128 in both rewarded arms, a tie not correct), an assertion derived from the rules (in a rewarded arm a wrong selection's pair never consolidates, so every synapse outside the two answer pairs ends the run bit for bit the image's), a prediction (yes, the selection passing 40 of 64 per block by about trial 900, the mirrored first), the relation to H-12 and its own stopping rule. Brief 037 is the one round the rule allows. This ADR records it.

The question is the one H-12 asked and could not answer on the prior with the baseline at 0.5 — whether a reward the engine **earns by its own selection** moves that selection to the answer — asked on the one configuration where the rule's direction has been read: H-13 showed that a reward *handed* to a pair on the settled network raises its coupling and the response the engine reads; the reinforced form starts at the frozen network's level, where about half the trials are correct, and only a correct trial's pair is rewarded. Two things ADR-0080 asked the round to hold rather than read: the derivation that a wrong pair cannot consolidate (the signal at a trial's end is at most 0.712, the punishment of 1.0 leaves it negative, `decay_dopamine` never crosses zero, and `Modulations::for_synapse` clamps the addressed modulation at zero), and the shuffled arm read for lock-in rather than bounded by a clause.

## Decision Drivers

- **Nothing chosen after a rewarded run.** H-14's constants, arms, length, criterion, assertion and stopping rule are ADR-0080's; the round's integer form of the criterion, of the assertion and of the lock-in reading is committed before the first rewarded run, and no clause is dropped or added because of what a run read.
- **The calibration before anything else**, and a mismatch a stop (H-14's stopping rule, step 2).
- **The derivation asserted beside the verdict and not in place of it**: a failure of ADR-0080's derivation is a finding about the derivation, not an outcome of H-14, so the run dumps every arm and computes the verdict before the assertion is made.
- **The engine read before a description of it is trusted**: `Task::trial` (the addressed set written whatever the feedback, the sign of the reward, the signal read after the reward), `Executor::address`, `NeuromodulatorState::reward` and `decay_dopamine`, and ADR-0079's oracle (`consolidated` clamps a negative modulation to zero, `decayed_signal` moves a negative signal toward zero by its own rule) were read for the harness below.
- Latest ≠ Newest: no mechanism, no constant of the engine, no record, no dependency. The delivery is the task's own; the image is ADR-0077's; the oracle is ADR-0079's with one field.
- [ADR-0075](0075-the-dispatch-scope-follows-the-diff.md): the dispatch's scope follows the diff, and the clause that applied is stated here.
- [ADR-0073](0073-the-whole-domain-tests-sharded.md)'s shard budget: `instrument.rs` took 3 030 s of its 7 200 in ADR-0079's dispatch, and the brief's empowerment sends the runs to a binary of their own only if the shard would otherwise pass 60 per cent of its bound.

## Considered Options

1. **The task as built, composed in the harness** (test code): `Task::trial` under `Delivery::Addressed` with the arm's feedback, the task itself addressing the selection's pair and delivering the signed reward; or a variant in `task.rs`.
2. **The three arms as one `exhaustive` test from one image**, the calibration a frozen block from a decode of the image; or each arm its own test with its own lead-in.
3. **The runs in `instrument.rs`** beside ADR-0079's harness, or in a test binary of their own.
4. **The oracle set from the outcome** — the pair the task addressed and the signal its reward left — with one field added for the reward the task delivers before the reading; or a second replay path for the reinforced form.
5. **A withheld arm** beside the three, as in H-13.
6. **The gate's delivery over eight trials on the instrument's network** at 1 024 units, as ADR-0079's gate ran it; or on the settled network behind its lead-in of ninety-six windows.
7. **The lock-in reading's integer form**: per stimulus, the larger of the shuffled arm's two readout counts over the last 128 trials at least the smaller of the two rewarded arms' correct counts for that stimulus.

### Consequences

- Good: for the first time on the learning line a reward the engine earns by its own selection moved that selection to the answer — in every trial of the last 128, in both assignments — under a criterion, a prediction, an assertion and a stopping rule written before the round existed; H-12's behavioural question is answered yes on a configuration its rounds never had.
- Good: the attribution is exact by construction and by reading — the only weights that moved are the answer pairs' in a rewarded arm, the excitatory sum rose by exactly their rise, the wrong pairs are the image's to the bit, and the oracle agreed with the record at every one of 4 608 trials — so what rose is the reward's and nothing else's, and ADR-0080's derivation is now a reading as well as an argument.
- Good: the lock-in question is answered by both halves ADR-0080 named: a wrong pair cannot consolidate in a rewarded arm (asserted), and a reward without information does lock a stimulus onto a readout (read, on A), which is why the mirrored pair and not a bounded control is the information control.
- Good: the next decision is named by the rule and not chosen here, and the readings it needs are in the tree: the rise is not settling at 1 536 trials, the ties vanish once the couplings part, the readout still sees at 1.45 of the image's coupling.
- Neutral: the gate's runtime tests gain one test of $2^{17}$ ticks at 1 024 units; the weekly job gains one run of about 2.2 of ADR-0079's, and `instrument.rs` reads 56 per cent of its shard's bound, four points under the threshold at which the next measuring round's runs go to a binary of their own.
- Bad: a yes on H-14 is a claim at 1 024 units on one settled network with plasticity confined to the addressed synapses under a symmetric reward of one magnitude; plasticity everywhere, reversal, size and regime — everything the line would need to say "the engine learns" beyond this task — stay after it, and the next decision chooses among them.
- Bad: where the rise stops is not read, and the shuffled arm's lock-in on A says the same positive feedback that carries the answer would carry an error if the reward's sign ever stopped wiping the wrong pair.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **A variant in `task.rs`** (option 1): the task already does what ADR-0080 fixed, and a change under `src/` would send the round through the whole-tree sweep for a change no test exercises differently.
- **Each arm its own test** (option 2): three lead-ins for one image, and the pairing would rest on three decodes asserted equal rather than on one.
- **A binary of its own** (option 3): the threshold the empowerment set was not reached by the estimate; the harness stays in one file and the shard's reading is in the evidence.
- **A second replay path** (option 4): the oracle's rules already clamp a negative modulation and decay a negative signal as the engine does; one field for the reward the task delivers before the reading is the whole difference.
- **A withheld arm** (option 5): under the baseline at zero it is the frozen run, which H-13's withheld arm read over 512 trials of the same image and seed and the calibration reads over the first block; its cost is a third of the test.
- **The gate on the settled network** (option 6): its lead-in is the weekly test's cost, and the rules are the same on any network with the baseline at zero.
- **A lock-in rule loosened after the run**, so that A at 60 of 61 reads as locked: a clause moved after a rewarded run; the rule stays and the reading is stated beside it.
