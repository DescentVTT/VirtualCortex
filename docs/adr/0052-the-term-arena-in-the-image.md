# ADR-0052: The term arena in the image and the discovery loop inside the tick — the clause store, the term nodes, the arena's counters, the affect state and the binding of an episode to an invented predicate are the executor's and the image's (format 14); a committed invention's outputs are instantiated through the bindings so the store reads without the table; the search runs on a cadence of the tick while awake from a cursor it keeps

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0050](0050-the-train-inside-the-executor.md) ran the discovery loop of [ADR-0045](0045-clause-search.md) in one call between ticks on the executor's own train and left three things outside the image: "the clause store, the affect state and the association in the image: three sections and format 14 with the loader's checks, taken by the round that gives the image a term arena; after it a cadence inside `tick()` can run the search on a rewarded moment without a caller". Until then the engine learned in three places a restart lost: the store was a caller's `TermNode` arena ([ADR-0043](0043-discovery-path.md)), the affect state a caller's record, the association between an invented predicate and its episode a caller's table, and no search ran without a caller.

Two facts of the tree shaped the design. First, a committed invention's outputs read through the binding table: `intra_construct`'s outputs "are instances of their inputs, and the bindings the matching made stay in the caller's table" ([ADR-0041](0041-induction-on-the-term-arena.md)), so `tests/reference.rs` built a fresh table per search and dropped it after, and a second search over the same store read the clauses without the bindings the first left. A store in the image therefore needs either the table beside it or outputs that stand without it. Second, `search` walked the pairs from the first one on every call, so a bounded budget on a cadence would repeat its first pairs forever and a large store would never be walked past them.

## Decision Drivers

- §8.3: a run is a function of the image, the seed and the trace; what the loop reads and writes changes what a run does, so it is in the image or in the trace, never in a caller's memory alone.
- ADR-0016's one-owner rule: the arena's record and rules are `cortex-reasoning`'s, the affect record `cortex-affect`'s, what an episode's pattern stands for is the episode's, the composition the executor's; a quantity that belongs to an existing subsystem is a field in that record, not a crate or a table.
- TC-5: nothing allocates in the loop; the arena, the store, the scratch and the records are sized once in `Executor::new`.
- Principle 5: the loader refuses what the writer never produces, so a loaded arena cannot hold a forward reference, a cycle, a store index that is not a clause or a record that does not describe what was loaded.

## Considered Options

1. The binding table in the image beside the store, a fourth section.
2. **The three outputs of a commit instantiated through the bindings at the commit and the table unbound, so that the store's clauses stand without it and the image holds nodes alone; the arena, the store's indices, a third record of `cortex-reasoning` for the arena's counters and the search's cursor, cadence, budget and tag, and the affect record as sections; the episode carrying the symbol its pattern stands for; the loop as an executor method between ticks and on a cadence inside the tick.**
3. A section of association records, one per rewarded commit, beside the episodes.
4. The search restarted from the first pair on every cadence, with no cursor.

## Decision Outcome

Option 2; image format 14 (with [ADR-0053](0053-the-waking-day-and-the-target-period.md) and [ADR-0054](0054-the-causal-count-inside-the-loop.md), one bump for the round); the determinism pin moves for ADR-0054's reason, not this one.

- **The instantiation** (`cortex-reasoning`, `induce.rs`). `instantiate(term, s)` is a copy of a term through the bindings: every bound variable is replaced by the instance of what it is bound to, a constant, an unbound variable and a compound with no bound variable beneath are returned as they are (the same index), a copied compound's children are allocated before it, the depth is bounded by `INSTANTIATE_DEPTH` (32 levels; a clause is three deep) and every walk by `WALK_LIMIT`; every failure restores the scratch. The instance's `size` and `term_hash` with an empty table equal the original's through the bindings, which the test holds. `InduceScratch::unbind(&mark)` undoes the bindings made since a mark and cuts the trail, keeping the nodes. The runtime's `search_from` instantiates a commit's three outputs, unbinds to before the attempt, and writes the instances into the store and into the discovery it reports; the exit search of `tests/discovery.rs` commits the same two inventions to the same lengths (106 nodes become 100), every goal provable before is provable after through fresh bindings, and the table is empty after every search.
- **The cursor** (`search_from(store, len, scratch, affect, budget, after, out)`). The walk starts after `after` (the start when `None`) and reports the pair after which the next search resumes: the last pair attempted when the budget ended the walk with a pair in hand; the start when no pair was left or the last action was a commit (the store changed, so the pairs did). `search` is `search_from` from the start with the report alone. With a budget of one over three clauses whose every pair is rejected, four searches attempt (0, 1), (0, 2), (1, 2) and then (0, 1) again, the third leaving the cursor at the start because no pair follows the last; over the exit store a budget of one commits one invention per search and restarts.
- **The records.** `TermNode::{encode, decode, is_well_formed}`: a kind the constants name, an arity of zero for a constant, a variable or an empty node and at most eight for a compound, every slot below the arity holding a child and every slot at or beyond it empty, the pad and the reserved bytes zero. `InteroceptiveState::{encode, decode, is_well_formed}`: the comfort and the mood within $[-1, 1]$, the reserved bytes zero. `InductionState`, the third record of `cortex-reasoning` (64 bytes; admitted under ADR-0016's test: the gap is what an engine carries its arena and store across a restart with, no record owns the arena's cursor or the search's, and the crate that owns the operators owns their counters): `free`, `next_variable`, `next_invented`, the store's `clauses`, the cursor as the pair after which the next search resumes (each index + 1, zero the start), `search_budget`, `search_shift` (0 never searches inside the tick; at most `SEARCH_SHIFT_MAX`, 63, the cadence's width) and `tag` (the REM ripples an invention's episode survives); well formed with the next invention inside the invented band, the cursor at the start or a pair $i < j$ below the store's length, the pad and the reserved bytes zero. `Episode::symbol` at `[56..60)` (the id of what the pattern stands for, zero for none) with `Episode::bind(symbol)`, refused for zero and for an episode bound already: what a pattern stands for is written once, as the pattern is; the reserved bytes are `[60..64)`.
- **The executor** (`store.rs`, `executor.rs`). `Config::{terms, clauses, search_shift, search_budget, discovery_tag}`; the arena of `terms` nodes, the store of `clauses` slots, a binding table and a trail of the arena's size, a work stack of twice it, a pair table of it, the affect record, the induction record and a discovery buffer of the store's size (a commit needs a store slot, so the buffer never fills first), all allocated once; a store without an arena and a shift at the cadence's width are refused. The inputs between ticks: `term(node)` appends a node (refused for an engine without an arena, a full arena, a node not well formed or empty, a child at or beyond the cursor, a variable outside the table; a variable moves `next_variable` above its number) and `assert_clause(head, body)` appends the clause and its index into the store with the affect state primed to the new length (refused for a full store before anything is written); `bind_episode(index, symbol)`. The loop, `discover()` between ticks and the same body on `Cadence::new(search_shift, 0)` inside `tick()` after the tally while the stage is awake: one search from the cursor with the budget, the committed rewards' total into the modulator when positive, then the densest basal time constant (`COINCIDENCE_TICKS`, 512) of the ripple before now (`DISCOVERY_WINDOW`, 2 048) tagged from the executor's own train with the record's tag and bound to the first commit's predicate; a search of two commits binds the one episode to the first and reports both (`discoveries()`). The search's refusals as it gives them, its commits before one standing; the ledger's or the train's refusal of the moment as `Tag`, the reward the modulator's by then; inside the tick the refusals are counted (`untagged`, `search_failures`) and never returned. The affect state is primed to the store's length at every change and read from there, never kept twice. The compositions of ADR-0050 over a caller's store (`discover`, `ClauseSearch`, `Tagging`) are gone; the three functions over a caller's train and the three `recent` forms stay, `tag_discovery` and `tag_discovery_recent` binding the episode they tag.
- **The image** (`image.rs`). The term section (kind 40) holds the arena's first `free` nodes when there are any, the clause section (kind 49) the store's indices as 4-byte records when there are any, the affect section (kind 47) and the induction section (kind 48) one record each, always. The loader refuses a node that is not well formed, is empty, names a child at or beyond its own index (so no loaded arena is cyclic: F-32's class cannot be loaded) or is a variable outside the table (`MalformedTerm`); a store index at or beyond the cursor, naming a node that is not a clause or named twice (`MalformedClause`); an induction record not well formed, whose cursor and length are not the loaded counts or whose next variable is at or below a loaded variable's number (`MalformedInduction`); an affect record not well formed or not primed to the loaded store's description length (`MalformedAffect`); an episode bound to a symbol outside the invented band (`MalformedEpisode`). The image's cadence, budget and tag outrank the configuration's; the configuration's `terms` and `clauses` are room beyond what the image holds. The image carries no train (the ring is the executor's, ADR-0050), so a loaded engine tags from the spikes after the load.
- **What the exit tests hold.** `tests/store.rs`: the inputs' refusals; the loop between ticks over the exit store of ADR-0045 (two attempts, two commits, 106 to 100 nodes, a reward of 1.5, the episode bound to `INVENTED_BASE`, the second commit reported and bound to nothing; a second search attempting nothing; a rewarded search over a full ledger or with a tag of zero counted as untagged with the reward standing; a store with no slot for a commit ending in `StoreFull` with nothing rewarded and the failure counted); the cursor under a budget of one; the cadence (a search at every 128th tick while awake, none asleep, none without a cadence); an image without an arena writing seven sections, a searched engine's ten round-tripping byte for byte, a loaded engine searching what the un-loaded one searches to the same store and episode; every loader clause on the four sections. `tests/no_alloc.rs`: the loop on a cadence of 512 ticks over a store of three rules allocates nothing in 20 000 ticks. `tests/reference.rs`: `capture_night` at 256 and 1 024 units on the engine's own store, every number of the night as [ADR-0049](0049-dale-principle-in-plasticity.md) pinned it (437 spikes to the reward's tick, the same pattern, burst, readouts, replays and tags), the store 26 clauses after the search, the episode bound to `INVENTED_BASE` and the network's own pattern to nothing; the determinism test with the exit store asserted and the loop on a cadence of one bin, bit-identical arena, store, records, ledger and train on one and four workers, two searches and two inventions in two bins.
- **Not adopted.** *Option 1*: a scratch persisted is a second store; the table would grow with every search and every proof over the store would read through bindings it did not make. *Option 3*: the episode owns what its pattern stands for, and a section of pairs would be a second owner of that fact; one moment is one episode, and a search of two commits binds it to the first and reports the second. *Option 4*: a budget without a cursor repeats its first pairs; the cursor is eight bytes of the record.

### Consequences

- Good: an engine that asserts clauses and runs discovers on its own, rewards itself and binds what it invented to the pattern that was active; everything it learned is in the image, and a restart continues from it.
- Good: the store's clauses are self-contained nodes; the binding table is empty between searches and a proof over the store makes its own bindings.
- Good: the loader's checks make a loaded arena acyclic by construction, which no walk had to guarantee before.
- Bad: instantiation copies a compound that holds a bound variable, so a commit leaves its inputs and the copied intermediates as garbage in the arena; a compaction is Specified, and the arena's capacity is the bound until then.
- Bad: a search on the cadence over an unchanged store after a complete pass walks its pairs again, at most the budget's attempts per cadence; a store that changed since the last pass is what the cursor is for.
- Bad: the tag's window and coincidence are the rule's constants (one ripple, one basal time constant); a moment read at another width is a caller's `tag_burst_in`.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **`instantiate` always copying**: doubles the arena's use per commit for outputs that hold no bound variable; the copy-on-need form returns the same index and the test holds that nothing is allocated for an unbound term.
- **A trigger that is not a cadence** (a search when a clause is asserted, or when the affect state moves): a rule inside the tick that runs on an input's arrival is a schedule that is not a function of the tick; the cadence is, and runs at the same ticks on every worker count. Specified for a round with an objective that says when to search.
- **The search's parameters in `Config` only**: the image outranks the configuration for the baseline, the step and the shift (§8.3); the record holds them for the same reason.
