# ADR-0094: The signed gate built — ADR-0093's parameter placed as a flag byte at `[25]` of the modulator section and the image format moved from 15 to 16; `cortex-neuromod`'s `signed_modulation` beside `modulation`, and `cortex-core`'s `consolidate_signed` beside `consolidate`, which is untouched; the coordinator's addressed word carried below zero only while the gate is set and floored at zero for an inhibitory slot, so that no word, no allocation and no syscall is added to the tick; unset, every run is the run it was, bit for bit

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0093](0093-the-punished-pair-consolidates-against-its-trace.md) decided the rule and left its placement to this round: *"when it is set, an addressed excitatory synapse consolidates under `m = clamp(baseline + dopamine, −1, 1)`, and: for `m ≥ 0` the rule is today's, bit for bit …; for `m < 0` the weight moves against the trace's sign by `round(|trace| × |m|)`, clamped as today to `[0, 2^15)` of the magnitude, and the trace is spent toward zero by what the magnitude moved"*, every other synapse as today, unset every synapse as today, the parameter in the image with the format moved from 15 to 16. Brief 041's empowerment names the choices: where the gate lives in the configuration and the image, how "set" is encoded, whether the configuration takes a `bool` or an enum, whether `consolidate` takes the signed domain or a rule beside it does, and how the fan-out selects the signed modulation — so long as unset is today's rule bit for bit, the loader refuses what the writer never produces, the hot path gains no allocation and no syscall, and every existing caller of `consolidate` is unchanged.

**What the engine did before this decision**, read for it (`crates/cortex-core/src/dynamics/synapse.rs`, `crates/cortex-neuromod/src/lib.rs`, `runtime/cortex-runtime/src/executor.rs`, `image.rs`, `crates/cortex-connectome/src/lib.rs`):

- `NeuromodulatorState::modulation(baseline)` is `clamp(baseline + dopamine, 0, 1)`, saturating; its comment says *"There is no anti-Hebbian reversal: a dip below zero is clamped, as in Izhikevich 2007."*
- `SynapseBlock::consolidate(slot, m, polarity)` clamps `m` to $[0, 1]$, moves `round(|trace| × m)` into the magnitude with the trace's sign, clamps the magnitude to $[0, 2^{15})$ and takes what it absorbed out of the trace. Its callers are the executor's fan-out (`consolidate_each`, since [ADR-0068](0068-the-reward-addressed.md)) and tests; `consolidate_all` calls it per slot. One of its tests passes −1 and reads *"clamped to 0"*.
- `Modulations` holds `addressed` (`modulation` with the signal), `at_rest` (with the signal at rest) and, since [ADR-0086](0086-the-inhibitory-baseline-built.md), `inhibitory`; the coordinator publishes them as three `AtomicI32` before the tick's first barrier and every worker reads them once per tick; `for_synapse(addressed, polarity)` picks per slot. Under the task's `Delivery::Addressed` the addressed synapses are the presented stimulus's units onto the selected readout's (`Task::trial`), all excitatory by the geometry.
- The modulator section is one 64-byte record: the modulator's 16 bytes, the baseline at `[16..20)`, the inhibitory rule's target period at `[20..24)`, the inhibitory baseline's flag at `[24]` and value at `[28..32)`, and reserved bytes at `[25..28)` and `[32..64)` the loader refuses unless zero. `CortexFileHeader::validate` refuses any version but `FORMAT_VERSION` (L-6).

## Decision Drivers

- ADR-0093's rule as written, and its conditions: the sign reaches an addressed excitatory synapse and no other; unset is today's rule bit for bit.
- `CLAUDE.md`: a change to a record's bytes, reserved ones included, moves `CortexFileHeader::version`, updates whitepaper §5.2's table and gets a changelog entry; a parameter that changes what a run does is part of the image (§8.3); the signed gate is not a registry entry (F-37).
- Principle 5: the loader refuses every byte the writer never produces, by name, and a test holds each refusal at its byte.
- [ADR-0023](0023-executor.md)'s hot path: no allocation, no syscall.
- [ADR-0030](0030-verification-governance.md): every new rule over the lattice of `testkit/prop.rs`, every mutant in the changed lines caught, the determinism pin unchanged.
- Latest ≠ Newest (§2.1): the rule is Florian (2007) and Frémaux, Sprekeler and Gerstner (2010) with ADR-0093's spent trace, and nothing newer; no dependency, no tool.

## Considered Options

1. **Where the gate lives in the image**: a byte of `[25..28)`; a bit of `[24]` beside the inhibitory baseline's flag; a byte of `[32..64)`.
2. **How "set" is encoded**: a flag byte, 1 set and 0 unset, anything else refused; any byte that is not zero.
3. **The configuration**: a `bool`; an enum of the gate's kinds.
4. **The rule in `cortex-core`**: `consolidate` taking the signed domain; a rule beside it, `consolidate_signed`, that is `consolidate` at and above zero.
5. **Where the signed modulation comes from**: a rule in `cortex-neuromod` beside `modulation`; the clamp written in the executor.
6. **How the fan-out selects it**: the addressed word the coordinator already publishes carrying the signed value while the gate is set, an inhibitory slot taking it at no less than zero; a fourth word with a sentinel for unset; a flag word beside the three.

## Decision Outcome

**Options 1 (`[25]`), 2 (a flag byte), 3 (a `bool`), 4 (a rule beside `consolidate`), 5 (a rule in `cortex-neuromod`) and 6 (the addressed word, floored for an inhibitory slot).**

- **The rule in `cortex-core`** (`SynapseBlock::consolidate_signed`). Over a modulation in $[-1, 1]$: at and above zero it is `consolidate`, call for call — the match on the modulation is a range pattern, `i32::MIN..0`, so no comparison at zero exists for a mutant to move. Below zero (`consolidate_against`, private) it takes $|m|$ with `m` clamped at −1.0, moves `round(|trace| × |m|)` out of the magnitude for a positive trace and into it for a negative one, clamps the magnitude to $[0, 2^{15})$ as `consolidate` does, and adds what the magnitude moved — which carries the sign opposite the trace's and is at most the trace — to the trace, so the trace moves toward zero by exactly what the magnitude moved and never past zero: the magnitude less the trace is conserved, where `consolidate` conserves their sum. A synapse whose trace is negative is therefore potentiated below zero, the product of two negatives, which ADR-0093 names as the signed rule of the literature and H-18 reads. One detail the rule needed and ADR-0093 did not say: the magnitude is read within $[0, 2^{15})$, so an inhibitory weight of $-2^{15}$ — which no rule writes, since every rule writes `polarity.weight` of a magnitude at most $2^{15} - 1$ — reads as the rail; read as $2^{15}$, the clamp would move the magnitude by one LSB the trace never carried and spending it would grow a zero or negative trace by one. `consolidate` itself is not touched, so its contract, its callers and its tests are what they were; `MODULATION_MINUS_ONE_Q16` is a private literal tied to 1.0 by a compile-time assertion.
- **The modulation in `cortex-neuromod`** (`NeuromodulatorState::signed_modulation`): `baseline + dopamine`, saturating, clamped to $[-1, 1]$; at and above zero it is `modulation`, and `modulation` is it at no less than zero, which the property test holds over the lattice. The comment on `modulation` stands: the gate's own modulation has no reversal; the signed one is a second rule, read only where the gate is set.
- **The configuration and the executor.** `Config::signed_gate: bool`, default `false`; nothing to refuse. The executor holds it beside the inhibitory baseline, reads it back through `signed_gate()` and takes the loader's through `set_signed_gate`. `Modulations::of(modulator, baseline, inhibitory_baseline, signed_gate)` computes `addressed` from `signed_modulation` while the gate is set and from `modulation` while it is unset; `at_rest` and `inhibitory` are what they were. `for_synapse(addressed, polarity)` returns `inhibitory` for an inhibitory slot while the inhibitory baseline is set, `at_rest` for any slot not addressed, `addressed` for an addressed excitatory slot, and `addressed` at no less than zero for an addressed inhibitory slot while the inhibitory baseline is unset — a range pattern again. So the sign reaches an addressed excitatory slot and no other (ADR-0093: *"no signed modulation on an unaddressed or an inhibitory synapse"*). The coordinator publishes `addressed` in the word it already publishes, and `consolidate_each` calls `consolidate_signed` for every slot: unset, `addressed` is never below zero, the floor is the value, `consolidate_signed` is `consolidate`, and every run before this decision is the run it was, bit for bit; the tick gains one comparison per slot and no word, no allocation and no syscall.
- **The image** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/src/image.rs`; `crates/cortex-connectome/src/lib.rs`). The modulator section's `[25]` is the gate's flag, `SIGNED_GATE_SET` (1) while set and zero while unset; `[26..28)` and `[32..64)` stay reserved, 34 bytes. `FORMAT_VERSION` moves from 15 to **16**, with its note in the version history. The loader reads a flag of zero as unset — the byte a format-15 writer left there, so a format-15 record's bytes read as unset under the new version — and of one as set, refuses any other flag as a byte the writer never produces (`ImageError::ReservedNotZero { section: 42, index: 0 }`), and the image's gate, set or unset, outranks the configuration's (§8.3). A version-15 header is refused as every foreign version is (`HeaderError::ForeignVersion(15)`, L-6).
- **The tests.** `cortex-core` (`synapse.rs`): `a_negative_modulation_moves_the_weight_against_the_trace_and_spends_the_trace` — a positive and a negative trace at −1.0, at −0.5 with the rounding up (2.5 → 3), at −0.25 on odd traces, at −1 LSB (a trace below half the width moves nothing and the most negative moves one), below −1.0 read as −1.0, a magnitude at zero and at the rail with the rest pending, an inhibitory weight both ways, an inhibitory weight of $-2^{15}$ with a zero, a negative and a positive trace, a weight on the wrong side of zero, a zero trace, an empty slot and slot 4 untouched, and `consolidate` bit for bit at 0, 1 LSB, 0.5, 1.0 and `i32::MAX` — and, over the lattice of `testkit/prop.rs` and 400 000 seeded draws, `a_signed_consolidation_is_consolidate_at_and_above_zero_and_spends_the_trace_below_it`: at and above zero `consolidate` bit for bit; below it the weight on its polarity's side, the trace never growing and never crossing zero, the magnitude less the trace conserved, and the magnitude an oracle's in `i64`. `cortex-neuromod`: `the_signed_modulation_is_the_modulation_above_zero_and_reaches_minus_one_below_it` (the edges, and the fixed points of a punishment every trial: −1.0 after the punishment and −0.712 at a trial's end) and, over the lattice and 100 000 draws, `the_signed_modulation_is_the_sum_clamped_to_plus_and_minus_one_and_the_modulation_its_floor_at_zero`. `executor.rs`: `the_signed_gate_is_unset_by_default_and_publishes_a_modulation_below_zero_only_while_set` (the default, the configuration, the setter, and the word the tick publishes under a punishment set and unset, clamped at −1.0, and the same above zero either way); `consolidate_each_under_one_modulation_is_consolidate_all` becomes `consolidate_each_is_consolidate_signed_slot_by_slot_and_consolidate_all_above_zero`, the previous call held bit for bit wherever the modulation is at or above zero and the signed rule slot by slot everywhere; over the lattice and four thousand seeded triples, `only_an_addressed_excitatory_slot_consolidates_under_the_signed_modulation_while_the_gate_is_set` (every other slot's modulation as unset and never below zero, the addressed excitatory slot's the signed one set and its floor unset, and H-18's configuration under the signal after a punishment, at a trial's end, at rest and the mirror under a reward); the calls of the two earlier property tests gain the unset gate and nothing else. `tests/modulation.rs`: `a_punished_synapse_moves_against_its_trace_under_the_signed_gate_and_not_without_it`, on a fork of two excitatory synapses at half the width with the gate at zero — three pairings leave a trace above zero onto one target and below zero onto the other, both weights waiting; a punishment and the next presynaptic spike move the first weight down and the second up, each held exactly to an oracle block consolidated by `consolidate_signed` under the modulation the executor published at the spike's tick, near −1.0, each trace spent to within a hundredth of what was pending and never grown; unset, the same punishment moves neither weight; a reward at rest consolidates as `consolidate` does; the image carries the gate set and unset under a configuration that says the other, and a loaded engine continues alike under the next punishment. `tests/image.rs`: `the_signed_gate_is_written_to_and_read_from_the_image_and_a_byte_left_zero_reads_as_unset` (unset writes zero and reads as unset under a configuration that says set; set writes `[24]` and `[25]` beside the inhibitory value and reads back under a configuration that says unset; flags 2, 3, 0x80 and 0xFF refused; the reserved bytes 26, 27, 32 and 63 refused; a version-15 header refused); the existing reserved-byte cases move from `[25]`, now the flag, to `[26]`, and the inhibitory baseline's test reads the previous version as 15. `crates/cortex-connectome`: the version's two assertions read 16. `tests/differential.rs` and `tests/contention.rs` name the field as `false`.
- **Every pinned number unchanged with the gate unset, bar one.** The workspace's tests in the debug profile pass, 634 passed and 51 ignored where main read 626 and 51 (the eight tests above); the determinism pin of [ADR-0030](0030-verification-governance.md) (`the_random_network_hashes_to_the_pinned_value_on_every_architecture`) is unmoved. One pinned number moves with the format and not with the gate: `REVERSAL_IMAGE_CRC_1024`, the CRC-64 of H-17's whole inhibited image, header included, whose version field this decision moves; it is left as it stands here, failing in the weekly job, and the decision after this one amends ADR-0093's stopping rule for it before any rewarded run, with the evidence that every other byte of that image stood.
- **The mutation gate** ([ADR-0030](0030-verification-governance.md)): every mutant `cargo-mutants` makes in the changed lines is caught, in CI, as the measurement's ADR records with the pull request.

### Consequences

- Good: ADR-0093's rule exists in the rule, the modulator, the executor and the image, and unset it is the rule before it bit for bit, by construction — `consolidate` is not touched, the addressed word is never below zero unset, and the floor and the delegation are identities there.
- Good: the tick publishes nothing new and reads nothing new: the signed modulation is the addressed word itself while the gate is set, and the fan-out makes one more comparison per slot.
- Good: the gate is part of the image and outranks the configuration on load, so a run with it set is defined by its image (§8.3); it is not a registry entry (F-37).
- Neutral: the format is 16 for one flag byte; a format-15 image is refused by its header, and its record's zero reads as unset under the new version.
- Neutral: `Modulations::of` takes the gate, so its one caller in the tick and the property tests pass one more argument; `addressed` is in $[-1, 1]$ while the gate is set, which the field's comment says.
- Bad: under the gate set an addressed synapse with a negative trace is potentiated by a punishment; that is ADR-0093's rule, read by H-18 and not argued away here.
- Bad: the magnitude's reading within $[0, 2^{15})$ is a sentence ADR-0093 did not write; it changes nothing any rule writes, and the property test holds it.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **A bit of `[24]`** (option 1): the inhibitory flag's byte would then carry two meanings, and its refusals (a flag of zero with a value that is not; a flag that is neither zero nor one) would have to be rewritten as masks; a byte of its own keeps each flag's refusals as they are.
- **A byte of `[32..64)`** (option 1): no reason to leave a hole at `[25..28)` between the two flags and the inhibitory value.
- **Any byte that is not zero is set** (option 2): the writer never produces 2 to 255, and a loader that read them as set would accept bytes that no engine wrote.
- **An enum** (option 3): the gate has two states; an enum would name a third nobody has decided.
- **`consolidate` taking the signed domain** (option 4): every caller today passes $[0, 1]$, so it would change no run, but the rule's contract — *no anti-Hebbian reversal* — and the test that holds −1 to *"clamped to 0"* would change meaning for every caller; a rule beside it changes no line of `consolidate` and makes the delegation, not a reading of the callers, what holds unset bit for bit.
- **The clamp in the executor** (option 5): the modulation's rules are `cortex-neuromod`'s, and the property test that ties the two rules together belongs beside them.
- **A fourth word, or a flag word** (option 6): a second atomic for a value the addressed word can carry, since unset it is never below zero; and a flag word would be a race between two loads the one word avoids.
- **The sign on every synapse**: rejected by ADR-0093 — under the gate an unaddressed synapse's modulation is the baseline, zero, and on an inhibitory synapse it would put the dopamine term back where ADR-0085 took it out.
