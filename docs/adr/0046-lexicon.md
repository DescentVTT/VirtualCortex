# ADR-0046: The lexicon — a caller's table of token ids to concept ids with a lexical shape, in the runtime; a token's category instantiated on the term arena, a token sequence read into a frame with its markers, a complete frame realised into token ids in the template's order with its nested clause, its markers and its particle; a role term that names its own filler; `surface_token_id` written; the words the host's

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0040](0040-categorial-reduction.md) gave the runtime `comprehend`, which reads a sequence of lexical categories into a `LinguisticFrameSlot`, and [ADR-0039](0039-hypervector-body.md) the frame's sealing and Layer 1's readout; the lexicon that turns a host's tokens into categories and a frame back into tokens stayed Specified in whitepaper §5.2.20, §6.9 and §8.8, and the only lexicon in the tree was a seven-word table in `runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/language.rs` with its category shapes written by hand. Three fields and one rule waited on it: `surface_token_id` ("Surface token the lexicon last realised") was written by no rule; `politeness_level` "the lexicon reads it" and `GATE_PARTICLE_OPEN` "the lexicon may emit the marker" were read by nothing; and `realisation_order` with `ROLE_CHILD` ([ADR-0021](0021-native-cognitive-language-and-conceptual-blending.md)) named a descent into a child frame that nothing made. The proposal of brief 023 asked for a "fixed-capacity cache-friendly lexicon table" mapping 32-bit token ids to categories on the `TermNode` arena, a comprehension path through `comprehend` and a realisation path emitting role concepts in the template's order with the speech act, the politeness level and the prosody particle, "in the runtime (or `cortex-linguistic` without external dependencies)", with round-trip tests.

Three facts shaped the answer. A category is a `TermNode` of `cortex-reasoning`, so a table that instantiates one cannot live in `cortex-linguistic` (TC-2, `scripts/check-deps.mjs`): the lexicon is a composition and the runtime's. `comprehend` binds a role to the head of the category that filled it, so a modifier category `X/X` or `X\X`, whose argument is the phrase it modifies, fills a role with that phrase's head and never with its own concept: no shape of category could put a hedge or a tag into the affect role, which the epistemic template requires. And a realised utterance is the frame's concepts in order, without the function words that made them phrases, so it can be read back only where a bare content token is a noun phrase on its own: a name, a pronoun, or every noun of a language without articles.

## Decision Drivers

- ADR-0016's one-owner rule: the frame owns the role assignment, the reducer the derivation, the lexicon the mapping between a host's ids and the engine's; the runtime composes ([ADR-0023](0023-executor.md)).
- §1.5 and rule L-3: words stay outside; a token is an id the host chose, and no string, word or language name enters a crate.
- TC-5 and [ADR-0025](0025-term-arena-and-unification.md)'s precedent: the table is a caller's slice, every bound is the caller's and every exceeded bound a value; a category is instantiated with fresh variables from a counter the caller keeps (standardising apart is the caller's).
- ADR-0040's reducer stays as it is: the four rules, the greedy order, the log; what H-10 asks of it is unchanged by a table of shapes.
- ADR-0010 and principle 3: what the exit test's thirteen-token table reads and realises is stated; what a lexicon of a language would is not.

## Considered Options

1. The proposal: the table in `cortex-linguistic`, categories as strings or as a record of the crate.
2. **A runtime module: entries `(token, concept, shape, formal)` in a caller's slice sorted by token, eight lexical shapes as constants each naming the category the reducer takes, three atom functors and the act and particle markers in the table, a reading that instantiates each token's category on the caller's arena and calls `comprehend`, a realisation that walks the frame's order with an explicit stack for nested frames, and a role term `ROLE(c)` that names its filler.**
3. A hash table or trie over tokens: needs allocation or a caller's bucket layout for a lookup a binary search does over a sorted slice.
4. Categories kept as term templates in a lexicon arena, copied per use: an arena to size and a copy per token for what a shape builds in at most ten nodes.

## Decision Outcome

Option 2, in `runtime/cortex-runtime/src/lexicon.rs`, with the extension of `comprehend` in `language.rs`; no record changes.

- **The table.** `Entry { token, concept, shape, formal }`: a token id, its concept id (the codebook index a frame holds), its lexical shape and the token the formal register uses in its place (0 for none). `Lexicon { entries, sentence, np, n, act_open: [u32; 4], act_close: [u32; 4], particles: [u32; 6] }` over a caller's slice; `is_well_formed`: the entries strictly sorted by token with no token zero and every shape one of the eight, the three atoms distinct and below the role band, every marker that is not zero no entry's token and no other marker's, the particle of `PROSODY_NONE` zero; `lookup(token)` by binary search; `token_of(concept, politeness)` the first entry by token with that concept, its formal variant at or above `POLITENESS_FORMAL` when it has one. A refusal of the table is `LexiconError::Malformed`.
- **The shapes**, each instantiated at the scratch's cursor with variables from the reading's counter, the cursor and the counter put back on a refusal: `SHAPE_NOUN` `N(c)`; `SHAPE_NOMINAL` `NP(c)`, a bare noun phrase (a name, a pronoun, a mass noun, or a noun of a language without articles); `SHAPE_DETERMINER` `NP(X)/N(X)` and `SHAPE_ADJECTIVE` `N(X)/N(X)`, the head passing up, the role term a constant of the band with no bit, which names no role; `SHAPE_INTRANSITIVE` `S(c)\NP(A):subject`; `SHAPE_TRANSITIVE` `(S(c)\NP(A):subject)/NP(P):object`; `SHAPE_HEDGE` `S(V)/S(V)` and `SHAPE_TAG` `S(V)\S(V)`, their role term `AFFECT(c)`. An adjective's concept reaches no slot: the frame has none for a modifier of a noun.
- **The role term that names its filler** (`language.rs`). `comprehend` now reads a reduction's role term as a constant of the band (the role's filler is the argument's head, as before) or as a compound of one child over a constant of the band, `ROLE(c)` with `c` a constant (the role's filler is `c`); anything else names no role. The reducer is untouched: a compound role term unifies like any child, and a composition copies its index. So a hedge applied to a sentence fills the affect role with the hedge's own concept, which the epistemic template's realisation puts first, and a tag likewise, last.
- **Comprehension.** `comprehend_tokens(tokens, lexicon, scratch, reading, template, speech_act, politeness)`: a token that is one of `act_open` or `act_close` sets the frame's act (the caller's `speech_act` when there is none), one that is a particle sets the frame's marker and opens the particle slot, every other token is looked up (`UnknownToken(position)`) and its category instantiated into `reading.categories` in order (`CategoriesFull`, `ArenaFull`, `UnknownShape(position)`); then `comprehend` with the lexicon's sentence atom, whose errors pass through as `Language`. `Reading { categories, next_variable }` is the caller's slice and counter; two readings over one scratch never share a variable.
- **Realisation.** `realise(frames, root, lexicon, out) -> Result<usize, LexiconError>`: the act's opener (`act_open[act]`, none for zero or an act the table does not name); the root's `realisation_order`, each role's concept as its token at the frame's own politeness, and at `ROLE_CHILD` the frame at `child_frame_idx` realised in place by an explicit stack of at most `MAX_NESTING` (8) frames, every frame visited once (a child slot that returns to a frame already realised, or a nesting past the bound, is `TooDeep`; the walk's budget is six steps per frame of the arena, so it ends by construction); then the closer; then the particle of the root's marker when its particle slot is open. A child clause's markers and particle are not realised: they are the utterance's. The last token realised is written into the root's `surface_token_id`; `Incomplete(frame)`, `NoSuchFrame(index)`, `NoToken(concept)` and `OutFull` refuse with the output partly written.
- **What the exit test holds** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/language.rs`; a table of thirteen tokens with their words exists in the test alone, the tokens 1 000 onward, the markers 2 001, 2 003 and 3 001 to 3 005). "the dog chased the cat" reduces in the four steps of ADR-0040 into a causative frame (dog, chase, cat) at 1.0 and realises as the three content tokens in subject–action–object order, the determiners gone, the surface token the object's; sealed and read back it holds ADR-0040's pinned distances (2 574, 2 562, 2 482; the affect at 4 997, unbound) and the decoded frame realises the same tokens; the swapped sentence reads back swapped and "the bird slept" cannot be realised as a causative frame (`Incomplete`). "maybe the dog chased the cat" reduces in five steps into an epistemic frame whose affect is the hedge's concept, realises hedge-first, and read back through the codebook holds four roles at 3 195, 3 157, 3 157 and 3 227 bits (confidences 0.376, 0.383, 0.383, 0.370); "the dog slept sadly" reduces in three into a state frame with the tag's concept, realised last; a tag before its sentence, and an adjective's phrase without a determiner, are no derivation. "please spot chased felix ba", two names as bare noun phrases, reduces in two steps into a directive frame with the suggest marker and its slot open, realises as exactly the five tokens it came from, and read back gives the same act, marker and roles: the round trip is closed. A causal frame with a state frame bound in its child slot realises subject, action, object and then the child's two roles, the formal register's variant for the pronoun at the frame's own politeness, the plain token at a plain register. An unknown token is refused at its position before the reducer runs, and a formal variant is realised, never comprehended.
- **What is decided, and what is not.** R-9's realisation direction exists on ids: a frame becomes tokens with its markers, and tokens become a frame, with `surface_token_id` written for the first time. No word is in the engine: which token a host binds to which concept, and how a language renders a determiner it lost, is the host's. H-10 is unchanged: the shapes are eight categories the reducer takes, and what it cannot derive (an object relative, a coordination, a verb-phrase adverb after a subject) it still cannot. Layer 3's key and value from the concept and the affect, and the affect slot realised through an attached metaphor, stay Specified.
- **Not adopted.** *Option 1*: TC-2, and words in a crate. *Option 3*: a bucket layout for what a sorted slice gives at $O(\log n)$. *Option 4*: an arena of templates copied per use for categories a shape builds in ten nodes at most. *A conjunction shape* (`(S\S)/S` for "because"): the reducer's greedy order takes the subject before a following clause arrives; a nested frame is bound by the caller and realised by the descent, and the shape waits for the chart of H-10. *A determiner realised*: the frame holds no determiner; a host that needs one renders it. *Standardising apart in the runtime*: the reading's counter is the caller's, as ADR-0025 left it.

### Consequences

- Good: the three fields and the rule that waited on a lexicon have their reader and their writer; the language stream is one path on ids from a host's tokens to a frame, a hypervector and back to tokens.
- Good: no record, no field, no format bump, no allocation; the reducer is untouched, and the role term's extension is one match arm in the composition.
- Good: a lexicon of any language is a slice of entries and seven markers; its shapes are the engine's, its words are not.
- Bad: a noun shaped `N(c)` needs its determiner to be read, and a realised utterance has none; only a nominal round-trips. A host whose language has articles renders them on the way out and strips them on the way in, or shapes its nouns as nominals and loses the determiner's phrase.
- Bad: the eight shapes are the constants of this decision; a shape the reducer cannot use (type raising, a conjunction) waits for H-10's chart.
- Bad: the runtime module has no reader in the engine: `comprehend_tokens` and `realise` are called by tests and by a host, as `comprehend` was.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **A marker per act as one token** instead of an opener and a closer: a directive opens (請) and a question closes (嗎) in the language the reader's guide is written in; two positions cost eight words of the table.
- **The particle before the closer**: the closer is the act's, the particle the prosody's; the order is stated and a host may leave either zero.
- **The register's variant per concept in a second table**: a field of the entry is one slice, not two to keep sorted.

## Confirmation

`lexicon.rs`: the table's well-formedness clause by clause (unsorted, a token twice, token zero, shapes 0, 9 and 255, atoms alike or in the band, a marker that is a word or a marker twice, a particle for no marker); lookup and the reverse lookup with the formal variant and the first entry by token; every shape's node and variable count and its root's structure, an unknown shape's position, a full arena putting the cursor and the counter back; tokens into a frame with the markers taken out, a hedge and a tag in the affect role, an adjective reaching no slot, a nominal a noun phrase and a noun not, the refusals; a frame realised in order with its child, its markers and its particle, the formal register from the child's own politeness, the slot closed, a marker without a particle, an act the table does not name, the epistemic hedge first; the refusals of realisation (a missing frame, an incomplete one, a concept without a word, an output too small, a cycle of three frames, a chain of nine). `language.rs`: a role term that is a constant, a compound over a constant, a compound over a variable, and one of two children. `tests/language.rs` as above. `npx spec-guard` asserts `fn comprehend_tokens` and `fn realise`. The mutation gate on the changed lines (ADR-0030) passes in CI.
