# ADR-0087: Inhibition off the reward's gate, measured — H-16 is yes: on the network ADR-0077 settled, with the modulation baseline at zero and the inhibitory baseline at 0.5, the task as built moves the selection to the answer in 128 of the last 128 trials in the assignment and in the mirrored assignment, the crossing of 40 of 64 at trials 512 and 256 beside H-14's 448 and 384, while the inhibitory sum falls in every block to 0.41 of the image's, as it did under H-15, and every excitatory synapse outside the two answer pairs ends the run as the image holds it, bit for bit; H-15's no is thereby put on the excitatory side — the unrewarded excitatory consolidation, not the inhibitory rule's course; the withheld arm's excitatory weights stay the image's and its selection reads a coin either way, so ADR-0085's second predicted reading did not hold as written for stimulus A; the calibration reproduced ADR-0077 first; H-16's stopping rule at step 3, the configuration the engine learns in named and the next decision named and not taken

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0085](0085-inhibition-off-the-reward-gate.md) took the decision H-15's stopping rule named after [ADR-0083](0083-plasticity-everywhere-measured.md)'s no — the reward's gate, with its named cost taken first — and wrote **H-16** in whitepaper §11.1 with everything fixed before any run: H-14's configuration — [ADR-0077](0077-the-background-side.md)'s settled image, the gain 1.75, the controller off, [ADR-0076](0076-two-injections.md)'s stimulus, the task as [ADR-0059](0059-a-task-a-readout-and-a-reward.md) and [ADR-0068](0068-the-reward-addressed.md) built it under `Delivery::Addressed` and `Feedback::Answer`, the modulation baseline at **zero**, 1 536 trials — with one constant moved: **the inhibitory baseline at 0.5**, a parameter [ADR-0086](0086-the-inhibitory-baseline-built.md) built into the executor and the image, under which every synapse of an inhibitory block consolidates half of what it pairs at each presynaptic spike, the dopamine term never reaching it, while every excitatory synapse stays under the gate; the arms (the assignment, the mirrored assignment, the reward withheld as a reading); the criterion (at least 80 correct selections of the last 128 in both rewarded arms, a tie not correct); the assertion (every excitatory synapse outside the two answer pairs ends a rewarded arm as the image holds it, bit for bit, and every excitatory synapse ends the withheld arm so); the prediction, yes, with two predicted readings; and its own stopping rule. Brief 039 is the one round the rule allows. This ADR records it.

The question is which of the two parts of H-15's no was the no: with the baseline at 0.5 the excitatory synapses consolidated unrewarded and leaned every pair toward readout 1, *and* the inhibitory rule halved the arena's inhibition in twenty-four blocks; one run at 0.5 could not tell them apart. H-16 runs the inhibitory rule's course alone, under a gate that lets no excitatory synapse move but the ones a reward reaches.

## Decision Drivers

- **Nothing chosen after a rewarded run.** H-16's constants, arms, length, criterion, assertion, predicted readings and stopping rule are ADR-0085's; the round's integer form of the criterion, of the assertion and of the two readings was committed before the first rewarded run (`5094704` on `main`; `aa35d22` on the branch before the rebase that merged pull request #109), and no clause is dropped or added because of what a run read — the predicted reading that did not hold is recorded as read.
- **The calibration before anything else**, with the mechanism unset, and a mismatch or a moved pin a stop (H-16's stopping rule, step 2).
- **The assertion and the readings computed beside the verdict and not in place of it**: the run dumps every arm and computes the verdict, the reach by polarity, the fall, the lean and the crossings before anything is held.
- **The engine read before a description of it is trusted**: `Modulations::for_synapse` under the polarity (ADR-0086), `Image::decode` (the inhibitory baseline read from the modulator section's flag and value, outranking the configuration's), `Task::trial` under `Feedback::Withheld`, and ADR-0079's oracle, which consolidates the stimulus–readout synapses — all excitatory — under the signal where addressed and under zero elsewhere, H-14's rule, and does not model the inhibitory synapses, which the readings read from the record.
- Latest ≠ Newest: no mechanism beyond ADR-0086's, no constant of the engine, no dependency. The image is ADR-0077's with the flag and the value written; the oracle is ADR-0079's as ADR-0083 left it; the delivery is the task's own.
- [ADR-0075](0075-the-dispatch-scope-follows-the-diff.md): the dispatch's scope follows the diff, and the clause that applied is stated here.
- [ADR-0084](0084-the-shards-take-tests.md): the weekly shards take tests, so the new binary's name steers nothing.

## Considered Options

1. **The image**: ADR-0077's frozen image with the modulator section's flag and value written (`inhibited_image`), or the settled engine re-encoded with the parameter set in its configuration.
2. **The oracle**: ADR-0079's, modelling the excitatory pair synapses only and reading the inhibitory ones from the record; or extended to replay the inhibitory rule.
3. **The assertion's integer form**: the reach of a run by polarity (`reach_by_polarity`) and a rule over it (`excitatory_held`): in a rewarded arm no excitatory synapse outside the two answer pairs moved, in the withheld arm none at all; the inhibitory synapses outside the clause.
4. **The predicted readings' integer forms**: (a) `falls_every_block` — each block's inhibitory sum below the block's before, the first below the image's; (b) `lean` — the readout each stimulus selected more often over the withheld arm's last 128 trials — against `frozen_lean`, the same over ADR-0077's pinned frozen block of sixty-four trials, computed before the run and held as `FROZEN_LEAN_1024`.
5. **One `exhaustive` test for the three arms** from the one image, the calibration a frozen block from the zero image with the parameter unset; the gate's eight trials on the instrument's network with the parameter set, and the same with it unset.
6. **The binary**: `tests/inhibition.rs`, on the shared harness, with nothing added to the harness.

- Good: H-15's no is attributed by one run with one variable against each of H-14 and H-15: the inhibitory rule's course, alone, leaves the learning at 128 of 128, so what stopped it at 0.5 was the excitatory synapses' unrewarded consolidation.

- Good: the assertion held to the LSB on every arm, so the attribution is exact: the only excitatory synapses that moved are the two answer pairs' 1 584 and 1 604, and none moved with the reward withheld.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **The parameter in the configuration rather than the image** for the runs: the image defines the run (§8.3), and ADR-0086 put the parameter in it; the harness decodes it under a configuration that says unset to show the image's outranks.
- **A longer run, to read where the rise stops**: a constant moved after a rewarded run; the next decision names the reward-prediction error and the reversal, which are the ways to read it.
- **The shuffled reward**: not run; ADR-0085 named the withheld arm as the reading, and H-14's shuffled arm already read lock-in under the gate.
- **A rule rewritten so that the lean reads as predicted** (a wider window, the ties counted, a margin): a clause changed after a run; the reading stands as written and as read.
