# ADR-0077: The background side — step 2 of H-12's stopping rule: the network settled before the task under the gain held (ADR-0055's criterion as an integer rule, held at the ninety-sixth window with the excitatory sum at 0.925 of the prior's and the readout units' rate the prior's) and the criticality controller on at its step of an eighth (settled at the thirty-fifth at 0.453, the gain swinging between 2.2 and 2.8 and the readouts seven to twenty-four times louder, as predicted), each alone, each run quiet, saved as an image with the baseline at zero and read over a frozen run by three measures written first; the settled network fires once, is seen at 62 of 64 and its trace is net potentiation on average for the first time at a gain at which the readout sees, +0.4 per synapse per trial, but the sign reads 53 against 56; the controller fails all three; no candidate passes, there is no rewarded run, and by the rule's step 3 H-12 closes as a no for this task in this regime, the three routes the next decision chooses among named and not built

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0076](0076-two-injections.md) reached the first step of H-12's stopping rule (whitepaper §11.1, written before that round's outcome): under a stimulus that fires every unit of the presented set once and not again, the trace on a stimulus's synapses into a readout is still net depression — by 2.0 per synapse per trial where it had been 15.7 — the sight passes at 62 of 64 and the sign reads 13, so the stimulus side is exhausted. ADR-0076 said what remains and whose it is: at one presynaptic spike per presentation the rule pairs every readout unit's last background spike as depression at the volley, the response of one volley pairs about two per cent of the synapses as potentiation, and *the ratio of the two is the background rate's, not the stimulus's, and no stimulus that fires once can move it*. The rule's step 2 allows at most one round on that side and names its two routes — the criticality controller on, or a network settled before the task — with the sight and the sign as the calibrations and every other constant held; its step 3 closes H-12 as a no for this task in this regime if that round cannot make both pass; its step 4 yields to a calibration that passes.

Brief 035 is that round, and it says so. It tries both routes, each alone and in a fixed order — the settled network first, the controller second — and lets three measures that read no selection, reward or outcome pick: ADR-0074's measure that the stimulus still fires once, [ADR-0065](0065-the-instrument-recalibrated.md)'s sight and [ADR-0072](0072-what-the-trace-is-made-of.md)'s sign, all at their marks of 56 of 64 and none changed. The stimulus is ADR-0076's pick, pinned by its efficacies under both candidates; the rule, the geometry, the readout window, the trial and the seeds are unchanged; no constant of the engine moves, the controller's step is [ADR-0055](0055-a-weight-that-settles.md)'s eighth and the settled candidate's gain is 1.75, neither searched. The brief wrote one prediction before the round ran and named it a Hypothesis: the controller, measured at 1 024 units on the lattice prior from a gain of 2.0 ([ADR-0053](0053-the-waking-day-and-the-target-period.md), ADR-0055), raises the gain to between 2.1 and 2.75 and the rate to between 9 and 36 Hz per unit, swinging window to window — an order of magnitude louder than the instrument's readouts, the opposite of what ADR-0076 said is needed — so it is expected to fail at least one of the three measures; it is tried because the rule names it and because a line closed without trying it would leave the objection that it was not.

Three facts of the tree shaped how the round was built, each read before it ran (principle 2). *Brief 026's settling clause reads a rate, not a level* ([ADR-0070](0070-where-256-units-settle.md)): it held at the ninth window while the sum fell by a third more, so this round's settled point is ADR-0055's reading — every one of the last sixteen windows within 0.75 per cent of the window sixteen before — written as an integer rule over windows before the lead-in ran, and the lead-in stops at the first window the rule holds at. *Encoding an image requires a quiescent engine* (`Image::encode` refuses otherwise, [ADR-0024](0024-cortex-image-and-clock-sweep.md)), so the settled network is saved only after it is run quiet without the drive, and what the quiet run changed in the sums is read, not assumed. *The image carries the modulation baseline and the homeostasis record, and the clock resumes where the image was written* ([ADR-0033](0033-tick-duration-in-the-header.md)): the frozen run's baseline of zero is one patch of the modulator record in the image bytes, the gain and the step the lead-in reached are carried as they are, every stamp keeps its meaning, and the executor's train — which the image does not hold — is what ADR-0072's oracle replays, so the oracle is seeded from the record before the first trial and held to the record at every trial as before.

## Decision Drivers

- H-12's stopping rule, applied as written: step 2 is one round, on the background side, one variable with two named settings, the sight and the sign as its calibrations; step 3 is a decision the rule was written to reach and not a failure of the round; the round reports which step it reached.
- The brief's standing directives: no mechanism (the controller is [ADR-0036](0036-criticality-control.md)'s, run as built; a settled network is the harness running the engine under its drive), no constant of the engine, no new crate, no record change, the image format 14; nothing chosen after a rewarded run, and no rewarded run unless a candidate passes all three measures; every loop bounded by construction, a settling lead-in among them; every number an oracle can produce computed by it first, and the engine read before an oracle is trusted about it.
- Latest ≠ Newest (§2.1): the round adopts nothing; a lead-in, a quiet run and an image round trip are the harness's existing operations in a new order.
- [ADR-0061](0061-the-learning-runs-leave-the-gate.md), [ADR-0073](0073-the-whole-domain-tests-sharded.md) and [ADR-0075](0075-the-dispatch-scope-follows-the-diff.md): the runs are weekly `exhaustive` tests in the `instrument.rs` shard, whose bound ADR-0073 named; the gate gains one test running no whole run; the dispatch's scope follows this round's diff.
- The brief's empowerment: the exact form of the settling criterion (at least as strict as ADR-0055's, written first), the image or a settling per run, one test or several, one ADR or two, and which numbers the ADR restates, are the round's to decide and record here.

## Considered Options

1. **Both candidates, each alone, in the brief's order, each a lead-in by the rule to its bound, run quiet and saved as an image with the baseline patched, then a frozen run read by the three measures; the first that passes is the configuration, and none is the rule's step 3.**
2. Settle per run — the lead-in before every frozen run instead of once — and no image. Twice the lead-in's cost for the same state, and no way to freeze the weights: the baseline lives in the image (§8.3), and the runtime exposes no setter between ticks.
3. The controller alone, or the settled network alone. The rule names both; a line closed after one leaves the other as an objection.
4. A third background — a quieter drive, a different inhibitory period, targeted inhibition of the readouts, a mix of the two candidates. The brief forbids each by name, and every one is a constant chosen by the reading.

### Consequences

- Good: H-12 has a decision, reached by a rule written before three of its rounds' outcomes were known and applied as written at each step; the line stops where it said it would, and the next decision is an architectural one with three named routes and the readings that bear on each.
- Good: the background side's two named settings are measured on the instrument's own configuration rather than inferred from another prior: the settled network keeps the readout's rate and turns the trace net potentiation on average, the controller drowns the instrument, and the controller's prediction held in every clause.
- Good: the harness can run and compose from an executor a lead-in left — the oracle seeded from the record and held to it at every trial — so a later round that needs a settled or a regulated network before a task has the run it needs, and every pinned number before this round stands under the refactoring.
- Neutral: the gate's runtime tests gain one test of $2^{18}$ ticks at 1 024 units; the weekly job gains two runs, one of them ninety-six windows.
- Bad: no rewarded run, for the sixth round; the sign failed by three trials on the settled network, the nearest it has come, and the rule closes the line there.
- Bad: the closing is a no about this task in this regime, and the readings say the regime is the harder half of it — the instrument reads sub-critical and the controller saturated, and neither round on the line touched that.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Options 2, 3 and 4**: above.
- **Extending the settled lead-in past 160 windows, or the criterion's tolerance**: the rule held at 96, inside the extended bound, and the sum's rate of fall there (0.03 to 0.06 per cent a window) says a longer lead-in starts the run a fraction of a per cent lower on the same curve; the readout's rate, which the sign follows, did not move over ninety-six windows.
- **A stricter or a looser settling rule**: the empowerment allowed a form at least as strict as ADR-0055's; the strict form was written first and held at 96 by 0.001 of a per cent, and a looser one would have held at 80 with a sum 0.7 per cent higher, which the frozen run's reading does not turn on.
