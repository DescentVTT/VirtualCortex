# ADR-0091: The assignment reversed, measured — H-17 is no: on the configuration H-16 named, an engine that learned the answer in 128 of the last 128 trials before the flip selected the new answer in 0 of the last 128 after it, in both arms; over the 1 536 trials after the flip it selected the other readout 2 and 1 times, earned 2 and 1 rewards and tied 0 and 1 times, while the counts' gap stood at 16 to 24 spikes a trial and the old answer's pairs, punished 1 534 times each arm, did not move; ADR-0090's assertion held; the first half reproduced H-16's arm bit for bit and the calibration ADR-0077 before it; H-17's stopping rule at step 4, the next decision named with this round's readings as the need it must meet, and not taken

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0089](0089-the-assignment-reversed.md) chose, among the candidates H-16's stopping rule named, the assignment reversed within a run and wrote **H-17** in whitepaper §11.1 before any run: the configuration [ADR-0087](0087-inhibition-off-the-reward-gate-measured.md) named — [ADR-0077](0077-the-background-side.md)'s settled image, the modulation baseline at zero, the inhibitory baseline at 0.5, [ADR-0076](0076-two-injections.md)'s stimulus, the task as built under `Delivery::Addressed` and `Feedback::Answer` — over **3 072 trials with the answer's mapping flipped once, between the 1 536th and the 1 537th**, by `Task::mirrored` and nothing else; two arms, the assignment first and the mirrored first; the criterion (clause 1, at least 80 of trials 1 409 to 1 536 correct under the first mapping; clause 2, at least 80 of the last 128 correct under the second; a tie not correct; yes when both hold in both arms, a clause-1 failure a failure to replicate H-16); an assertion; the readings; the prediction, **no**; and its stopping rule. [ADR-0090](0090-the-old-answer-held-from-the-trial-after-the-flip.md) amended the assertion before any run, because the first trial after the flip still consolidates the last reward of the first mapping. Brief 040 is the one round the rule allows. This ADR records it.

The question is the one a learning engine is judged by after "does it learn": can it change its mind. The prediction was written as a no from two rules of the tree — the selection is a deterministic comparison of the two readouts' counts with no exploration in it, and under the reward's gate a pair consolidates only in the trial after one that selected it and was rewarded, while nothing depresses what a reward raised — and the round was run for the size of the no, which is the measured need the mechanism after it must meet.

## Decision Drivers

- **Nothing chosen after a rewarded run.** H-17's constants, arms, length, flip, criterion, assertion and stopping rule are ADR-0089's, its assertion as ADR-0090 amended it; ADR-0090 was committed before the constants and the constants before the first rewarded run (`67d0124` and `5262769` on `main`; `1d06422` and `f2ba2e7` on the branch before the rebase that merged pull request #113), and no clause was dropped or added because of what a run read.
- **The calibration before anything else**, and a mismatch a stop (H-17's stopping rule, step 2).
- **The assertion and the readings computed beside the verdict and not in place of it**: each arm's run dumps everything and computes the clauses, the assertion and the readings before anything is held.
- **The engine read before a description of it is trusted**: `Task::trial` writes the addressed set and the reward between a trial's last tick and the next trial's first, and `Executor::address` holds the set until it is written again, so a trial's reward is consolidated during the trial after it (ADR-0090's reason); `Readout::select` and `compute_gating` select the larger count with nothing else in them.
- Latest ≠ Newest: no mechanism, no constant of the engine, no dependency; the flip is the task's own flag.
- [ADR-0088](0088-a-fourth-shard.md): the floor of a weekly shard is its longest test, so the two arms are two tests.
- [ADR-0075](0075-the-dispatch-scope-follows-the-diff.md): the dispatch's scope follows the diff, and the clause that applied is stated here.

## Considered Options

1. **How the flip is made**: a variant of the harness's run that negates `mirrored` before one trial's index (`run_on_flipped`, `earned_run_flipped`), with `run_on` and `earned_run_under` calling it with no flip; a loop of the arm's own; or two runs on one executor.
2. **Where the arms live**: `tests/inhibition.rs`, beside H-16, on the shared harness; or a binary of their own.
3. **What is pinned**: both halves; or the second half only, the first held to H-16's pinned arm.
4. **The calibration**: its own test; or the first step of each arm's test, as H-16's.
5. **One ADR or two.**

## Decision Outcome

### Consequences

- Good: the property the configuration had not been asked for is read, and the answer is exact: an engine that learned the answer in 128 of 128 does not revise it in 1 536 trials after the mapping turns, selecting the other readout three times in two arms.
- Good: the need the next mechanism must meet is a number, not a guess: the counts' gap, the rate at which the gate crosses by itself, what one reward moves, the lead it must overturn, and the trace a punished trial already addresses.
- Good: ADR-0090's carry-over was read where it was derived — one old pair, 68 and 69 synapses, in the 1 537th trial — and nowhere else.
- Neutral: the verdict matched the prediction, so the round confirms what two rules of the tree implied; the numbers are what it adds.
- Bad: the inhibitory sum is still falling at the 3 072nd trial, at 0.17 of the image's, so the network the revision was asked of is a network in transit, as H-16's was; the operating regime stays open.
- Bad: one reversal at the half of one run, one seed, one size; a reversal before the learning has saturated, or a schedule of them, is not read.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **A loop of the arm's own, or two runs on one executor, for the flip**: either would duplicate `run_on`'s readings or split a run the criterion reads as one; the variant keeps one code path and every earlier run bit for bit.
- **One test for both arms**: ADR-0088's floor is the longest test; two arms of 3 072 trials in one test would be the longest in the tree.
- **Reading the next decision into this ADR**: H-17's stopping rule gives it to an ADR of its own.
