# ADR-0076: Two injections — F-46's drive kept whole and a cancel, a negative basal message into the same units inside the trial, built as a field of the task's stimulus with its refusals and a bit-for-bit pin; the brief's offset lands inside the refractory window, which the membrane rule drops, and its three sizes are too small for the soma the coupling has primed, so the cancel is derived from the engine's rule instead — the tick after the window, over the nine ticks the volley spreads, six messages at the bound per tick, each step by an integer oracle over `integrate` held to the engine's own probe through the task; under it every unit of the presented set fires once and not again (0.00 spikes per unit after the opening against F-46's 2.06), the volley's depression goes to nothing and the background's halves, but the volley's potentiation halves too, because the response ADR-0072 measured was the response to three volleys, and the terms sum to −2.0 per synapse per trial against ADR-0072's hypothesis of +5: the sight passes (62 of 64) and the sign does not (13 of 64), so there is no rewarded run, and by H-12's stopping rule, whose first step this round is, the stimulus side is exhausted

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0074](0074-a-stimulus-that-fires-once.md) proved that no stimulus of one message fires each unit of the presented set once inside the pair window on this instrument: the units carry the drive's standing potential, one message adds the same amount to every one of them, and the band between the drive that fires a unit once and the drive that fires it twice is narrower than the spread. It named the shape that would not weaken the response to fire the volley whole — F-46's own two messages of 1.25, which pass the volley clause, and then a message at the end of the refractory window that cancels what the basal compartment still holds — and did not build it. Brief 034 asks for that and nothing else: a cancel on the task's stimulus (`runtime/cortex-runtime/src/task.rs`, the runtime's composition of [ADR-0059](0059-a-task-a-readout-and-a-reward.md)), a stimulus with no cancel injecting what it injects today bit for bit, refusals for the new ways to be wrong, the offset `REFRACTORY_TICKS` and not searched, three sizes in a fixed order derived after the gain (F-47) by an integer oracle from `BASAL_LEAK_SHIFT` and `REFRACTORY_TICKS` and probed on a unit at rest before use, the pick by ADR-0074's measure over a frozen run, the composition re-read against ADR-0072's +5 as the Hypothesis it is, the two calibrations at 1.75, Deliverable D's three readings, and the criterion only if both calibrations pass.

Whitepaper §11.1 gained, before this round's outcome was known, a stopping rule for H-12 (pull request #92): brief 034's two injections are the last attempt on the stimulus side; if no cancel makes the trace net potentiation with the readout seeing — [ADR-0072](0072-what-the-trace-is-made-of.md)'s sign and [ADR-0065](0065-the-instrument-recalibrated.md)'s sight, both 56 of 64 at the gain 1.75 — the stimulus side is recorded as exhausted; at most one further round follows on the background side; then H-12 closes as a no for this task in this regime. This round is that rule's first step and says below which step it reached.

Two facts of the tree, neither in the brief's arithmetic, shaped the round (principle 1: the repository wins over the document). **The membrane rule drops an input that lands inside the refractory window** (`integrate` in `crates/cortex-core/src/dynamics/membrane.rs`: `basal_in` is zero while `refractory_ticks` is above zero; [ADR-0018](0018-membrane-integration.md) chose the hard window so that a burst of input cannot fire the unit the tick the window ends). A unit that fires its volley spike on trial tick $k$ drops every input on ticks $k + 1$ to $k + 200$ and integrates again on $k + 201$; F-46's drive lands on tick one (a message injected before a tick is integrated on the next, [ADR-0023](0023-executor.md)), the earliest volley spike is on tick one, so the earliest tick any volley unit integrates again is 202 — and the brief's cancel, injected at offset 200 and landing on tick 201, lands inside the window of every unit that fired in the volley and is dropped. **What fires the unit again is the soma, not the basal residual.** Through the window the coupling pulls the soma toward half of what the basal compartment holds, so on the first tick the unit integrates again the soma already stands above the threshold, and a cancel that lands then must pull it back below within that tick through the coupling's sixteenth: it must take the basal compartment well below rest, not to it. The brief's clause (c), "the cancel lands inside every such unit's refractory window, and the residual and the cancel decay in the same compartment at the same rate from there", is the opposite of what the rule does, and the brief's three sizes — the residual, twice it, the bound of one message — are sized to a quantity that is not the one that fires the unit.

## Decision Drivers

- The brief's standing directives: no mechanism (a negative basal message is the executor's existing message, delivered by an inhibitory synapse every tick; what changes is when the task injects), no constant of the engine, no dependency; the gain does not move; nothing chosen after a rewarded run; every claim Implemented, Specified, Target or Hypothesis; every number an oracle can produce computed by it first **and checked against the engine's probe**, because ADR-0074's oracle disagreed with the engine on the gain's scaling (F-47).
- Latest ≠ Newest (§2.1): a second injection of the message the executor already carries, at a moment the runtime already controls. Nothing is adopted.
- Principle 2: the brief's offset and sizes were derived from a picture of the window, not from the rule; this round reads the rule before it builds, and probes what it built before it runs.
- The brief's empowerment: the shape of the cancel in the type ("a field of `Stimulus`, a list of timed injections"), the oracle's form so long as it is integer and checked against the engine, and how the probe and the calibration are organised into tests, are the round's to decide and record here.
- [ADR-0061](0061-the-learning-runs-leave-the-gate.md), [ADR-0073](0073-the-whole-domain-tests-sharded.md) and [ADR-0075](0075-the-dispatch-scope-follows-the-diff.md): the runs are weekly `exhaustive` tests in the `instrument.rs` shard; the gate gains one test running no whole run; the dispatch's scope follows this round's diff, which changes `src/`.

## Considered Options

1. **Build the brief's cancel as written — offset `REFRACTORY_TICKS`, the residual, twice it, the bound — probe it, record that every candidate is dropped or too small, and stop with no rewarded run.** Faithful, and a round spent on an arithmetic error the tree could have corrected on the first day.
2. **Build the cancel the engine's rule needs, derived and not searched, beside the brief's as probed evidence: the offset the tick after the window, the span the volley's spread from the census, the size in messages at the bound by the oracle at rest and at the extreme standing state; then the brief's measure, calibrations and rule for a rewarded run, unchanged.**
3. Time each unit's cancel from its own volley spike, read from the train inside the trial. Exact per unit, and a trial that reads the train every tick.
4. Search the offset or the size on frozen runs until the clauses hold. The brief forbids it, and the lesson of seven rounds.
