# ADR-0068: The reward addressed — the modulation a synapse consolidates under is a function of both its ends: the dopamine term reaches the synapses from the units of the stimulus presented onto the units of the readout the engine selected, every other synapse consolidating under the baseline alone; two flags per unit in the executor's shared state and a modulation per slot in the fan-out, `cortex-core`'s rule, the records and the image unchanged, a run that addresses every unit ADR-0066's bit for bit; and, written before any rewarded run, what this delivery can do on a two-alternative task with a symmetric reward: the sign and the selection combine into the stimulus's answer, so the delivery is a teaching signal toward the answer, lagged by one trial, whose direction on the behaviour is the sign of the trace it consolidates

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0066](0066-the-reward-path-measured-again.md) asked whitepaper §11.1's H-12 through an instrument shown to see the stimulus ([ADR-0065](0065-the-instrument-recalibrated.md)) and answered no, with a mechanism: the two readouts' responses to a stimulus differ by under 0.7 spikes per trial in every block, every coupling from a stimulus set into a readout set falls alike under every feedback, and the four stay equal within a few per cent, "so no asymmetry the assignment could read emerges". Its reading of why is Legenstein, Pecevski and Maass 2008 stated on this network: a reward that is one number, broadcast to every synapse, can separate two synapses only where their traces differ, and the traces a stimulus unit's synapses carry into the two readouts are alike by the geometry's construction. Its first named next step was "a per-unit learning signal: a third factor that reaches the synapses of the readout that fired, or the units the selection read, rather than every synapse alike".

Brief 030 asks for exactly that and nothing else: the rule of [ADR-0032](0032-three-factor-plasticity.md) untouched, the instrument of ADR-0065 unchanged and not recalibrated, the controls, the seeds and the criterion ADR-0066's, and one variable, where the dopamine term is delivered. Three facts of the tree shape the answer. `SynapseBlock::consolidate` in `cortex-core` already takes its modulation per slot; `consolidate_all` is the one that broadcasts a single value over a block, and the executor's `phase_fan_out` called it with one `shared.modulation` for the whole arena. A block's polarity is its presynaptic unit's ([ADR-0049](0049-dale-principle-in-plasticity.md)) and its slots' targets are postsynaptic units, both read in the same loop. And `cortex-neuromod`'s `modulation(baseline)` is `clamp(baseline + dopamine, 0, 1)`, one signal for the engine, so that with the signal at rest it is `clamp(baseline, 0, 1)`, the fixed-modulation control's rule.

## Decision Drivers

- Latest ≠ Newest (§2.1): addressing a reinforcement signal to the synapses between the input that was active and the output that was selected is the classical answer to credit assignment in a three-factor setting (Roelfsema and van Ooyen 2005, AGREL, where the update is the presynaptic activity times a feedback signal to the units that determined the action times the reward-prediction error; Roelfsema and Holtmaat 2018). It stays inside ADR-0032's three factors: the pairing, the trace, the modulator. No gradient, no surrogate gradient, no e-prop, no reward predictor, no critic, no per-synapse learning rate, no structural rule, no dependency.
- [ADR-0016](0016-thirty-two-crate-architecture.md): every quantity has one owner. The modulation stays `cortex-neuromod`'s (the executor calls `modulation` twice, once with the signal as it stands and once at rest, and never computes one itself); the trace and the consolidation `cortex-core`'s; the selection `cortex-basal-ganglia`'s; the task, its sets and the addressed set the runtime's. No new crate, no record change, the image format stays 14, no reserved byte taken.
- The determinism pin of [ADR-0030](0030-verification-governance.md) and every number ADR-0066 pinned do not move: with every unit addressed the fan-out must be the fan-out before this decision, call for call, and a test must hold it with the previous call as its oracle, as [ADR-0062](0062-the-first-complete-sweeps-list.md)'s rewrites are held.
- Nothing is chosen after a rewarded run: the addressed set's definition, whether the presynaptic side narrows it, and what the delivery is expected to do are argued here and committed before the first rewarded run, with the instrument's constants restated unchanged; the measurement is [ADR-0069](0069-the-addressed-reward-measured.md)'s, whatever it reads.
- Principle 5: a set that cannot name a unit outside the arena is better than one refused on reading; where a refusal is needed it is the executor's, by name, whole, and tested.

## Considered Options

1. **Two flags per unit in `Shared`, a source and a target, and a modulation per slot in `phase_fan_out`: a synapse is addressed when its presynaptic unit is a source and its target a target; the coordinator publishes two modulations before the tick's barrier, the addressed one and the one at rest; the task writes the presented stimulus's units as the sources and the selected readout's units as the targets between the trial's last tick and the reward.**
2. One flag per unit, the target side alone: the synapses onto the selected readout, whatever their presynaptic unit — the brief's default shape.
3. A flag in the unit record: a record change, a format bump, a bit taken from a reserved byte for a quantity that is the run's and not the image's.
4. A closure into the fan-out: a trait object or a generic on the worker, on the hot path, for a choice two bits per unit make.
5. A modulation stored per synapse in the block: no room in the 64 bytes, and a record change.

## Decision Outcome

Option 1.

- **The addressed set** is a set of synapses named by its two ends: the product of a set of sources and a set of targets. A synapse from a source onto a target consolidates under `clamp(baseline + dopamine, 0, 1)`; every other synapse under `clamp(baseline, 0, 1)`, which is `cortex-neuromod`'s `modulation` with the signal at rest. The addressing reaches the dopamine term only: with the signal at rest the two modulations are one number, so a run with no reward is the same run whatever is addressed; and every unit is a source and a target until the set is written, so an executor that is never addressed runs as it did before this decision.
- **The executor** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/src/executor.rs`). `Shared` gains `modulation_at_rest: AtomicI32` beside `modulation`, and `sources` and `targets`, one `AtomicBool` per unit each, allocated in `Executor::new` and never after. `Modulations { addressed, at_rest }` is the pair of a tick, `Modulations::of(&modulator, baseline)` computes both by `cortex-neuromod`'s rule, and `for_synapse(addressed)` picks one; `tick` stores both before the barrier that starts the tick, as it stored one. In `phase_fan_out` the source flag is read once per spiked unit, beside its polarity, and the target flag once per slot beside the target's last spike; the per-slot modulations go through `consolidate_each`, which is `SynapseBlock::consolidate` for every slot in order — `consolidate_all`, call for call, when every slot's modulation is the same, which `consolidate_each_under_one_modulation_is_consolidate_all` holds over two thousand seeded blocks with the previous call as its oracle. No allocation, no syscall, no `#[cfg]`, no branch beyond the choice of a number per slot; an empty slot's modulation is never read, since `consolidate` moves nothing there.
- **The interface**, between ticks. `Executor::address(sources, targets)` makes the addressed set exactly the synapses from a unit of `sources` onto a unit of `targets`; it scans both sides before it writes either, and a set that names a unit outside the arena on either side is refused whole as `AddressError::NoSuchUnit` with the set left as it was (the iterators are `Clone` for the scan; `Set::units()` is). `address_all` is every unit on both sides, the state a new executor starts in; `is_source`, `is_target` and `addressed_counts` read the set. An addressing is an input between ticks, like a reward: a run that replays its addressings at the same ticks is the same run. **The image does not carry the addressed set**: it is the run's, as the task's cursor and channels are, and an engine decoded from an image addresses every unit — ADR-0059's debt (a learning run is not resumable from an image) restated with one more item.
- **The task** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/src/task.rs`). `Task` gains `delivery: Delivery`. Under `Delivery::Global` a trial calls `address_all`: every synapse alike, ADR-0066's form. Under `Delivery::Addressed` a trial writes, after the selection and before the reward, whatever the feedback, the presented stimulus's units as the sources and the selected readout's units as the targets, and no target at a tie, where nothing was selected: the set is the outcome's, never the reward's. `TaskError::Address(AddressError)` surfaces the executor's refusal by name, as `Inject` surfaces the injector's; `Task::check` holds every readout inside the arena, so a trial never meets it, and the refusal is tested where it lives. Every refusal `check` wrote before is kept.
- **Why the presynaptic side narrows the set**, argued before the run. The reward of a trial is consolidated at the next presynaptic spike of each synapse, and for a synapse from a stimulus unit that spike is, almost always, the next presentation of a stimulus — the stimulus units fire once per presentation and about once per trial from the background. With every unit a source (option 2), the reward of a trial that presented A would reach, at the next trial's volley, the synapses of whichever stimulus that trial presents onto A's selected readout: A's own at half the trials, B's at the other half. With A's units as the sources, B's synapses are not addressed at all, and the reward reaches A's synapses at A's next presentation, if the addressing has not been overwritten by then — which it has, unless the next trial is A's. So under either shape the dopamine term reaches a stimulus's own synapses only at the trials that present the same stimulus twice running, about half of them; the narrowing removes the other half, in which the reward would reach the wrong stimulus's synapses. The prose of the brief's first deliverable ("a function of the unit the synapse targets") becomes a function of both ends, which the brief's (c) and its empowerment allow.
- **What this delivery can do on this task, written before the run.** Let a trial present stimulus $s$ and select readout $r$, with the answer $a(s)$ and the reward $\pm R$ signed by $r = a(s)$. During the next trial the synapses from $s$ onto $r$ consolidate under $\operatorname{clamp}(\tfrac12 \pm R)$ and the synapses from $s$ onto the other readout under $\tfrac12$. If the trial was correct, $r = a(s)$ and the synapses onto $a(s)$ consolidate under $1$ against $\tfrac12$; if wrong, $r \ne a(s)$ and the synapses onto $r$ consolidate under $0$ against $\tfrac12$ onto $a(s)$. **In both cases the synapses from $s$ onto $a(s)$ consolidate half the width more of their trace than the synapses from $s$ onto the other readout, whatever was selected**; at a tie nothing is addressed. The sign and the selection combine into the answer, so on a two-alternative task with a symmetric reward this delivery is not a credit assignment on the selection but a teaching signal toward the answer, lagged by one trial and reaching the same stimulus's synapses at about half the trials. What it does to the behaviour is therefore the sign of the trace it consolidates more of: a net-positive trace raises the coupling from a stimulus into its answer readout above the coupling into the other, and the selection follows it toward the answer; a net-negative trace lowers it, and the selection follows it away. ADR-0066 read the trace on this background as net depression (a readout unit's background spike within the pair window before the volley is depression, and the background's are the more). The expectation this decision records, to be read against the tables of ADR-0069 and not adjusted to them: **the four couplings, equal within a few per cent under every feedback in ADR-0066, part in the addressed rewarded run, the answer couplings (A→R0 and B→R1; in the mirrored assignment A→R1 and B→R0) moving away from the other two; the direction is the trace's sign, and by ADR-0066's reading it is down; the behaviour follows the couplings, so an accuracy away from chance in the same direction in both assignments is what a delivery that reaches its synapses looks like, and an accuracy at chance with the couplings still equal is what one that does not looks like.** The shuffled reward replaces the sign by a coin, under which the favour toward the answer has no mean; the fixed modulation has no signal, so its addressed form is its global form bit for bit; the global form is ADR-0066's. The criterion (at least 80 of the last 128, in both assignments, the controls at most 76, the sequence the same on one worker and on four) decides whether the engine learned, and nothing here changes it.
- **The uniform case, held.** `the_addressed_modulation_is_the_baseline_with_the_signal_and_the_other_the_baseline_alone` (over the lattice of `testkit/prop.rs` and four thousand seeded pairs) holds that the addressed modulation is `clamp(baseline + dopamine, 0, 1)`, the other `clamp(baseline, 0, 1)`, both `cortex-neuromod`'s, and one number at rest; `consolidate_each_under_one_modulation_is_consolidate_all` holds the per-slot call to the previous call, bit for bit, and to the per-slot rule under a modulation per slot; `an_addressing_is_exact_and_refused_whole_outside_the_arena` holds the interface at its edges; `a_reward_reaches_the_addressed_synapse_and_no_other` (`tests/modulation.rs`) is the two-unit test of ADR-0032 on a fork of three units: a source that is not the presynaptic unit addresses nothing, the addressed branch consolidates its whole pending trace and the other keeps its trace and its weight exactly as the oracle block says, every unit addressed again consolidates the branch that waited, and a loaded image addresses every unit; `the_addressed_delivery_addresses_the_stimulus_onto_the_selected_readout_and_none_at_a_tie` holds the task's side, the withheld feedback included. And every pinned number of ADR-0060 and ADR-0066 — the twelve weekly runs of `instrument.rs`, the ten of `learning.rs`, the gate's two first blocks, the criterion tests — reruns unchanged under this code, as does the determinism pin, which is the uniform case held at scale.
- **Not adopted.** *The target side alone* (option 2): the algebra above; the cross-stimulus half would cancel the same-stimulus half over a balanced sequence, and the reading would be about the lag, not the delivery. *A flag in the record* (option 3): the addressed set is the run's, not the image's, and a record change for it is a format bump for a quantity the loader would then have to reset. *A closure* (option 4): the choice is two bits per unit; a trait object on the fan-out path is a cost and an indirection for nothing. *A per-synapse modulation* (option 5): no room, and the trace is already the per-synapse quantity. *Addressing the sources alone* (the stimulus's synapses onto both readouts): no asymmetry between the readouts, which is the whole question. *Narrowing further to the units that spiked in the window*: the selection is the count over a set, and the set is what the readout reads; a unit's own spike is in the trace already. *A reward of one sign*, so that the selection would decide which synapses consolidate rather than the answer: a change to a constant of the instrument, named for a next round in ADR-0069 and not made here.

### Consequences

- Good: the delivery ADR-0066 named as the first thing a next round would change exists, in the executor and the task, with the rule, the records, the image and every constant of the instrument unchanged; a run that addresses every unit is the run before it, held by a test with the previous call as its oracle and by every pinned number of the two rounds before.
- Good: what the delivery can and cannot do on this task is written down before it is run, with the algebra that says why: on a symmetric reward it is a teaching signal toward the answer, and its direction on the behaviour is the sign of the trace.
- Good: the interface is an input between ticks like a reward, refused whole and by name, and the fan-out reads two bits per unit and picks a number per slot; the hot path gains no allocation, no syscall and no platform-conditional branch.
- Neutral: `Task` gains a field, so every task names its delivery; the harnesses of ADR-0060 and ADR-0066 name the global one, mechanically. `Set::units()` declares its iterator `Clone`.
- Bad: the addressed set is not in the image; a learning run was not resumable from an image before this decision and is not now, with one more item of state the image does not hold.
- Bad: the reward reaches a stimulus's synapses only at the trials that present it twice running, about half of them, because the trace is consolidated at the next presynaptic spike; a delivery that consolidated a trial's pairings at the trial's end, for that trial's stimulus, would need a consolidation the fan-out does not have, a decision for a round that measures a need for it.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Option 2**: above.
- **Option 3**: a record change and a format bump for a quantity of the run.
- **Option 4**: a trait object on the hot path for a two-bit choice.
- **Option 5**: no room in the block, and a second per-synapse quantity beside the trace.
- **Changing the reward's sign structure**: a constant of the instrument; ADR-0069 names it.
