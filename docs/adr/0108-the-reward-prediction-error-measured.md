# ADR-0108: The reward-prediction error, measured — H-19 is no, clause 3 the one that failed: with the critic set the engine learned the mapping, 122 and 125 of the last 128 trials before the flip, and revised it, 119 and 121 of the last 128 at the 4 608th trial, in both arms, and did not settle: over each mapping's last 256 trials one answer pair of each mapping moved by 1.22 to 1.87 per cent of its image coupling and the other by −0.08 to +0.34, where H-18's moved by 3.6 to 9.5; the expectations stayed about a tenth below the reward because the selection stayed wrong or tied in two to five trials of 64, so every correct trial still delivered about a tenth; ADR-0106's two predicted readings held; after the flip the strong punishment of the old answer faded and came back as the expectations rose, the first new selection within 3 to 68 trials; the assertion held; the calibration reproduced ADR-0077 and H-18's first block; H-19's stopping rule at step 4, and the next decision — what else keeps the couplings rising — named and not taken

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0106](0106-the-reward-prediction-error.md) took the reward-prediction error and wrote **H-19** in whitepaper §11.1 before any run: [H-18](0096-the-punished-pair-measured.md)'s configuration — [ADR-0077](0077-the-background-side.md)'s settled image at 1 024 units, the excitatory baseline zero with the signed gate set, the inhibitory baseline 0.5, [ADR-0076](0076-two-injections.md)'s stimulus, the task as built under `Delivery::Addressed` and `Feedback::Answer` with a reward of 1.0, 4 608 trials with the mapping flipped between the 1 536th and the 1 537th — with a critic set in the task: an expected reward $V_s$ per stimulus, zero at the start, the dopamine signal receiving $\delta = r - V_s$, then $V_s \leftarrow V_s + (\delta \gg 5)$. Yes when, in both arms, (1) at least 80 of trials 1 409 to 1 536 are correct, (2) at least 80 of trials 4 481 to 4 608, and (3) each answer pair of the first mapping moves by less than one per cent of its image coupling over trials 1 281 to 1 536, and each of the second's over trials 4 353 to 4 608; a tie not correct. It wrote two predicted readings, the tension the rules predict between settling and revising, **no prediction for the verdict**, and a stopping rule. [ADR-0107](0107-the-critic-built.md) built the critic (`Task::critic`, off unless set) and fixed, before any rewarded run, H-19's constants, rules, calibration and gate, and two findings from reading the engine (F-52, F-53). Brief 046 is the one round the rule allows. This ADR records it.

## Decision Drivers

- **Nothing chosen after a rewarded run.** H-19's constants, arms, length, flip, shift, criterion, assertion, predicted readings and readings' rules are ADR-0106's as ADR-0107 wrote them into the tests, committed before the first rewarded run; no clause was dropped or added because of what a run read.
- **The calibration first**, and a pinned number that moves with the critic unset a stop (H-19's stopping rule, step 2).
- **The assertion and the readings beside the verdict, not in place of it**: each arm's run dumps everything and computes the clauses, the assertion and the readings before anything is held.
- **The engine read before a description of it is trusted**: the dopamine signal carries from one trial into the next (F-53), so a delivery's modulation is not its error alone, and the reading of the punishment's course is the signal a delivery left, not the error.
- Latest ≠ Newest: the rule is ADR-0106's; no dependency, no tool.
- [ADR-0088](0088-a-fourth-shard.md): the two arms are two tests; [ADR-0092](0092-the-shards-dealt-by-cost.md): the round regenerates the cost table from its own dispatch; [ADR-0075](0075-the-dispatch-scope-follows-the-diff.md): the dispatch's scope follows the diff.

## Considered Options

1. **Where the arms live**: `tests/inhibition.rs`, beside H-16 to H-18 on the shared harness; a binary of their own.
2. **What is pinned**: the whole run with each stimulus's expectation per block; the second half only.
3. **How the calibration reaches the configuration**: ADR-0077's settled candidate and H-18's image by its CRC; and H-18's first block reproduced with the critic unset.
4. **How many ADRs**: the mechanism's and this one; one.

### Consequences

- Good: the reward-prediction error does what it was taken for in part: at the flip the answer pairs stand 0.13 to 0.16 above the image's couplings where H-18's stood 0.37 to 0.44 above, and over each mapping's last 256 trials they moved by −0.1 to +1.9 per cent where H-18's moved by 3.6 to 9.5, half of them within a third of a per cent.
- Good: the engine learns and revises under it, 122 to 125 of the last 128 before the flip and 119 to 121 of the last 128 at the end, in both arms, and it tries the new answer within 3 to 68 trials of the flip where H-18 took 211 to 279.
- Good: what keeps the other half of the pairs rising is read, not argued: an expectation a tenth below the reward, which the selection's residual errors and ties set, and an error's carry-over into the next correct trial.
- Neutral: both predicted readings held; the verdict had no prediction.
- Bad: H-19 is no. The engine learns and revises and does not settle, and the accuracy is lower than H-18's, 119 to 125 of 128 where H-18's was 128.
- Bad: the inhibitory sum still falls in every block, to a tenth of the image's; the regime the answer holds in is still in transit.
- Bad: one shift, one seed, one size, one reversal.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Reading clause 3 over the settled pairs alone**: the clause names each answer pair, and a rule that picks the pairs after the run is a clause moved.
- **One test for both arms**: ADR-0088's floor is the longest test.
