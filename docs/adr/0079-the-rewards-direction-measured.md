# ADR-0079: The reward's direction, measured — H-13 is yes: on the network ADR-0077 settled, with the baseline at zero, a reward delivered after every trial to the synapses from the presented stimulus onto its assigned readout raises those couplings, by 12 to 15 per cent over 512 trials and still rising, and that readout's response, above the same trial's with the reward withheld in 128 and 127 trials of the last 128 by six spikes per trial, in both assignments, every synapse outside the pairs bit for bit the image's; the trace's potentiation grows as the couplings rise and the two mechanisms named as bounding it are outrun; the calibration reproduced ADR-0077 first; the next decision is an ADR on the reinforced form, not taken here

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0078](0078-the-rewards-direction.md) chose the task that asks for a sign and wrote it as H-13 (whitepaper §11.1) with its network, its constants, its delivery, its three arms, its criterion, its prediction and its own stopping rule, before this round existed; brief 036 is the one round the rule allows. The question is the one beneath H-12's: every rewarded run before this one — [ADR-0060](0060-the-reward-path-measured.md), [ADR-0066](0066-the-reward-path-measured-again.md), [ADR-0069](0069-the-addressed-reward-measured.md) — was made where the trace on a stimulus's synapses into a readout was net depression, and the couplings moved downward as the sign said. [ADR-0077](0077-the-background-side.md)'s settled network is the first configuration in the tree where that trace is net potentiation on average while the readout sees (+0.4 per synapse per trial, every pair's sum positive after the frozen block, the sight 62 of 64, the sign 53). H-13 asks whether a reward handed to those synapses after every trial, whatever the engine selected, moves them and the response the engine reads through them up, against the same trials with the reward withheld.

This ADR is the round's record: the calibration, the taught delivery, the constants committed before the first rewarded run, the three arms, the verdict by the rules written first, the readings the brief asked for, the step of H-13's stopping rule reached and the next decision the rule names — which this round does not take.

## Decision Drivers

- Nothing chosen after a rewarded run: the criterion's integer form, the arms and the prediction are committed before the first rewarded run, in a commit that holds no outcome, and no clause is dropped or added because of what the run read.
- The engine read before a description of it is trusted (brief 034's lesson, ADR-0076): the oracle that reads the consolidation is held to the record's weights, traces and signal at every trial of every arm, and the signal's figures are computed apart from the tree before the run.
- Latest ≠ Newest: no mechanism, no constant of the engine, no record, no dependency. The delivery is two calls the executor has, in a new order; the image is ADR-0077's; the oracle is ADR-0072's, extended to what a delivery does.
- [ADR-0075](0075-the-dispatch-scope-follows-the-diff.md): the dispatch's scope follows the diff, and the clause that applied is stated here.
- [ADR-0073](0073-the-whole-domain-tests-sharded.md)'s shard budget: `instrument.rs` took 2 928 s of its 7 200 in ADR-0077's dispatch, and the brief's empowerment sends the runs to a binary of their own only if the shard would otherwise pass 60 per cent of its bound.

## Considered Options

1. **The taught delivery in the harness** (test code): after a `Withheld` trial, `Executor::address` to the presented stimulus's units onto its assigned readout's units, then `Executor::reward`.
2. **A `Delivery` or `Feedback` variant in `task.rs`** (source): the task itself addressing the assignment and rewarding whatever the selection.
3. **The three arms as one `exhaustive` test from one image**, the calibration the withheld arm's first block.
4. **Each arm its own `exhaustive` test**, each building the settled image again.
5. **The runs in `instrument.rs`** beside ADR-0077's harness, or **in a test binary of their own** with the harness shared.
6. **The oracle extended to replay the consolidation**, held to the record's weights; or the composition read only on frozen copies of the arms.

## Decision Outcome

Options 1, 3, `instrument.rs`, and the oracle extended, in `runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/instrument.rs`, the block "brief 036"; the harness, the oracle, the criterion's rules, the constants and the gate committed in `6caa3a6` before any rewarded run, the readings and the gate's assertions over them in `a982efc`; the run made once.

- **Why the harness and not `task.rs`.** The delivery is two calls the executor already has in a new order — a variant in the task would be a mechanism the engine offers, which ADR-0078 did not ask for and the standing directive forbids — and a change under `src/` would send the round through the whole-tree sweep under ADR-0075's first clause for a change no test of the swept suite exercises differently. The harness rewrites the addressed set between the trial's last tick and the reward, where `Task::trial` had written it (`Delivery::Addressed`, the selection's), so the task's own write never reaches a tick; the reward is the task's `REWARD_Q16` through the same `Executor::reward`, and the run asserts after every rewrite that the addressed set is 51 sources and 459 targets.
- **Why one test from one image.** The arms are paired by construction only if they decode the same image; building it three times costs three lead-ins of ninety-six windows, and the calibration ADR-0078 asks for before any rewarded run is exactly the withheld arm's first block — a run of 512 trials from the image is, over its first 64, the run of 64 trials from it, the inputs being the same. So `the_rewards_direction_at_1024_units_exhaustive` builds the image once (`settled_image`), holds it to ADR-0077's tables step by step, runs the withheld arm first and holds its first block to ADR-0077's frozen run (`calibration_holds`) before the first rewarded arm decodes the image. A calibration that did not reproduce would have stopped the test there, before any rewarded run, as H-13's stopping rule's second step says.

- Bad: a yes on the taught form says nothing about the reinforced one — a delivery the selection decides starts at chance, where the taught one starts at the answer — and the rise read here is positive feedback whose stop is not read; both are the next ADR's to weigh.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **A variant in `task.rs`** (option 2): above.
- **Each arm its own test** (option 4): three lead-ins for one image, and the pairing would then rest on three decodes of three images asserted equal rather than on one.
- **A binary of its own** (option 5): the threshold the empowerment set was not reached; the harness stays in one file and the shard's reading is in the evidence.
- **The composition read on frozen copies** (option 6): a frozen copy would read the trace's terms but not what the delivery moved into the weight, and the record would not be held at the trial where it moved; the extended oracle reads both and is held at every trial.
- **A second attempt, a constant moved, a clause dropped**: H-13's stopping rule, item 5.
