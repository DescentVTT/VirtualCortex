# ADR-0078: After H-12 — the reward's direction before a task's difference: of the three routes H-12's stopping rule named, the task that asks for a sign is chosen and written as H-13 (on the network ADR-0077 settled, a reward delivered to a stimulus's synapses onto one readout raises the couplings it reaches and that readout's response, the count the engine reads, against the same trials with the reward withheld), with its criterion, its prediction and its own stopping rule written before any run; the structural rule deferred for want of a measured need; the operating regime recorded as the open question a no on H-13 makes the next decision

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0077](0077-the-background-side.md) applied the third step of H-12's stopping rule (whitepaper §11.1): *the three-factor rule with ADR-0068's addressed delivery does not learn the two-alternative difference task on the reference network at 1 024 units, because its pairing statistics are the background's.* The rule then says: "The round after it chooses, by an ADR, among a task that asks for a sign rather than a difference (ADR-0069's fifth named change), a structural rule that adds a synapse (its fourth), and a review of the operating regime against the criticality the network is built to hold (ADR-0036) — the last being an architectural question and not a constant." This is that ADR. It chooses, and it builds nothing.

What ADR-0077 read bears on the choice more than any round before it did. On the settled network — ADR-0055's criterion held at the ninety-sixth window under the gain held at 1.75, the excitatory sum at 0.925 of the prior's, the readout units' rate the prior's 0.28 spikes per trial — ADR-0076's stimulus fires every unit of the presented set once and not again, the readout sees it in 62 trials of 64, and the trace on a stimulus's synapses into a readout is **net potentiation on average for the first time at a gain at which the readout sees**: +0.4 per synapse per trial (the volley's +8.1 and −0.2, the background's +6.4 and −10.2), every one of the four stimulus–readout pairs' sums positive after the frozen block (+0.7, +14.5, +8.4, +13.6). What failed is the sign trial by trial: positive after 53 trials of 64 where ADR-0072's mark asks 56, because the net is the small difference of the background's two terms and the volley's, and its sign trial to trial is the background's. The difference task needs that per-trial sign, since each trial's reward consolidates that trial's trace, and it needs one readout's coupling to rise against the other's by an offset of two spikes per trial where a delivery has been read to move 0.4 to 0.8 ([ADR-0074](0074-a-stimulus-that-fires-once.md)'s reading of ADR-0069's blocks).

Every rewarded run so far — [ADR-0060](0060-the-reward-path-measured.md), [ADR-0066](0066-the-reward-path-measured-again.md), [ADR-0069](0069-the-addressed-reward-measured.md) — was made where the trace was net depression, and ADR-0069's addressed delivery moved the couplings from a stimulus into its rewarded readout *downward*, as the trace's sign said before the run. No run has yet asked the question beneath H-12's: whether the reward, handed to the synapses whose trace is net potentiation, moves them — and the response the engine reads through them — *up*. H-12 could not be answered yes without that; the settled network is the first configuration in the tree where it can be asked.

## Decision Drivers

- H-12's stopping rule, applied as written: the choice is among its three named routes, made by an ADR, and no route is built here.
- **Nothing chosen after a rewarded run** (every brief from 027): the new hypothesis's criterion, its configuration, its controls and its stopping rule are written before its first run, here, rather than by the round that runs it. H-12's stopping rule was written after six rounds; this one is written at birth.
- The repository's order of evidence: the smallest question whose answer routes the others, before the largest build. A route that adds a mechanism needs a measured need (principle 5; ADR-0016's test for anything structural).
- Latest ≠ Newest (§2.1): the choice adopts no method; the delivery, the image, the executor, the task and the oracle it names are all in the tree.
- The pairing rule as the tree holds it (`SynapseBlock::step_stdp`, read for this ADR): at a presynaptic spike an excitatory slot reads the target's **last** somatic spike only; the potentiation decays with that spike's distance after the previous presynaptic spike, and the depression with its distance before this one, scaled by the slot's magnitude ([ADR-0055](0055-a-weight-that-settles.md)). Any claim here about what a rise does to the trace is read from that function.

## Considered Options

1. **The task that asks for a sign, as H-13 in its taught form**: on ADR-0077's settled network with the baseline at zero, a reward delivered after every trial to the synapses from the presented stimulus onto its assigned readout, whatever the engine selected; the couplings it reaches and the response the engine reads from that readout compared, trial by trial, with the same trials under no reward; the mirrored assignment beside it.
2. **The sign task in ADR-0066's form**: a stimulus that already drives one readout more than the other, the reward asked to keep or to reverse it.
3. **A reward of one sign on H-12's task** (a punishment of zero; ADR-0069's second named change): the selection decides which synapses consolidate.
4. **The sign task in its reinforced form first**: the delivery decided by the selection (ADR-0068's), rewards of one sign, the rise read on the rewarded pair.
5. **The structural rule** (ADR-0069's fourth): a synapse added from a stimulus onto the rewarded readout where none was.
6. **The review of the operating regime** against [ADR-0036](0036-criticality-control.md)'s criticality.

## Decision Outcome

**Option 1.** The route is the sign task, and its first form is the one whose answer means one thing either way.

- **Why a sign first.** It is the smallest of the three routes — no mechanism, no constant of the engine, no record, no crate — and it asks exactly what ADR-0077 made newly askable: whether the trace that is now net potentiation on average, consolidated by the reward, raises a behaviour the engine reads. Its answer routes the other two. A yes says the rule moves a response in this regime when the dopamine reaches the right synapses, and the questions that remain are the delivery (whether the selection can steer it) and the magnitude (whether the rise reaches the two spikes a difference needs). A no says the rule does not move a response the right way even when handed the dopamine at the synapses whose trace is positive on average, and then the regime is where the line stands, which is route 3.
- **Why the taught form, and why it is not H-12 again.** The delivery reads the assignment and not the selection: after every trial the addressed set is the synapses from the presented stimulus's units onto its **assigned** readout's units (`Executor::address`) and the reward is delivered (`Executor::reward`), whatever the engine selected. That is a teaching signal and not a credit assignment, and it is named so. It removes the selection's chance level from the delivery, so a no cannot mean that the selection never reached the synapses; and it is not H-12's task, which asked the selection to become right under a reward the selection itself earned. Nothing here reads the selection as a criterion, and H-12 stays closed whatever H-13 reads.
- **Why the baseline at zero.** ADR-0077's settled image carries the baseline patched to zero, and under it a synapse consolidates only under the dopamine term and only where it is addressed (`Modulations::for_synapse`: `clamp(baseline + dopamine, 0, 1)` for an addressed synapse, `clamp(baseline, 0, 1)` for every other). So in a rewarded run **every synapse outside the arm's two assigned pairs ends the run as the image holds it, bit for bit**, and in the run with the reward withheld every synapse does: the only weights that move are the ones the delivery reaches, and the composition ADR-0077 measured under that baseline is the composition the delivery consolidates, at least at the start. Brief 027's baseline of 0.5 would let every synapse consolidate half its trace every trial and continue the settling ADR-0077 stopped, so what moved would no longer be attributable to the reward.

**H-13 (a reward raises the response it is delivered to)**, written into whitepaper §11.1 as a Hypothesis with the following, all fixed here and none by the round that runs it:

- **The network.** ADR-0077's settled candidate, built as ADR-0077 builds it in `tests/instrument.rs` (`candidate` at 1 024 units with the controller's step at zero, `lead_in_until_settled` by `settled_within` to the first window the rule holds at, `quiet`, `frozen_image` with the modulator's baseline patched to zero, `frozen_from`); every arm starts from that one image. **The calibration first**: before any rewarded run the round re-derives the lead-in's length (96) and the frozen block's three measures — the stimulus fires once, the sight 62, the sign 53 of 64 — and holds them to ADR-0077's pinned tables. A mismatch stops the round before any rewarded run and is reported as a defect, not an outcome.
- **The constants.** ADR-0076's stimulus (the two injections as pinned); the instrument's geometry, readout window (`WINDOW`, from tick 100 for 500 ticks), trial (`TRIAL_TICKS`, $2^{14}$), seed (`SEED`, 27), run (`TRIALS`, 512 trials in eight blocks) and reward magnitude (`REWARD_Q16`, 1.0); the gain 1.75 as the image holds it and the controller off.
- **The delivery.** After each trial, the addressed set the synapses from the presented stimulus's units onto its assigned readout's units, then a reward of 1.0, whatever the selection; consolidation where and when the engine does it — at an addressed synapse's next presynaptic spike, which [ADR-0068](0068-the-reward-addressed.md) found is the next presentation of that stimulus, or a background spike of its units. The dopamine signal accumulates (`NeuromodulatorState::reward` adds, saturating) and decays by `DOPAMINE_TAU_SHIFT` per tick, one time constant per trial, so under a reward every trial it approaches about 1.58 after a reward and 0.58 at a trial's end (a geometric sum of $e^{-1}$), and the addressed modulation is at the ceiling for a little under half of each trial and not below about 0.58. That is arithmetic from the two functions, not a reading; the round reads the signal from the record.
- **The arms.** Three runs of the same 512 trials from the same image, presenting the same stimulus at every trial (`Task::stimulus_at` reads the seed and the index only): **the assignment** (A onto readout 0, B onto readout 1); **the mirrored assignment** (A onto 1, B onto 0); and **the reward withheld**, which under the baseline at zero is the frozen run of 512 trials and the reference each rewarded arm is paired with trial by trial.
- **The criterion, both clauses in both rewarded arms:**
  1. **The couplings.** Each of the arm's two assigned pairs' excitatory coupling sums, read from the record after the 512th trial, is above the image's. Strict.
  2. **The response the engine reads.** Over the last 128 trials (`LAST_BLOCKS`), the trials in which the presented stimulus's assigned readout counted more spikes in the task's window (`Outcome::counts`) than it counted at the same trial of the withheld run number **at least 80** (`REWARDED_MIN`: by chance about once in 337 runs, `the_criterion_reads_as_written`); a tie counts against. The comparison is a sign test on paired trials: the same image, seed, drive and stimulus, so that the only difference between the two counts is what the reward consolidated and what that set in motion.

  H-13 is **yes** when both clauses hold in both rewarded arms, and **no** otherwise.
- **The readings, not criteria**, reported in both arms: the other readout's paired count by the same test (the delivery never reaches the presented stimulus's synapses onto it, so a rise there is the network's and not the delivery's); the selection toward the assigned readout over the last 128 trials beside 80, which reopens nothing of H-12; the assigned pairs' couplings and the trace's composition by ADR-0072's oracle block by block, so that whether the potentiation holds as the couplings rise is read rather than assumed; and the dopamine signal from the record.
- **The prediction, a Hypothesis written before the run.** The couplings clause holds in both arms: the trace is positive after 53 of the frozen block's 64 trials and every pair's sum after the block is positive (ADR-0077), and the delivery moves into the weight what the trace holds. For the response clause this ADR writes **no prediction**, and names the two mechanisms in the tree that bound the rise: the depression scales with the slot's magnitude (ADR-0055), so a rising coupling depresses more at each pairing, which is how ADR-0055 made a weight settle; and the rule reads the target's last spike only, so a readout that fires more — which is what a rising coupling makes it do — moves that spike later on both counts, shrinking the potentiation (farther after the previous presynaptic spike) and growing the depression (nearer this one). Whether the response rises past the count's own variation before those stop it is what the round measures.
- **H-13's stopping rule, written with it:**
  1. **One round** (brief 036): the calibration, the three arms, the criterion committed before the first rewarded run.
  2. **A calibration that does not reproduce stops the round** before any rewarded run; it is a defect, reported as a numbered finding, and not an outcome of H-13.
  3. **Yes**: H-13 is recorded yes with its scope — this network, this taught delivery, this regime, this rule. The next decision is an ADR on the reinforced form, a delivery the selection decides, on this network; it is not decided here.
  4. **No**: H-13 is recorded no with its scope, and the next decision is route 3, the review of the operating regime, by an ADR. The learning line spends no further round in this regime.
  5. **No constant moves after a rewarded run** — not the reward, the baseline, the run's length, the window, the mark or the arms — and there is no second attempt at H-13 in this configuration.

**The structural rule is deferred**, not rejected. ADR-0077 read that the couplings a synapse would be added to are 775 to 809 synapses per pair and that the response to a volley of 51 units is 8.5 to 9.9 spikes over 459 readout units: an added synapse changes the response's size and the census, not the sign of what the existing synapses pair, and nothing read says what count the response needs. It is a rule (§8.8), very likely a field in a record, and the largest build of the three; the round that proposes it brings the measured need first.

**The operating regime is recorded as an open question** in whitepaper §11.1, beside H-13, with ADR-0077's readings: the instrument's regime, the gain held at 1.75, reads 1.6 to 1.8 Hz per unit with the estimator at 0.0 to 0.45, sub-critical by the estimator's reading; the controller's, at ADR-0055's step, is a limit cycle at the estimator's ceiling with the gain alternating by a quarter every window and 12 to 40 Hz per unit, saturated; neither is critical by that reading, and H-8 says the lag-one slope is not a reading of $\sigma$ at this scale anyway. Which regime the network is built to hold is a question about ADR-0036 and ADR-0044 before it is one about a task. It becomes the next decision if H-13 is a no, and it stays open if H-13 is a yes.

### Consequences

- Good: the learning line continues with the question ADR-0077 made askable, under a criterion, a prediction and a stopping rule written before any run, so the line cannot drift the way H-12's did for six rounds before its rule existed.
- Good: whichever way H-13 reads, the next decision is already named — the reinforced form after a yes, the regime after a no.
- Good: the attribution is exact by construction: with the baseline at zero, the only weights that move are the ones the delivery reaches, and the withheld arm is the same trials with nothing moving.
- Neutral: the taught form is weaker than a task the engine solves by its own selection, and H-13 says so in its name; a yes is a claim about the rule's direction in this regime, not about learning a task.
- Bad: the structural rule and the regime review wait on H-13, and the regime question is the deeper one; the order is chosen for the size of the step, not for the depth of the question.
- Bad: brief 036's three runs of 512 trials after a lead-in of 96 windows cost about three times what ADR-0077's settled candidate cost in the weekly job, on a shard already at 41 per cent of its bound.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **ADR-0066's form** (option 2). The geometry makes the four stimulus–readout couplings equal within ten per cent by construction (`ROTATION_1024`, held against the census), so an existing asymmetry is a geometry or a coupling chosen for the task; and then "keep" asks nothing of the rule while "reverse" asks for a difference again.
- **A reward of one sign on H-12's task** (option 3). That is H-12's task with a constant moved after its rewarded runs — ADR-0069's own option 2, rejected there for that reason — and it would be judged by H-12's criterion, which the stopping rule has closed.
- **The reinforced form first** (option 4). It puts the selection's chance level into the delivery: under it a no could mean that the rule cannot move a response or that the selection seldom reached the synapses, and the round could not tell which. It is the rung after a yes.
- **The structural rule now** (option 5). Deferred above: a mechanism built without a measured need.
- **The regime review now** (option 6). Recorded above and deferred with a trigger: it is a review of two accepted ADRs rather than a round, and H-13's answer either makes it the next decision or leaves it open with one more reading behind it.
- **Brief 027's baseline of 0.5** in H-13's runs. Every synapse would consolidate half its trace every trial and the settling would continue under the task, so the couplings' change would not be the reward's.
- **Leaving the criterion to brief 036.** Every learning brief since 027 wrote its criterion before its run, and each held it; writing H-13's here, in a document the round cannot edit without a new ADR, is the stronger form of the same rule.
