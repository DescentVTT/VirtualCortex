# ADR-0044: The reference network — a seeded anatomical prior in `cortex-connectome`, the runtime's synthesis, a drive that is a function of the tick, and a causal branching-ratio oracle by perturbation of forks with attribution through the connectome; what the estimator, the controller and a night's replay do on it at 256 and 1 024 units; H-8 and H-9 dispositioned at that scale

## Context and Problem Statement

For four rounds the closing assessment named one blocking item: "a reference image with a traced run, which decides H-8, H-9 and whether a rewarded invention is ever read back". The tree held networks a test wires by hand (three units, 64, 128) and no rule that makes one from a seed; whitepaper §11.1's H-8 asked for "the estimate against a causal count from the trace" while `HomeostaticDrivePool::update_branching_ratio(descendants, ancestors)`, the causal form, had no caller that could attribute a spike to its cause; H-9 asked for episodes tagged from a network's own activity, a night, and a spiking readout. The proposal of brief 022 asked for a procedural connectome synthesizer at "$N \ge 10^3 \sim 10^5$ units" with laminar priors, a traced run comparing `update_branching_ratio_q16` (a name the tree does not have) against "the true causal branching ratio extracted from the spike trace", and a pattern-completion exit test after a night.

Four facts shaped the answer. A state crate declares no dependency (TC-2), so an anatomical prior in `cortex-connectome`, whose responsibility §5.2.2 already names, cannot write `cortex-core`'s records: it can yield synapses as integers, and the runtime can write them. A deterministic engine can attribute a spike to its cause exactly: two forks of one image under one drive that is a function of the tick differ only by the one kick one of them receives, so every spike in the perturbed fork and not in the baseline descends from that kick, and every spike in the baseline and not in the perturbed fork was moved or removed by it; with the kicked unit's synapses known, a descendant of the first generation is an extra spike of a target within a latency after that synapse's delay. A unit near its threshold fires on one message of 1.5 and, since its basal potential outlives its refractory window, may fire again on what is left; and in a driven network a kick mostly advances spikes the drive would have produced, so a gross count of descendants overstates what the kick added. And "reference scale" has two meanings: Appendix A's 43 million units, which no test can run, and the size a hypothesis is decided at, which the tree had never named.

## Decision Drivers

- ADR-0016's rule that a quantity has one owner, and TC-2: the prior in `cortex-connectome`, the synthesis, the drive and the forks in the runtime.
- §8.3: a run is `(image, seed, input trace)`; a drive that is a function of the tick and a seed is an input trace as a rule, and a fork of the image under it is the same run up to the kick.
- ADR-0036's "What is not claimed" and H-8: the estimator's regime is the reference population's; the round that measures decides whether the record needs a second line.
- ADR-0038's "What is not claimed" and H-9: a reference image, episodes tagged from its own activity, a night, a readout that is a spiking one.
- ADR-0010 and principle 3: every number below is the engine's own on a stated network at a stated size, pinned from one run and held on every worker count and architecture; what the same rules do at Appendix A's scale is a Target with the same generator.
- CI runs the tests in both profiles and the mutation gate reruns them per mutant: a window on a thousand units is 22 s in the debug profile on a developer machine, so the pull request's gate runs a reduced form of the harness at 256 units (one gain, one window, four kicks, a four-window loop from 2.0) and the weekly job runs the full sweep at 256 and at 1 024 units as ignored `exhaustive` tests.
- "Latest ≠ Newest": the ring lattice with rewiring (Watts and Strogatz 1998), the sparse excitatory-inhibitory network with a fifth inhibitory (Brunel 2000), the branching-ratio estimators (Wilting and Priesemann 2018), Knuth's generator (1997) and SplitMix64's finaliser (Steele, Lea and Flood 2014) are all older than the rule requires and have documented failure modes.

## Considered Options

1. The proposal: a synthesizer in `cortex-connectome` writing records, laminar and columnar priors, a "true" branching ratio read from the trace by a time window.
2. **A prior in `cortex-connectome` that yields synapses from one seeded walk (a ring lattice with a local window and a rewired fraction, every $k$-th unit inhibitory with negative weights, a local delay band for window synapses and a far one for rewired ones), the runtime's synthesis into the arenas, a drive that is a function of the tick, and an oracle that forks the image, kicks one unit, and attributes the extra spikes through the kicked unit's synapses, netting the spikes the kick advanced; the exit tests at 256 and 1 024 units, both scales' numbers recorded here.**
3. A prior as a record in the image (section kind 38, the laminar section the format names).
4. Attribution by a time window alone (the first generation as the extra spikes within twice the shortest delay).

## Decision Outcome

Option 2.

- **The prior** (`cortex-connectome`, module `prior`; no record). `Lcg` is Knuth's MMIX generator, the property kit's, with `below(n)` and `between(lo, hi)` by the product's high word. `Prior { units, inhibitory_every, synapses_per_unit, window, rewire_q0_8, delay_min, delay_max, far_delay_min, far_delay_max, weight_min, weight_max, inhibitory_gain_q4_4, apical_q0_8, seed }`; `is_well_formed` (two units or more, a window of at least one below the unit count, ordered non-empty bands, at least one synapse per unit), `is_inhibitory(unit)` (unit $i$ when $(i + 1) \bmod k = 0$). `synapses()` yields `Synapse { source, target, weight_q1_15, delay_ticks, apical }` unit by unit in slot order from one seeded walk, so a synapse's draw depends on every synapse before it: for each, a rewired one with probability `rewire_q0_8` of 256 takes a target uniform over the ring and a delay from the far band, any other a target within $\pm W$ (a place in $1..2W$, the first $W$ to the right) and a delay from the local band; a self-target is moved one place along; the weight is uniform in the excitatory band, and from an inhibitory source it is that times the gain in sixteenths, saturating at the width, negated; apical with probability `apical_q0_8` of 256. `census()` counts the inhibitory units and the synapses by kind (`Census { inhibitory_units, synapses, inhibitory_synapses, long_range, apical }`, a long-range synapse being one whose target is outside the window); `ring_distance` is the shorter way round. The inhibitory unit's flag is the reader's annotation: the dynamics read the weight's sign, which the 18-bit message carries (§5.2.1).
- **The synthesis** (the runtime, module `synthesis`). `synthesize(units, blocks, prior)` writes the prior into the two arenas (`Executor::arenas_mut` lends both): every unit at its base threshold with the short-term factors at rest and `FLAG_INHIBITORY` set where the prior says, its fan-out at block `unit × blocks_per_unit` with its blocks chained in order, every synapse in the slot the walk names; refused with nothing written for a malformed prior, a unit arena of another size, fewer blocks than `blocks_for`, or a delay in either band at the wheel's horizon; it returns the census of what it wrote. `Drive { every, messages, efficacy_q16, units, seed }` is a stationary external input: on every tick that is a multiple of `every`, `messages` basal messages of `efficacy_q16` into units drawn by `mix64` (SplitMix64's finaliser) of the seed, the tick and the message's index, injected between ticks; a function of the tick alone, with no state.
- **The oracle** (module `branching`). `Perturbation { unit, tick, messages, efficacy_q16 }` is a kick; `fork(image, config, drive, kicks, ticks)` decodes the image, runs it under the drive with every kick injected before its tick, and returns the sorted spike train, refused when a worker dropped a spike (`ForkError::Dropped`) or a kick is at a tick the fork had passed (`Passed`). `cascade(baseline, perturbed, unit, at, synapses, latency, advance)` reads what one kick caused: the ancestor is the kicked unit's first extra spike at or after the kick (`None` when the kick added none, in which case nothing is attributed); the ancestors are that spike and every later extra spike of the kicked unit (the kick firing it again); a first-generation descendant is an extra spike of a target within `latency` ticks after an ancestor spike plus the delay of a synapse to that target; it is advanced rather than added when the baseline has a spike of that unit within `advance` ticks after it that the perturbed fork lacks (paired once); the extra spikes not attributed and the missing spikes not paired are counted as the forks drifting apart. `Attribution::of(cascades)` sums the cascades with an ancestor; `branching_ratio_q16` is the gross ratio through the record's own causal rule, `update_branching_ratio(descendants, ancestors)`, which has its first caller with attribution; `net_branching_ratio_q16` is descendants less advanced over ancestors, rounded to nearest. The exit tests use a latency of 128 ticks, an advance of 512 (a basal time constant), a kick of one message of 1.5, and the drive's messages every tick.
- **The network the tests measure on** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/reference.rs`). The prior at 256 and at 1 024 units: every fifth unit inhibitory at the rail (`inhibitory_gain_q4_4` 255 over weights that saturate), 32 synapses per unit (eight blocks), a window of eight, a quarter rewired, local delays of 100 to 300 ticks (1 to 3 ms), far ones of 1 400 to 2 559 (14 to 25.6 ms), excitatory weights in [6 000, 12 000], no apical synapses, seed 22; the drive one message of 0.125 per 128 units every tick, seed 3; two workers, the production wheel. The census at 256: 51 inhibitory units, 8 192 synapses, 1 632 inhibitory. The prior is written and read back whole through the image, and a driven run of two bins is bit-identical on one and four workers at a quiescent point (a mailbox node's index is a position in its worker's pool, so the arenas are compared where the writer writes them).
- **What the estimator and the oracle read at fixed gains** (the modulation baseline at 0, so the weights do not move; two windows per gain; the estimate is Q16.16, the ratios in decimals):

  The ceiling is one spike per unit per bin: 8 192 spikes per window at 256 units, 32 768 at 1 024. At 2.25 the forks drift apart by more than a thousand spikes over three horizons at 1 024 units (`extra` 1 777, `missing` 1 795 over sixteen kicks), which is why the attribution goes through the synapses and not by time.
- **What the controller does** (a step of an eighth from a gain of 1.0, the estimate and the spikes per window): at 256 units the gain climbs an eighth per window while the network is silent or the slope reads below 1 (1.125, 1.266, 1.424, 1.602, 1.751, 1.969, 2.175, 2.447), the ninth window is at the ceiling (9 940 spikes, read as 16) and the gain falls to 2.141, the tenth is below it (4 643, a slope of 0) and the gain rises to 2.409; at 1 024 units the same climb reaches 2.445 at the ninth window, the tenth is at the ceiling (39 759) and the gain falls to 2.140, the eleventh below it (18 361, slope 0.207) and the gain rises to 2.352, the twelfth at the ceiling again (33 001) and the gain falls to 2.058. The loop turns at the ceiling, not at a slope of 1.
- **What a night does** (the modulation baseline at 1.0, the gain at 2.0, the step at 0, the sleep shift 5). Awake under the drive for two bins, then an experience: a cue of two messages of 1.25 into each of twelve excitatory neighbours (units 0 to 14 less the inhibitory 4, 9 and 14), which fires them together; a third bin under the drive; quiet until quiescent. The cluster is tagged (a tag of 200), and a random pattern of twelve excitatory units spaced across the ring; the record is put to slow-wave sleep at a pressure of 1.0 through the image; the night runs three windows of slow-wave sleep, two of REM, two more of slow-wave sleep and wakes (376 replays of the two episodes in turn, 128 depotentiations, sixty-four to each episode, whose tags of 200 end at 136). At 256 units the cluster holds 147 synapses among itself, at 1 024 units 143; the random pattern 5 and 2. Every synapse among either pattern ends at the rail (32 767; from a mean of about 8 700 and 8 000). The readout is a cue of six units on a fork with no drive, over two horizons: on the image before the night the cued six fire and no other unit (18 spikes); on the image after it the cluster's other six fire and no other unit (39 spikes); the random pattern's other six never fire, before or after, at either size. A local delay band of 300 to 600 ticks fires two of six (a probe, not a pinned test) and one of 1 400 to 2 559 none: completion needs the cued units' messages to land within a basal time constant of one another.
- **What is decided.** *H-8 at 256 and 1 024 units under this prior:* the lag-one slope of one window of thirty-two bins is not a reading of $\sigma$ (it swings between 0.032 and 0.560 at one gain); the causal ratios are of the slope's order and share no trend with the gain (at 1 024 units the gross ratio rises, 0.53, 0.64, 1.00, while the net does not, 0.44, 0.34, 0.48; at 256 units both dip at 2.0, gross 0.39, 0.30, 0.58 and net 0.39, 0.20, 0.31; the slope's per-window values, 0.00 to 0.74, bracket every net ratio and every gross one but the 1.00); the population reaches the ceiling before either the slope or the net causal ratio reaches 1, so the controller's fixed point at this scale is the ceiling, around which it oscillates by an eighth per window. What the estimator reads at Appendix A's scale is a Target with this generator; a smoothed estimate across windows would need a second line of the record, which ADR-0037 left without reserved bytes (an open question in §11.1). *H-9 at 256 and 1 024 units under this prior:* a night's replay drives every synapse among a tagged pattern to the rail, and a cue of half a pattern completes the rest when the pattern's synapses are many and their delays fall within a basal time constant (a local cluster of twelve: 147 and 143 synapses), and not when they are few (a random pattern of twelve: 5 and 2) or their delays are long. The transfer the literature describes is the first case; whether a pattern a network's own activity produces is of that kind is the round that tags from the trace.
- **A review pass** (probes in a scratch clone after every gate was green; a fuzz of 3 000 random arenas through the proof search, the pair walk and the search; the drive's mixing over $2^{17}$ ticks; every number of the tables against the tests) found four behaviour defects, fixed before the merge: `cascade` paired a descendant only with the first missing spike in range, paired or not (now the first unpaired one; no pinned number moved); `fork` ran past `ticks` for a kick beyond the run (now `Unreached`); `Drive::unit_at` shifted the message index into sixteen bits, so indices 65 536 apart drew the same unit (now a rotation, a bijection); a local target on a ring of more than $2^{31}$ units wrapped before the remainder (now widened). It also found the claims this decision now states as they are: the gate's reduced form, the ratios' lack of a shared trend with the gain, the tags after the night, a stale time-window sentence in the oracle's module doc, and a `synthesize` that could have written part of a slice larger than a chain index names before refusing (now refused first).
- **Not adopted.** *Option 1's record-writing synthesizer in `cortex-connectome`*: TC-2. *Laminar and columnar priors*: the laminar sheet stays Specified with section kind 38; the ring lattice is the prior a measurement asked for. *Option 3*: a record the loop never reads (F-30's class). *Option 4*: a time window cannot tell the first generation from the forks' drift once the delay bands mix, and it counted the kicked unit's second spike as a descendant. *A change to the estimator, the ceiling, the bin or the sleep constants*: one prior and two sizes are what the measurement holds; a second prior that shows the slope reading $\sigma$ somewhere below the ceiling is the measurement a change would need. *The multistep regression*: the record has no line for its sums; the open question says so.

### Consequences

- Good: the blocking item of four rounds is a rule and two tests; a network of any size the machine holds is one prior and a seed, and the same generator is the Target's at Appendix A's scale.
- Good: the causal form has a caller, and the estimator has a measurement beside it instead of a hypothesis.
- Good: no record, no field, no format bump; the pin holds.
- Bad: the pull request's gate holds a reduced form at 256 units (at a gain of 2.0 one window's estimate of 0.659 over 3 156 spikes; four kicks, eleven ancestor spikes, five descendants of which two advanced, a gross ratio of 0.455 and a net one of 0.273; the loop from 2.0 at 2.085, 2.310 and 2.553, the ceiling at the fourth window with 11 846 spikes and the gain down to 2.234); the full sweep's numbers at both sizes are the weekly job's, and a change that moves them is seen a week later unless the round runs the ignored tests.
- Bad: the oracle's forks replay the run from the image, so a kick at tick $t$ costs a run to $t$; a fork of a running engine would need a copy of the wheels and the pools, which the runtime does not have.
- Bad: the estimator's noise and the ceiling's role are recorded, not resolved.
- Good, by accident: the exit tests are the first to construct the production wheel geometry (`Executor<2048>`), and they found that the wheel builder's stack reservation of twice the wheel overflowed on Linux in the debug profile (finding F-34); the builder now reserves eight wheels and a megabyte, and every CI target constructs the production geometry from now on.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **A stateful drive** (a generator advanced each tick): a fork would have to replay it from the start anyway; a function of the tick needs no state and no replay.
- **A kick of three strong messages** (the tests' kick): it fires a unit near its threshold three or four times; one message of 1.5 fires it once or twice, and the cascade counts every one.
- **Two priors, one per hypothesis**: one network for both is what "a reference image" meant; the delay band that completion needs is the local one both use.

## Confirmation

`cortex-connectome`: the walk's order, count and determinism; a local synapse within the window with a local delay, a rewired one anywhere with a far delay; the census; every bound of the prior with its neighbours; the inhibitory magnitude at the width and at zero gain; `ring_distance` both ways round and modulo the ring; the generator's draws in range. The runtime: the synthesis unit by unit against the walk, its four refusals with nothing written; the drive's schedule, its range and its refusal at a full ring; the cascade's attribution through the synapses, the second ancestor spike, the arrival tick excluded, a missing spike paired once; the attribution's sums and both ratios with their roundings; `tests/reference.rs` as above, the `exhaustive` pair at 1 024 units. The mutation gate on the changed lines (ADR-0030) passes in CI.
