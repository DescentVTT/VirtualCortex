# ADR-0074: A stimulus that fires each unit once — the one change F-46 names, tried as one message at the unit's threshold, then one message of 1.25, then the midpoint between them, each read over a frozen run at 1 024 units and the present gain by a measure written first that cannot see the answer; the executor scales an injected message by the synaptic gain as it scales a synapse's, so the two messages of 1.25 arrive as 4.375 and fire a unit at rest twice (finding F-47), and one message fires a unit at rest once or not at all but never every unit once under the drive's standing potential, whose spread is wider than the one-spike band: no candidate fires every unit once and adds under a tenth of a spike after, the trace stays net depression under each (the sign 2, 1 and 0 of 64; ADR-0072's hypothesis of +5 per synapse per trial is not met, the terms reading −3.7, −5.5 and −4.7), so there is no rewarded run and no constant moves; what the criterion requires is read off the trials for the first time — one to three spikes per trial on the answer readout, the size the delivery moves and in the wrong direction — and what remains is named and not built

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0072](0072-what-the-trace-is-made-of.md) measured what three decisions had inferred and found the instrument's stimulus firing each of its units about three times within one pair window of the volley — once before the readout window opens, which is what [ADR-0065](0065-the-instrument-recalibrated.md)'s calibration counted, and 2.06 more inside the pair window after it — and named that as finding F-46, because the pair rule enters a depression at every presynaptic spike and those spikes are most of the background's depression on a stimulus's synapses into the readouts and all of the volley's own. It wrote what removing them would meet, as a Hypothesis from its terms: the volley's depression "to nothing", the terms summing "to about +5 per synapse per trial, net potentiation at a gain at which the readout sees". And it closed the gain as a handle: below 1.75 the readout stops seeing before the sign passes.

Brief 033 asks for the one change F-46 names and nothing else: a stimulus that fires each unit once inside the pair window, from candidates fixed in a fixed order, picked by a measure that reads no selection, reward or outcome; the composition re-read under it against the +5, named as the Hypothesis it is; the sight and the sign asked again at 1.75, which does not move; and, only if both pass, [ADR-0069](0069-the-addressed-reward-measured.md)'s criterion with [ADR-0068](0068-the-reward-addressed.md)'s delivery. It also asks, for the first time in five rounds, what the criterion requires: the instrument's bias, the offset the criterion needs and what the delivery moved, three integers read off the counts the runs record, in every branch of the round.

Three facts of the tree shaped the reading. The executor scales every input of a unit by the tick's synaptic gain ([ADR-0036](0036-criticality-control.md); `scaled` in `executor.rs`, one factor per turn over the summed batch), and the injected stimulus is an input like a synapse's message, so at the present gain of 1.75 the stimulus of two messages of 1.25 arrives as 4.375 of basal drive, not the 2.5 [ADR-0038](0038-episodic-ledger-and-replay.md) described as the middle of the band that fires a unit at its base threshold exactly once. The membrane's basal compartment has a time constant of 512 ticks and its refractory window is 200 ([ADR-0018](0018-membrane-integration.md)): what a drive leaves in the dendrite when the window ends can fire the unit again, which is what ADR-0038 read at 3.0 and named as the band's edge. And the instrument's drive delivers a message of 0.125 — 0.219 after the gain — to a unit every 128 ticks on average, so a unit of the presented set is not at rest when the stimulus arrives: it carries a standing basal potential of the order of the threshold with a spread of the same order, and one message adds the same amount to every unit whatever it carries.

## Decision Drivers

- The brief's standing directives: no mechanism, no constant of the engine, no dependency; the gain does not move and the ladder is not walked; nothing chosen after a rewarded run; every claim Implemented, Specified, Target or Hypothesis; every number an arithmetic oracle can produce computed by that oracle first.
- Latest ≠ Newest (§2.1): the round changes how many messages an existing harness injects and at what efficacy, a constant of the instrument, and reads two measures that exist. Nothing is adopted.
- Principle 1: the repository wins over the document. The membrane oracle written apart from the tree for this round disagreed with the engine on a unit at rest, and the engine's own probe is what the decision cites; where a document says the replay drive fires a unit once, the tree's number at the gain is recorded as a finding.
- Principle 2: the sentence "fires each once" was written from one window and read wrong; this round reads the stimulus in every window it fires in, before any rewarded run.
- [ADR-0061](0061-the-learning-runs-leave-the-gate.md), [ADR-0067](0067-the-weekly-dispatch-has-a-scope.md) and [ADR-0073](0073-the-whole-domain-tests-sharded.md): the runs are weekly `exhaustive` tests in the `instrument.rs` shard, whose time against its bound the round states; the gate gains one test running no whole run; the round dispatches `scope=both`.

## Considered Options

1. **The candidates in the brief's order, each a frozen run of sixty-four trials at 1.75 read by the sight, the sign and a measure written first; the first that fires once is the stimulus; none, and there is no rewarded run and the candidates are recorded with their censuses.**
2. Pick the candidate whose sign is best, whatever the measure reads.
3. A stimulus of two injections, a drive and then a message that cancels what the refractory window leaves: a change to the task's trial, which injects once before its first tick.
4. A candidate below (a) until the after clause holds: a search, which the brief forbids.

### Consequences

- Good: the one change F-46 named is measured, in three shapes, and the answer is a number either way: one message cannot fire every unit once under this drive, and the trace stays net depression under a stimulus that fires each unit 1.3 times where it fired 3.
- Good: ADR-0072's Hypothesis is read against a run and not met, by a stated margin, with the reason: the volley's potentiation falls with the response it was paired with, and the background's depression is not all the after-spikes.
- Good: what the criterion requires is a number for the first time — one to three spikes per trial — beside what a delivery moves and the instrument's own bias, so a next round can say what size of effect it is after before it builds anything.
- Good: the executor's scaling of injected messages is a finding with the engine's own probe behind it, and a stated reach into ADR-0038's replay that a sleep round can read.
- Neutral: the gate's runtime tests gain one run of eight trials at 1 024 units and four probes of one pair window; the weekly job gains one test of three frozen runs.
- Bad: no rewarded run, again; H-12 does not move, and the expectation written for a positive trace stays untested.
- Bad: the stimulus that fires once does not exist as one message on this instrument; the document's sentence is replaced by a measurement and not by a stimulus.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Option 2**: above.
- **Option 3**: a change to the task's trial, and a second injection whose timing is a constant chosen against the membrane's time constants; a round that needs it should measure that the sign follows.
- **Option 4**: the lesson of six rounds.
- **A criterion clause on the after-count**: the measure is a calibration, not a criterion; H-12 is about a behaviour.
