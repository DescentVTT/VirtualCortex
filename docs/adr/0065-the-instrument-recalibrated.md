# ADR-0065: The instrument recalibrated — a set as a periodic pattern of units so that a stimulus is units spaced beyond the prior's local window and fires each once, a readout window derived from the prior's delay band, a trial of one time constant of the dopamine signal, and a calibration that shows the readout sees the stimulus before any reward is given, with the weights frozen; at 256 units the gain of 1.75 fails and 2.0 passes, at 1 024 units 1.75 passes first; every constant committed before the first rewarded run and the image format unchanged at 14

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0060](0060-the-reward-path-measured.md) answered brief 027's question with a no and located the no in the instrument rather than in the rule: the readout counted a whole trial of background against a stimulus whose spikes land within a few milliseconds of the volley; a contiguous stimulus quarter reverberated through its own window, 3.4 spikes per unit per presentation, so that its synapses onto a readout paired as depression under every feedback; and a trial of $2^{12}$ ticks let the dopamine signal of five trials sum. It named four changes and built none: a readout over the sub-window in which the stimulus's spikes land, a stimulus that fires its set once, a trial at or beyond the dopamine window, and a quieter gain. Brief 029 asked for the four to be built as one instrument, calibrated by a measure that cannot see the answer, with every constant written or derived or picked before the first rewarded run, so that the question of [ADR-0066](0066-the-reward-path-measured-again.md) means more than ADR-0060's did: if the calibration passes, a no is about the rule on this task, not about the readout.

Three facts of the tree shaped the instrument. `Set { first, len }` of [ADR-0059](0059-a-task-a-readout-and-a-reward.md) was contiguous, and a stimulus whose units are nine or more places apart on a ring whose local window is eight ([ADR-0044](0044-reference-network.md)'s prior: `place = between(1, 2 × window)`) is not a contiguous set, nor is a readout that surrounds every stimulus unit with equal numbers of its own units on either side. `Readout::count` counted from the trial's start to its end and stopped at the first entry before the trial, so a window inside the trial needed a count that passes over the entries after it. And the executor consolidates every block's traces under `clamp(baseline + dopamine, 0, 1)` (`cortex-neuromod`'s `modulation`, [ADR-0032](0032-three-factor-plasticity.md)): with the baseline at zero and no reward, no weight of either polarity moves, which is what lets a calibration read the prior's own response to a stimulus without teaching it anything.

## Decision Drivers

- The standing directive of brief 029, brief 024's lesson ([ADR-0051](0051-the-estimator-at-4096-units.md)), brief 025's ([ADR-0054](0054-the-causal-count-inside-the-loop.md)) and brief 027's ([ADR-0060](0060-the-reward-path-measured.md)): nothing is chosen after a rewarded run. Every constant here is written in the brief, derived by a rule from the prior's parameters, or picked by the calibration from candidates listed before it ran, and the commit that holds them precedes the first that holds a rewarded run's outcome (`4f2da6a` before `1780b38`).
- [ADR-0016](0016-thirty-two-crate-architecture.md): every quantity has one owner. The selection stays `cortex-basal-ganglia`'s, the modulation `cortex-neuromod`'s, the trace `cortex-core`'s, the task and its sets the runtime's. No new crate, no record changes, the format stays 14.
- Principle 5: a mis-read refused at construction beats one detected in a reading. A set shape that could name a unit twice, a window that ends after the trial or holds no tick, and a count that could reach the drive's width are refused by name before a trial runs.
- Latest ≠ Newest (§2.1): the rule is ADR-0032's, unchanged; the selection is the linear gate of Gurney, Prescott and Redgrave 2001; the four changes are experimental design (a count in a window, a sparse stimulus, a trial matched to the reinforcement signal's time constant, a quieter background), not a new mechanism, and no dependency enters.

## Considered Options

1. **`Set` as a periodic pattern: `first`, `period`, `mask`, `count`, one shape for the contiguous run, the lattice and the interleaved roles; `Task` gains a `Window` of the trial; the geometry a rule of the prior's parameters with a rotation the census picks; the window a rule of the delay band; a calibration at two gains in a fixed order with a pass mark written first.**
2. `Set { first, len, stride }`, a lattice only. It names a stimulus of spaced units but not a readout that is the odd places of a period less one, so the readouts would have been strided sets that see a stimulus unit on one side only, or the union of two.
3. A second type beside `Set` for the sparse shape, with `Stimulus` and `Readout` generic over both. Two membership tests and two refusal paths for one quantity.
4. A readout window measured from a run (the ticks in which the response was seen), or a gain chosen from the rewarded run's first block. Both choose a constant from what they are about to measure.

## Decision Outcome

Option 1.

- **The set** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/src/task.rs`). `Set { first, period, mask, count }` names the units `first + k × period + b` for every `k` below `count` and every offset `b` below `period` whose bit of `mask` is set, in that order: a contiguous run is a period of one (`Set::contiguous(first, len)`, ADR-0059's literal), a lattice of one unit every stride is a period of the stride with a mask of one bit, and several roles interleaved in one period are one mask each over the same `first` and `period`. `is_well_formed` asks for a period in `1..=MAX_PERIOD` (32, the mask's width), a mask with a bit, and no bit at or beyond the period, which would name a unit of the next period twice; `len` is `count × popcount(mask)`, `end` one past the last unit, widened; `contains` reads the offset from `first` in period and place; `units()` walks the set in order and walks nothing for a set the rule refuses; `overlaps` tests the smaller set's units against the other's membership. `TaskError::MalformedSet` refuses a malformed set before `EmptySet`; `SetOutsideArena` and `SetsOverlap` read the new shape through `end` and `overlaps`. `Stimulus::inject` walks `units()`.
- **The window.** `Window { from, ticks }` is the sub-window of a trial the readout counts, `Window::whole(ticks)` the trial itself; `Task` carries one; `EmptyWindow` and `WindowOutsideTrial` refuse a window of no ticks or one whose end (widened) is past the trial. `Readout::count_window(train, start, ticks)` scans the train from its newest entry back, passes over the entries after the window and stops at the first before it; the side an entry is on is the sign of its wrapping distance from `start` read as `i32`, so the train's span must be below $2^{31}$ ticks (§8.4). On a train read at the trial's end with the whole trial as the window it is `count`, which stays as ADR-0059 wrote it with its contract and its test; `Task::trial` counts through `count_window` from `start + window.from`, and `CountBeyondWidth`'s bound is the window's, one spike per refractory interval of the window per unit, since the count is the window's. Brief 027's harness names the same quarters through `Set::contiguous` and the whole trial through `Window::whole`; the gate's first block of ADR-0060 reruns unchanged, and every number of ADR-0060 stands.
- **The geometry** (`runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/instrument.rs`, the block written before the run), a rule of the prior's window of eight and its inhibitory rule (every fifth unit, from the fifth). The ring is read in periods of twenty places from a rotation: stimulus A is the first place of every period and stimulus B the twelfth, so that every stimulus unit is nine or eleven places from its nearest neighbours in either stimulus, beyond the window (a presentation fires each once through no local synapse, and neither stimulus drives the other through one); the period is a multiple of five and the rotation is not three or four more than a multiple of five, so no stimulus unit is one the prior makes inhibitory; readout 0 is the odd places but the twelfth and readout 1 the even places but the first, nine each, so that every stimulus unit has four units of either readout on either side of it at mirrored distances, and every place of a period is in exactly one set. A compile-time assertion holds each of these on the four masks. The rotation at a size is the first place from which the four excitatory couplings of the prior (A→R0, A→R1, B→R0, B→R1, ADR-0060's definition: the sum of the weights an excitatory unit of the one sends to a unit of the other), read from the synthesized arena before any run, are equal within ten per cent (the largest at most eleven tenths of the smallest) and none zero. At 256 units it is 17, eleven whole periods (units 17 to 236; eleven units per stimulus, ninety-nine per readout; the couplings 1 487 772, 1 409 916, 1 458 815 and 1 477 095, within 5.5 per cent); rotations 0 to 16 all fail, six of them with a stimulus on the inhibitory lattice, whose couplings are zero. At 1 024 units it is 0, fifty-one periods (fifty-one and 459; 6 986 739, 7 212 710, 7 146 878 and 7 257 384, within 3.9 per cent). `the_geometry_holds_against_the_census_at_both_sizes` holds (a), (b) and (c) at the written rotation, that every smaller rotation fails, and that the task fits the executor for every feedback and both assignments; it is a gate test.
- **The readout window**, a rule of the prior's local delay band (100 to 300 ticks, 1 to 3 ms): it opens `delay_min` ticks after the stimulus is injected, before which no local synapse of the volley can have landed, and closes `2 × delay_max` after it, leaving the volley's own latency and the readout unit's as much again as the band's longest delay: ticks 100 to 600 of the trial, the same for both readouts and every trial, never adjusted from a run. The far band (1 400 to 2 559 ticks) lies outside it. The window before the injection, the calibration's comparison, is the same 500 ticks ending at the injection; the first trial is preceded by a lead-in of one window under the drive with no stimulus.
- **The trial** is $2^{14}$ ticks (164 ms), one time constant of the dopamine signal (`DOPAMINE_TAU_SHIFT`, asserted at compile time), so that the signal one outcome left has decayed to $e^{-1}$ of itself at the next trial's end; a quarter of the eligibility trace's window. The block is 64, the run 512 ($2^{23}$ ticks at either size, the size of brief 027's 1 024-unit runs), the baseline 0.5, the reward 1.0, the stimulus two messages of 1.25, the prior's seed 22 and the trials' 27, as brief 027 had them.
- **The calibration, before any rewarded run.** At each size and for each candidate gain, 1.75 then 2.0 (ADR-0044's table: five to six times quieter at 1.75), one run of sixty-four trials with the stimuli in the task's order, the modulation baseline at zero and no reward, so that no weight of either polarity moves (asserted: the sums by polarity after the run equal the prior's). The measure, per trial: the two readouts' spikes in the window after the volley against their spikes in the window before the injection. A gain passes when the window after held more readout spikes than the window before in at least fifty-six of the sixty-four trials, and each readout's total after exceeds its total before; the first candidate that passes is the gain at that size, the order fixed so that no preference chooses. **At 256 units, 1.75 fails and 2.0 passes:** at 1.75 the window after held more in 50 trials (148 and 181 spikes against 54 and 51 over the block), below the mark; at 2.0 in 58 (481 and 516 against 231 and 249). The gain at 256 units is 2.0. **At 1 024 units, 1.75 passes first:** 62 trials (775 and 741 against 255 and 241); 2.0, tried second, would have passed too (63; 2 119 and 2 094 against 1 293 and 1 215). The gain at 1 024 units is 1.75. The presented set fires one spike per unit in the ticks before the window opens, within two spikes, at both sizes and both gains (10.9 per presentation for eleven units; 50.8 for fifty-one), the reading ADR-0060's 3.4 per unit asked for. Both calibrations are weekly `exhaustive` tests pinned block for block with their traces; the gate holds that the picked gains are the first candidates that passed and that the measure reads as written at its edges.
- **The constants commit.** `4f2da6a` holds the whole block: the geometry and its rotations, the window, the gains the calibration picked, the trial, the block, the run, the baseline, the reward, the seeds and ADR-0066's criterion with its counts; `1780b38`, the first commit that holds a rewarded run's outcome, follows it.
- **Tests** (`task.rs`): the periodic shape's units, lengths, ends and overlaps by hand (two masks over one period interleave without a shared unit; a mask bit at the period is refused; a period of 32 takes every bit; a lattice's count is its units', not its span's); every new refusal at its edge; `count_window` over the hand-built train across the tick wrap, with entries after the window passed over and the whole trial equal to `count`; a trial whose window is a sub-window counts the spikes in it and no other; a stimulus over a lattice fires the lattice's units. The lattice property over `testkit/prop.rs`: over 2 000 seeded well-formed sets, the units a set walks are exactly the ones its membership test names, ascending and once, with `end`, `len`, `is_empty` and `overlaps` against brute force; and over the seeded trains of ADR-0059's property, `count_window` over a seeded sub-window is the filter's, with entries after it in the train.
- **What the mutation gate found.** Recorded in [ADR-0066](0066-the-reward-path-measured-again.md) with the round's evidence.
- **Not adopted.** *A stride alone* (option 2): the readout it admits sees a stimulus unit on one side only, and the balance (b) needs both. *A window measured from a run* (option 4): the rule of the band is written once and holds at both sizes; a measured one would be the run's. *A third candidate gain, or a gain between the two*: the candidates are the brief's; at 256 units the quieter one fails by six trials and the louder one passes, at 1 024 both pass, and the order picks. *Excluding the inhibitory units from the readouts*: a readout is a set of places, and the prior's inhibitory units fall on either readout alike (one in five of each, by the period's multiple of five); their spikes are counted as ADR-0060 counted them.

### Consequences

- Good: the instrument is a rule of the prior's parameters checked against its census before any run, and its one free choice, the gain, is picked by a measure that reads no selection, reward or outcome, in an order fixed first; whatever ADR-0066 reads through it is a statement about the rule on this task at these sizes, not about a readout that could not see.
- Good: the calibration passed at both sizes, and read that a sparse stimulus fires its set once, that the readout window holds the stimulus's response above the background in 58 and 62 trials of 64 with the weights frozen, and that the quieter gain is the one that sees at 1 024 units and not at 256.
- Good: `Set` is one shape with one membership test and one refusal path for the contiguous run, the lattice and the interleaved roles; `count` and every number of ADR-0060 stand.
- Neutral: `Task` gains a field, so every task names its window; ADR-0059's tests and brief 027's harness name the whole trial, mechanically.
- Bad: the calibration measures the prior's response with the weights frozen; what the pair rule does to that response once the weights move is ADR-0066's reading, not this decision's, and at 256 units it is the reading that matters most.
- Bad: at 256 units the pattern covers 220 of 256 units from the rotation the census picked, and the sixteen places at the ring's start and the twenty at its end are in no set; the couplings (c) are equal within 5.5 per cent there against 3.9 at 1 024, the smaller sample's price.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Option 2**: above.
- **Option 3**: a second type is a second membership test for one quantity and a second path through every refusal; the periodic shape names both with one.
- **Option 4**: the lesson of three rounds.
- **A contiguous stimulus of fewer units**, ADR-0060's other suggestion for a set that fires once: a run of two or more neighbours pairs among itself through the local window; a run of one is a lattice of one period.
- **A readout window that also covers the far band** (100 to 2 600 ticks): 25 ms of background, five times the local window's, for synapses that land uniformly over the ring and so add the same to both readouts by construction.
