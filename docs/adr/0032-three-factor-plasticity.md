# ADR-0032: Three-factor plasticity — an eligibility trace per synapse in the block, consolidated into the weight by the modulator at the presynaptic spike; the apical mask in the chain word; the engine's modulator in the executor and the image; format version 11

## Context and Problem Statement

Whitepaper §8.8 carried one row labelled Specified with an equation, a reference and a record to hold it: "Three-factor plasticity | `cortex-neuromod` | $\Delta W = \eta \cdot e_{ij} \cdot M$, with an eligibility trace $e_{ij}$ and modulator $M$ from the neuromodulator record" (Frémaux and Gerstner 2016; Izhikevich 2007 for the distal-reward problem it solves). [ADR-0022](0022-synapse-fan-out-and-stdp.md) had implemented the pair rule with the weight moved at the presynaptic spike and had left "an eligibility trace per synapse" to "the three-factor rule's question", noting that a per-synapse record quadruples the arena. `NeuromodulatorState` held a dopamine signal that `reward` moved and `decay_dopamine` relaxed ([ADR-0027](0027-vocal-synthesis-and-computational-humor.md)) and that nothing read. Brief 017 asked for the rule as an integer rule with the record, the composition in the executor, persistence and an exit test, under the standing constraints: 64-byte records, no allocation in the loop, every arithmetic operation by name, a run defined by `(image, seed, trace)`, and the determinism pin of [ADR-0030](0030-verification-governance.md) moved only with a stated reason.

Three facts shaped the answer. `SynapseBlock` had seven reserved bytes and four Q1.15 weights, so four Q1.15 traces need one more byte than the reserved ones. No reverse index exists (which synapses land on a unit), so anything per synapse happens where the block is: at the presynaptic spike, in the fan-out phase, by the worker that owns the block. And the reference rule integrates $e \cdot M$ continuously, which an engine that touches a block only when its unit fires cannot do.

## Decision Drivers

- §8.8's row and reference 5: the rule that is missing is the trace-and-modulator form, not a gain on STDP.
- The record's width (L-1, TC-3): the trace must fit the block, at the weight's precision, or the STDP window's shape is lost in the rounding.
- §8.3 and [ADR-0030](0030-verification-governance.md): a run is `(image, seed, trace)` and the pin holds unless a decision moves it and says why. The reference runs carry no reward, so the rule with the modulator at its default must be ADR-0022's rule, exactly where the two can agree.
- §8.1: every operation on a state field saturates or wraps by name; a decay must reach zero exactly (the shift-decay stall that [ADR-0018](0018-membrane-integration.md) and [ADR-0027](0027-vocal-synthesis-and-computational-humor.md) each met once).
- The mutation gate: every branch the rule takes needs a test at its boundary, and a comparison no input can distinguish is removed rather than excluded.
- [ADR-0031](0031-policy-amendment.md): a parameter the engine may amend by itself changes what it costs, never what it does. The modulation changes what it does, so nothing here joins the registry.

## Considered Options

1. A per-synapse 16-byte record with its own stamp and trace (ADR-0022's option 3).
2. A four-bit trace per slot in the seven reserved bytes.
3. **Four Q1.15 traces at `[56..64)` of the block, the apical mask moved into the four bits of the chain word that no block index can reach; the pairing amount into the trace, the trace decayed between presynaptic spikes, and a fraction of it consolidated into the weight at the presynaptic spike, with the fraction named by the modulator and the consolidated part taken out of the trace.**
4. The modulator as a gain on the pairing amount ($\Delta W = M \cdot \text{STDP}(\Delta t)$), with no trace.

## Decision Outcome

Option 3.

- **The record** (`cortex-core`, format version 11). `SynapseBlock` `[56..64)` is `eligibility_q1_15: [i16; 4]`, the eligibility trace per slot, Q1.15 signed, saturating. `[32..36)` is the chain word `chain`: bits 0–27 hold the next block index + 1 (`CHAIN_END` is 0; `CHAIN_MASK`), bits 28–31 the apical mask (bit 28 + $k$ for slot $k$). A block index is bounded to 26 bits by the wheel token ([ADR-0022](0022-synapse-fan-out-and-stdp.md), finding F-23) and the loader refuses more blocks, so 28 bits hold every index a loader accepts; `link` refuses an index above `MAX_CHAIN_INDEX` ($2^{28} - 2$) and keeps the mask, `unlink` keeps the mask, `set_synapse` and `clear_synapse` keep the chain and zero the slot's trace, `next_encoded` is the low bits. A version-10 image's mask at byte 56 would read as a trace: the version check refuses it.
- **The rule** (`cortex-core`). `step_stdp(slot, t, q)` is ADR-0022's pair rule into the trace: the trace gains $A_+ (1 - 2^{-11})^{q - p}$ when the target fired after the previous presynaptic spike and loses $A_- (1 - 2^{-11})^{t - q}$ when it fired before this one, saturating; the weight is untouched. `decay_eligibility(elapsed)` multiplies every trace by $(1 - 2^{-16})^{\text{elapsed}}$ (`ELIGIBILITY_TAU_SHIFT` 16, about 655 ms at 10 µs; Izhikevich uses 1 s; the largest shift `stp_decay_factor_q16` resolves), rounded to nearest, and by at least one LSB toward zero when elapsed is not zero, so a trace reaches zero exactly. `consolidate(slot, m)` clamps $m$ to $[0, 1]$ (`MODULATION_ONE_Q16`, Q16.16), moves $\operatorname{round}(e \cdot m)$ into the weight, saturating in $[-1, 1)$, and takes **what the weight absorbed** out of the trace: the weight and the trace conserve their sum, and a weight at the rail keeps its pending change in the trace, where it decays. `step_stdp_all(now, posts)` decays by the ticks since the block's stamp (a block with no spike on record has held its traces since tick 0, as `ticks_since_spike` counts), pairs every slot, then stamps; `consolidate_all(m)` follows it.
- **The one result that differs from ADR-0022.** At $m = 1$ the whole trace is consolidated at the spike that paired it, so the timing is ADR-0022's; but the pairing's two terms sum in the trace before the weight saturates, where ADR-0022 saturated after each, and what the rail cannot absorb stays pending in the trace (bounded by the trace's own saturation at 1.0) where ADR-0022 discarded it. At a weight of 1.0 a pairing of $+257$ and $-129$ left $32\,767 - 129$; it now leaves $32\,767$ with 128 pending, and the pending part is consolidated later, so the two histories differ from that pairing on, not only at the rail. For a weight that never comes within one pairing of a rail the two agree bit for bit (the crate's tests hold the old values through `pair`, the pairing followed by a consolidation at 1.0; a reviewer's replay of 400 random histories agreed at every step while the weight stayed at least 344 from both rails, and diverged permanently after a rail was hit).
- **The pin.** The reference network of `runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/differential.rs` (`wire_random`, weights drawn from $[8\,000, 32\,000)$, staying within $[8\,180, 31\,986]$ over the run, so never at a rail) keeps ADR-0022's dynamics bit for bit; its arena hash moved because the block's bytes changed (the apical mask of 77 of its 128 blocks moved from byte 56 into the chain word, and the trace occupies the old mask's bytes), and its spike count did not. The test restates the pin with this reason. (The brief's context had said the pinned network wires every weight at the rail; that is the un-pinned ring of the same file.)
- **The modulator** (`cortex-neuromod`). `modulation(baseline)` is `baseline + dopamine_rpe`, saturating, clamped to $[0, 1]$: 1.0 consolidates everything at once, 0 nothing, a positive reward-prediction error raises it, a negative one lowers it, and a dip below zero is clamped (no anti-Hebbian reversal; Izhikevich 2007). `DOPAMINE_TAU_SHIFT` (14; a time constant of $2^{14}$ ticks, 164 ms) is the per-tick decay the executor applies; `decay_dopamine`'s one-LSB floor makes the tail linear, so a reward of 1.0 is at half after 137 ms and at rest after about 460 ms. The record encodes and decodes its 16 bytes, is `Default` (at rest), and knows when it is at rest.
- **The composition** (`cortex-runtime`, which now depends on `cortex-neuromod`). `Config::modulation_baseline_q16` (default `MODULATION_ONE_Q16`; refused outside $[0, 1]$) is the fraction consolidated with the signal at rest. The executor holds one `NeuromodulatorState` for the engine (macro-columns do not exist yet), exposes it, and takes `reward(rpe)` between ticks as an input like an injection: a run that replays its rewards at the same ticks is the same run. At every tick, before the barrier that starts it, the coordinator publishes `modulation(baseline)` to the workers and then decays the signal by `DOPAMINE_TAU_SHIFT`; in the fan-out phase, after `step_stdp_all` and before the release, the worker that owns the block calls `consolidate_all` with the tick's modulation. So a reward is seen by the next tick's fan-out and fades over the next hundreds of milliseconds; a pairing waits in the trace for about the same time.
- **Persistence** (format version 11). Section kind 42, `SECTION_MODULATOR`, holds one 64-byte record, the engine's modulation state: the 16 bytes of the modulator at `[0..16)`, the baseline at `[16..20)`, and 44 reserved bytes that MUST be zero. The writer always writes it and the loader requires it (`MissingSection`), refuses a count other than one (`Directory`), a reserved byte that is not zero (`ReservedNotZero`) and a baseline outside $[0, 1]$ (`Config(ModulationOutOfRange)`), and reads both into the executor: the image's baseline outranks the configuration's, so that a run is still `(image, seed, trace)` (§8.3) with the rewards in the trace, and a trial's forks run under the live engine's baseline. The loader also refuses a trace or a compartment bit on an empty slot of a block, bytes the writer never produces ([ADR-0028](0028-edge-behaviour-audit.md)). A fork of a trial ([ADR-0031](0031-policy-amendment.md)) decodes it like everything else; the trial carries no rewards (Specified: the trace format's extension is the round that composes `cortex-affect`, whose mirth is where rewards come from, [ADR-0027](0027-vocal-synthesis-and-computational-humor.md)).
- **What is not claimed.** The rule consolidates at presynaptic spikes only: a synapse whose presynaptic unit never fires again never consolidates, and the continuous integral $\int e \cdot M\,dt$ of the reference is sampled at those spikes with the trace consumed rather than integrated. The engine has one modulator, not one per macro-column, and reads one of its four signals. No claim that the rule learns anything is made: the tests say which pairing moved which trace by how much, and which reward moved which weight (§2.3).

### Consequences

- Good: §8.8's three-factor row is Implemented, with the STDP row as its $m = 1$ limit; the distal-reward property has a test (a reward after the pairings consolidates them; a reward before them consolidates nothing; a late reward consolidates less).
- Good: no new crate, no new dependency outside the workspace, no new `unsafe`, no allocation in the loop; the trace decay reuses `stp_decay_factor_q16`, the consolidation is one multiply per slot.
- Good: the weight and the trace conserve their sum, so nothing is lost at a rail and nothing is applied twice.
- Bad: the pin moved, for the first time since [ADR-0030](0030-verification-governance.md) set it: the block's bytes, not the dynamics; the test states both values. A first draft of this decision gave the rail as the reason; a review pass reran the pinned network and found no weight near a rail, and the false premise was the brief's context line.
- Bad: the apical mask shares a word with the chain index; two accessors know the split, and a caller that wrote `next_block_idx` directly now writes `chain` through `link`.
- Bad: format version 11 makes version-10 images unreadable, as every bump does.
- Bad: with the default baseline at 1.0, a reward changes nothing (there is nothing above 1.0 to consolidate); a reference image that wants to learn from reward sets a lower baseline, which is milestone M5's tuning and not this decision's.

## Alternatives considered and why rejected

- **Option 1** quadruples the synapse arena's record count (Appendix A row 3 is 4.29 GB at 64 bytes per four synapses) for eight bytes of trace per block; the block had room once the mask moved.
- **Option 2**: a four-bit or eight-bit trace cannot hold the pairing amounts (0.0100 at the window's peak is 1.3 LSB of Q0.7), so the window's shape and the accumulation over several pairings would be lost; and converting between a coarse trace and the Q1.15 weight would not be exact at $m = 1$, so the reference runs would not reproduce ADR-0022.
- **Option 4** has no memory: a reward that arrives after the pairing finds nothing to consolidate, which is the problem the three-factor rule exists to solve.
- **The mask in the delay word** (bit 15 of each `u16`, since a delay is below $2^{12}$): possible, and left to the brief's empowerment; the chain word was chosen because one field changes instead of four and the walk reads delays per slot on the hot path.
- **Consolidation scaled by the interval** (an integral of $e \cdot M$ with both held constant since the last spike): closer to the reference, but it needs the modulation at the previous spike per block, which the block does not keep, and it does not reduce to ADR-0022 at any setting.
- **A periodic pass over every block** to consolidate between presynaptic spikes: an $O(\text{blocks})$ sweep per epoch on an arena Appendix A sizes at $2^{26}$ blocks, against TC-5's budget; the sweep of [ADR-0024](0024-cortex-image-and-clock-sweep.md) touches units, not blocks, and only quiet ones.
- **The modulation in the amendment registry**: it changes behaviour; [ADR-0031](0031-policy-amendment.md) forbids it.

## Confirmation

`cortex-core` (`dynamics/synapse.rs`, `serial.rs`): the chain word round-trips the largest index and refuses the next, keeps the compartments through `link`, `unlink` and a refill, and encodes with the mask at bit 28; the pairing amounts of ADR-0022 enter the trace and leave it whole at a modulation of 1.0; the trace decays to nearest (12 011 of 32 767 after one time constant, where a floor gives 12 010) by at least one LSB and reaches zero, and the most negative trace decays too; `step_stdp_all` decays before it pairs (970 + 123, not 1 089); consolidation moves the modulated fraction rounded to nearest, clamps the modulation, conserves the sum at both rails, and leaves an empty slot alone; the rail case states both values; two blocks with the same history stay identical under a varying modulation; the property test bounds a step's move of the trace by one window and the weight's by one window plus what was pending, across the tick wrap. `cortex-neuromod`: the modulation at every bound and beyond, the record's bytes. `cortex-runtime/tests/modulation.rs` (brief 017's exit test): pairings accumulate with the baseline at 0 and the block equals an oracle block given the same spike ticks; a reward consolidates the pending trace at the next presynaptic spike with the sum conserved; a reward before any pairing consolidates nothing; a late reward consolidates less; nothing is consolidated twice; the modulator is written when off rest and not when at rest, read back, and a loaded engine continues alike; the baseline is refused outside $[0, 1]$. `tests/differential.rs` restates the pin. `tests/image.rs`: the modulator section's reserved bytes, its count, its record size, its absence and a baseline out of range are refused on their own, the image's baseline is the engine's, and a trace or a compartment on an empty slot is refused while the same bytes on a filled slot load. The mutation gate on the changed lines ([ADR-0030](0030-verification-governance.md)) passes in CI.
