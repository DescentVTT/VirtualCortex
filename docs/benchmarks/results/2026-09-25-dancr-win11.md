Observations, for whoever runs the admissible version:

- A turn with no message is the gate and the integration, 9.8 + 11.0 ≈ 21 ns on one core, and a message adds one push and one drain, 75.2 / 16 ≈ 4.7 ns. The active-set runs of `runtime/cortex-runtime/tests/active.rs`, timed on the same machine around `Executor::tick` alone, read 24 to 25 ns of one worker per turn under the three densest drives in the run that reproduced their tables, up to 38 ns in an earlier run of the same tests, and 32 to 41 ns under the sparsest, where the barriers are most of a tick; ADR-0097's budget takes 25 ns.
- The four-worker idle tick moved from 18.9 µs to 1.73 µs between two runs minutes apart with no source changed: four unpinned spinning threads on a desktop scheduler measure the scheduler. The admissible run, pinned on isolated cores at the reference platform's worker count, is what whitepaper §11.1's lookahead question and the budget's $c_b(64)$ wait for.
